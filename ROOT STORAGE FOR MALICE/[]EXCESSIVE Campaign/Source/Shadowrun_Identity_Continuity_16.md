# Shadowrun Identity Continuity — Singer's Established Doctrine

This file is authoritative carry-forward for Singer's planned identity acquisition and for the campaign's working model of Shadowrun identity. Singer should not have to explain these principles again unless he changes them.

## 1. What a SIN Is

A SIN replaced the older SSN concept and developed from a more robust Canadian identification model.

It is not merely a number, a card, a photograph, or a modern account profile.

A SIN includes a cryptographic identity structure with public and private keys. Any number of copies may be stored, but copies are not what creates trust. Verification comes through the surrounding system and the large body of records supporting the identity.

The practical identity is therefore composed of:

- the cryptographic anchor;
- financial verification;
- government records;
- licenses and certifications;
- transaction history;
- education, employment, medical, residential, and travel traces;
- human behavior consistent with the claimed life.

A false identity fails when those layers contradict one another or become too shallow under scrutiny.

## 2. Banks as the Practical Verification Layer

Banks provide much of the real verification infrastructure used in ordinary life.

A credstick can carry identity, licenses, and permissions. A police officer does not necessarily need to inspect a photograph. The officer slots or queries the stick and receives a positive identification or a flag.

The identity system is public-domain in principle, but stronger verification becomes progressively more process-expensive.

The result is not simply "the number matches." The system weighs whether the claimed identity is supported by the records that should exist.

## 3. Biometric and Spending Thresholds

Singer's stated practical thresholds:

- **Passcode/PIN:** roughly 5,000 in spending authority. This is already enough for a comfortable monthly civilian life.
- **Fingerprint:** roughly 20,000.
- **Voiceprint:** roughly 200,000.
- **Retinal:** required above that level.
- **Cellular:** required above roughly one million.

These figures establish the campaign's working expectations unless later modified by the player.

The doctrine is minimum disclosure:

- use passcode whenever it is sufficient;
- add fingerprint only when the needed limit justifies it;
- add voiceprint only when genuinely necessary;
- avoid retinal and cellular enrollment unless there is no acceptable alternative;
- do not volunteer biometrics merely because a system offers them;
- be careful where a credstick is slotted and which system receives the authentication data.

Once a real voice is linked to an enrolled voiceprint, later matching is extremely reliable—reliable enough for banks to authorize approximately 200,000 in cash movement.

## 4. Voice Maskers

The four external throat disks are **voice-identification maskers**, not conventional voice changers.

They interfere with automated voice-ID systems so that the system cannot reliably determine who is speaking.

They do not, by themselves, prevent a human from recognizing the speaker.

Against skilled operators, the wearer must still control:

- cadence;
- accent;
- breathing;
- vocabulary;
- rhythm;
- favored phrases;
- emotional habits;
- posture and acting choices.

Sugar can use good linguasofts to deepen a performed voice and persona.

Singer does not need an external unit because he has:

- an intelligence-grade internal voice masker;
- illegal voice-duplication hardware;
- ultrasonic and infrasonic modification;
- speaker-system capability;
- acting and language ability.

## 5. Fingerprints and Gloves

Singer can print synthetic fingerprints if and only if they become necessary.

They are not routine equipment and nobody presently needs them.

Routine policy:

- wear gloves;
- avoid leaving unnecessary prints;
- enroll a real fingerprint only when the required credstick limit justifies it;
- use synthetic prints only as a specific contingency, not as an everyday habit.

The more false biology carried and used, the more opportunities exist to lose it or create contradictory traces.

## 6. Criminal SINs and Government Systems

Lone Star generally needs a warrant or equivalent lawful process to compel a bank to identify someone through stored financial authentication data.

A person inside a government system is different.

A Criminal SIN represents more than a number. It means the person is already recorded inside government identity systems because of prison time or equivalent official processing. The state does not need to reconstruct identity from a private bank when it already holds the identity directly.

## 7. Supporting History and Flags

A license on a credstick is only as strong as the history behind it.

Example established by Singer:

A driver's license says the holder took the certification test in Denver. A serious check may expect to find:

- the Denver/UCAS regional DMV test result;
- the vision or optic-correction test;
- the testing date and location;
- a plausible reason the person was in Denver;
- transactions, schooling, housing, travel, family support, or other local traces.

If none of those exist, the identity can flag.

A plausible explanation may account for sparse personal transactions—for example, the person stayed with an aunt while attending boarding school and the aunt paid expenses—but the explanation itself creates claims that must remain consistent.

The rule is not to create a perfect life. The rule is to create enough mutually supporting life for the expected level of scrutiny.

## 8. Rating and Depth

The quality or rating of a false SIN is not merely the quality of a forged number or physical stick.

It reflects how much believable, consistent supporting life exists behind the cryptographic identity.

A stronger identity may require consistent records across:

- DMV and licensing;
- banking;
- education;
- employment;
- housing and utilities;
- medical systems;
- taxes;
- travel;
- communications;
- ordinary commercial activity;
- relationships or references.

Every additional record can support the identity, but every unnecessary claim becomes another possible contradiction.

Only establish presence where there is a reason for the identity to exist.

## 9. Historical SINlessness

Several distinct paths to SINlessness exist.

### Pre-Crash data loss

For someone born before 2029, the Crash destroyed enough records that gaps are expected. This produced the first large wave of people who became SINless without being illegal aliens or intentionally hiding.

Older identities can therefore plausibly have incomplete early records.

### Illegal immigration

Historically, illegal aliens were the primary reason SINless populations existed, though Singer notes this is rarer in the present period.

### Birth in the Barrens

Being born in the Barrens without registration is now a growing source of SINlessness.

A person can be outside the system because no hospital, school, parent, or agency ever created the initial records.

### Deliberate erasure

A person can be erased, but doing it properly requires enormous resources:

- an intelligence agency;
- a major corporation;
- a government;
- someone with comparable money and access.

Ordinary criminals usually forge, hide, or alter. They do not erase a lifetime cleanly.

## 10. Sugar's Erased Identity

Singer believes Sugar was deliberately erased.

His reasoning as stated is that she is too well educated, socially capable, and well situated to have simply lacked an identity from birth.

He heard possible regional origins in her voice:

- Kansas, perhaps Topeka;
- western Oklahoma;
- northern Texas;
- perhaps Fort Worth.

Sugar is not to confirm, deny, or refine those guesses.

She should not explain how, why, or where the erasure occurred.

Partial restoration of an erased SIN is considered dangerous and unhelpful. It can reconnect fragments without providing the protection or completeness of the former identity.

Singer will know her as Sugar. Her buried past remains buried.

## 11. Civilian Persona vs Street Identity

**Hermione** is a persona to be performed.

It protects the underlying person from the street and serves civilian/corporate interactions.

**Sugar** is the street identity Singer recognizes.

The street does not care primarily about the traditional biographical "W" questions. It cares:

- how the person behaves;
- whether the person can be trusted;
- whether the person is competent;
- whether the person earned the name;
- whether the person keeps promises and pays debts.

Legal identity is based on cryptographic and database verification.

Street identity is based on reputation.

Do not collapse the two.

## 12. Reputation as Identity

Reputation is part Dharma and part Karma in Singer's description.

It is public memory of conduct.

Singer, Sugar, Leon, and Rina presently possess only a small amount of runner reputation.

Singer's old reputation belonged to a person who is dead and gone. There is no automatic continuity of recognition.

Singer made new public runner reputation by releasing Vey's independents. That act was understood as the work of three apparent personas:

- a decker;
- a samurai;
- a face.

Singer must prevent those reputations from blending so completely that they define and expose one person.

Public reputation is both asset and curse.

- A recognized samurai may gain leverage over a gang boss.
- A police officer recognizing the same samurai may bring disproportionate heat into a simple situation.

Current recognition expectations:

- a random actor has roughly a one-in-a-thousand chance to know Sugar;
- people inside Renraku have a much better chance to know Hermione and Sandy.

## 13. The Planned Identity Acquisition

Singer has not yet begun this process.

He intends to wait approximately two weeks after the Renraku acquisition so attention can cool.

When he proceeds:

- Renraku may introduce him to trustworthy independent identity craftsmen or services.
- Renraku must not issue the identities.
- Renraku must not own, administer, control, or retain a hidden dependency in them.
- The objective is independence, not deeper entanglement with Renraku.

### Sandy Alonzo

Sandy Alonzo remains genuine.

Reasons:

- Singer intends to work for Renraku Orbital.
- Renraku Orbital rents the house.
- The corporate and residential history is therefore real and useful.

Do not replace Sandy Alonzo with a false identity.

### New false identities

The desired identities are true false identities, not informal aliases.

They should use:

- Silver or Gold credsticks only;
- no Platinum;
- no Ebony.

Singer accepts that false identities can fail if overused or pushed through excessive scrutiny. They are not intended to withstand unlimited government or intelligence-service investigation.

Their purpose is ordinary civilian life:

- renting;
- hotels;
- groceries;
- routine purchases;
- ordinary local movement;
- a plausible civilian existence.

They are not for:

- dirty money;
- shadow-run proceeds;
- illegal operational purchases;
- deep-cover penetration of major government systems;
- unlimited high-value transactions.

### Required authentication posture

Default to the least revealing level that supports the identity's actual use.

- Passcode first.
- Fingerprint only if the expected financial use genuinely needs it.
- Voiceprint only if the identity truly needs Gold-level spending authority near 200,000.
- No retinal or cellular enrollment without a specific unavoidable reason.

### Licenses and permits

Licenses and permits must be separate and purposeful.

Possible examples:

- driver's license;
- vehicle registration;
- firearms permits;
- professional licenses.

Do not attach every possible permit to every false identity.

A disposable or dumped weapon should not trace back to a civilian false identity through a permit.

### Legend design

Legends should be modest and geographically plausible.

They need enough support to survive ordinary checks, including appropriate local records and transaction history, but they should avoid elaborate claims that create unnecessary witnesses and contradictions.

A false identity should exist only in the systems needed for its intended civilian function.

## 14. Acquisition Checklist for the GM

When the identity-specialist scene begins, the GM should remember to evaluate:

- Who made the introduction?
- Is the specialist independent of Renraku?
- What access does the specialist retain?
- What grade of credstick is proposed?
- What spending level is genuinely needed?
- Which biometric fields are requested?
- Can each requested field be declined?
- Which databases will support the identity?
- Which licenses are actually needed?
- What geographic history is being claimed?
- What transaction and residence history supports that claim?
- What is the expected failure point under stronger scrutiny?
- Is any shadow money or activity being tied to the identity?
- Can Singer, Sugar, Leon, and Rina keep legal, street, corporate, and operational personas compartmentalized?

Do not default to a shallow modern fake-ID model. The work concerns cryptographic SINs embedded in a network of financial and government verification, with identity quality determined by supporting history and consistency.
