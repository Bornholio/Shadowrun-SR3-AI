# Juan Herrero — Possessions and Personal Effects

GM-facing possession continuity for the retired prime runner operating under the alias **Juan Herrero**. The name is not a verified legal identity; it functions as the Spanish equivalent of **John Smith**.

Juan's base archetype is **Face**. He is a long-retired prime runner with extensive beta-grade cyberware, deep professional paranoia, substantial accumulated wealth, and a demonstrated willingness to kill personally when coercion or deniability required it.

This file details what filled the Malibu Tower duplex before Singer emptied it. It does not establish Juan's complete character sheet, legal identity, original team, or every hidden reserve outside the residence.

## Possession Profile

Juan did not collect objects casually. Nearly everything he owned served one of five purposes:

- preserve status and access;
- support negotiation, blackmail, or controlled violence;
- maintain multiple identities;
- reduce dependence on outside staff;
- survive betrayal, intrusion, or corporate abandonment.

His luxury was real, but curated. He preferred expensive objects that performed quietly, stored information, concealed capability, or made other people underestimate how dangerous the room was.

## Separation Rules

- Weapons, armor, electronics, robots, furniture, and luxury goods belong in loot or property files.
- Negotiation records and market intelligence belong in paydata files.
- Blackmail records, victim data, murder records, and target files are evidence, not ordinary loot.
- Identity packages require separate review before use or sale.
- Cyberware maintenance records may expose clinics, technicians, or legal identities and should not be treated as generic electronics.
- Safe contents remain unresolved until opened.
- Sentimental items should remain rare and should not become plot solely because they exist.
