# Juan Herrero — Weapons, Armor, and Rated Equipment 09

GM-facing recovered-property file for Juan Herrero's Malibu Tower residence.

This file replaces generic weapon, armor, and electronic-equipment descriptions with specific SR3 items. Weapon model, factory extras, and Concealability come from the local firearms reference. Armor uses established SR3 armor types and campaign-known ratings. Electronic and security equipment uses the exact ratings and cost formulas from `equipment Charts(2).md`.

Juan Herrero is a long-retired prime runner whose base archetype was Face. His equipment emphasizes concealment, social access, deniable violence, information control, escape, counter-surveillance, and surviving betrayal. It is expensive without becoming an exposed military armory.

## Notes on Selection

- The armory remains distributed. Juan did not maintain a single obvious gun room.
- His primary weapons are concealable and socially compatible with executive life.
- The Ares Executive Protector is the strongest fit for a Face who expects sudden close violence without advertising an assault weapon.
- The Ranger Arms SM-3 supports rare planned overwatch or assassination work, not ordinary residence defense.
- Light security armor exists as a retired prime-runner contingency, but daily protection is tailored secure clothing and form-fitting armor.
- Ratings 6–8 dominate the technical kit because Juan was wealthy, paranoid, and experienced enough to reject consumer-grade protection.
- Equipment costs use normal retail/list value. Street Index is not applied as the valuation base.
- Custom beta-grade medical interfaces, biomonitors, identity documents, ammunition, and some specialty armor do not have exact prices in the supplied equipment table; no unsupported price has been invented for them.
