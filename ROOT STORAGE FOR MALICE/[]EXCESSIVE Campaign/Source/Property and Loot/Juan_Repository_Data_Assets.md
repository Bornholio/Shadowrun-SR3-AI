# Juan Herrero Repository Data Assets

*Out-of-play GM reference.*

## Storage and handling state

Singer recovered **2,705 Mp** of unique expanded data from Juan's repositories. The archive is losslessly compacted to **541 Mp**, exactly 20% of its expanded size, and stored on one 1,000 Mp data unit. Compaction discards nothing; any block must be expanded before it can be searched, edited, executed, or transferred in usable form.

The encrypted source images remain preserved separately as read-only evidence. The consolidated archive contains decrypted, cleaned, indexed copies with executable traps, callbacks, resident agents, destructive validation routines, and external alert paths removed.

Juan did not need this storage for a powerful cyberdeck, large program library, raw simsense production, or magical research. Most of his physical capacity existed for duplication, isolated working copies, clean-media staging, encrypted backups, and empty reserve. His actual unique data is concentrated in medical planning, identity construction resources, raw surveillance underlying leverage files, and active professional records.

## Repository blocks

### Deal archive — 420 Mp expanded / 84 Mp compacted

The deal archive contains acquisition structures, ownership maps, negotiation timelines, executive profiles, shareholder pressure, debt and inheritance exposure, draft offers, fallback terms, private fears, and Juan's evaluations of how a transaction could be forced or redirected. It contains **seven complete deal dossiers**, including the active Viz-Roll acquisition dossier.

It was kept off Renraku systems because it combines private client instructions, illegally acquired seller intelligence, coercive planning, and Juan's own judgments about Renraku personnel.

This archive is not assigned generic paydata value. Each dossier requires a relevant buyer and case-specific appraisal. The Viz-Roll dossier is retained for current operations rather than offered for sale.

### Identity-forgery resource archive — 640 Mp expanded / 128 Mp compacted

The identity archive contains the real working material behind Juan's ten role kits: document templates, credential samples, seals, signature libraries, issuing-office examples, employment and address fragments, telecom-account structures, travel records, business stationery, banking forms, photographed source documents, validation notes, and records of which supporting systems accepted particular forgeries.

It was kept off Renraku systems because it documents illegal identities, compromised issuing procedures, cross-jurisdictional forgery methods, and aliases unrelated to any legitimate Renraku assignment.

The archive is valued as **640 Mp of datasoft-grade source material at 100¥ per Mp**, for a base value of **64,000¥**. Its useful market is limited to identity forgers, intelligence services, document mills, and specialist fixers. It does not begin decaying for **90 days**. Singer can instead retain it as a resource for separate identity-forgery and record-injection rules.

### Leverage archive — 790 Mp expanded / 158 Mp compacted

The leverage archive contains affairs, addictions, debts, illegal payments, misconduct, private recordings, surveillance extracts, medical and clinic records, family pressure, travel histories, threatened intermediaries, controlled-release packets, and fabricated alternatives. The large size comes from retained raw surveillance and working evidence rather than the compact chips used for finished blackmail packages.

It was kept off Renraku systems because it proves blackmail, illegal surveillance, coercion, fabricated evidence, and crimes involving targets outside the current corporate operation.

The archive resolves as **11 Orange paydata points**, worth **5,000¥ each** before fencing. Unsold stock loses **one point per day**, beginning with the least valuable remaining point.

### Betaware medical archive — 385 Mp expanded / 77 Mp compacted

The medical archive contains Juan's implant maps, surgical staging, clinic-specific procedure plans, diagnostic recordings, calibration cycles, anti-rejection schedules, recovery monitoring, revision histories, maintenance records, service ciphers, and emergency shutdown procedures. It is specialist betaware material derived from Juan's actual procedures rather than a general medical library.

It was kept off Renraku systems because it identifies deniable clinics and technicians, connects medical identities to Juan's aliases, exposes his exact implant vulnerabilities, and preserves proprietary betaware methods.

The archive resolves as **10 Red paydata points**, worth **5,000¥ each** before fencing. Its technical relevance decays slowly: unsold stock loses **one point every 30 days**, beginning with the least valuable remaining point.

### Violence and extraction archive — 230 Mp expanded / 46 Mp compacted

This block contains target schedules, route studies, floor plans, security summaries, specialist payments, dead-drop instructions, cleanup plans, completed intimidation records, disappearances, removals, and Juan's notes on targets he considered too dangerous to delegate.

It was kept off Renraku systems because it directly documents deniable violence, murder, kidnapping, illegal payments, and contractor identities.

This is evidence and operational intelligence, not generic sale stock. Individual files may later gain a specific buyer, victim, law-enforcement, or leverage value.

### Communications and contact archive — 96 Mp expanded / 19.2 Mp compacted

This block contains dead-drop formats, authentication phrases, call-routing notes, one-use account histories, payment-channel fragments, cutout procedures, and partial histories for specialists and frightened associates. It deliberately lacks one clean master contact list.

It was kept off Renraku systems because it links deniable assets, employers, cutouts, payment routes, and operations across otherwise unrelated assignments.

This block is retained as operational intelligence. Any sale requires extracting and validating a particular contact lane.

### Financial and access archive — 144 Mp expanded / 28.8 Mp compacted

This block contains credential associations and access records for **four credsticks, two bank accounts, one stock account, and three cutout corporate expense accounts**. It also contains service memberships, prepaid travel resources, investment access hardware references, and account-survival procedures tied to Juan's identity kits.

It was kept off Renraku systems because it mixes personal wealth, bribe funds, illegal operating expenses, cutout entities, and accounts built to survive the loss of any single identity.

These records are not automatically cash or generic paydata. Each credstick or account requires a separate access effort. A complete credential package can later be sold with its matching credstick, authenticator, or account device as high-density paydata, but values must be established after validation.

## Rules used

- Generic paydata has a base street value of **5,000¥ per point** before fencing.
- Orange point size is **2D6 × 10 Mp**.
- Red point size is **2D6 × 5 Mp**.
- Random paydata normally loses one unsold point per day; the medical archive uses the campaign ruling of one point per 30 days because betaware procedure data decays slowly.
- Datasoft-grade information uses **100¥ per Mp**, with more valuable information requiring separate appraisal.
- The identity archive uses its expanded size for valuation. Compacted size affects storage only.
