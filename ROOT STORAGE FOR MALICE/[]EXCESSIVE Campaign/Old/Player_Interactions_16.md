# Player Interactions — Session 16

## Singer and Sugar: Identity Instruction

Singer continued Sugar's education on identity, SINs, biometrics, and street reputation.

### Voice and Biometric Security

Singer clarified that the four throat-mounted Rating 8 voice masker disks are anti-identification devices, not voice changers. They interfere with automated voiceprint recognition, but human listeners can often still recognize the speaker. Defeating trained listeners still requires acting, cadence changes, language control, and other human performance.

Singer has an intelligence-grade built-in voice masker, highly illegal voice-duplication hardware, ultrasonic and infrasonic range modification, and the ability to function as a speaker system. He can further alter presentation through acting and languages. Sugar can rely on linguasofts to deepen a performed persona.

Synthetic fingerprints can be printed by Singer if truly needed, but nobody currently needs them. They remain a contingency measure. Gloves and full-time use of the anti-identification throat disks are the current practical precautions for Sugar, Leon, and Rina.

Singer explained the practical biometric thresholds used with credsticks:

- passcode for roughly 5,000;
- fingerprint for roughly 20,000;
- voiceprint for roughly 200,000;
- retinal verification above that;
- cellular verification above roughly one million.

The exact lesson was to provide only what is required and to be careful where a credstick is slotted. Once a real voice is linked to an enrolled voiceprint, matching becomes highly reliable. Lone Star generally needs legal process to obtain bank identification data, but a government-held identity such as a Criminal SIN is already inside official systems.

### SIN Structure and Verification

Singer explained that the SIN replaced the SSN and grew from a more robust Canadian model. It includes public/private key cryptography and distributed verification. Copies can exist, but validity is established through the supporting network.

Banks provide much of the practical verification system. A driver's license can reside on a credstick; an officer does not need to compare a photograph if the system returns a positive identification. A false identity may be flagged when its supporting history contradicts itself—for example, a Denver licensing record without credible Denver presence, transactions, education, housing, or a plausible explanation.

The quality of a false identity therefore depends on the supporting life behind it, not merely on a number or card.

### SINless Categories

Singer described several paths to SINlessness:

- records destroyed in the Crash of 2029, especially for people born before it;
- illegal immigration, historically the largest source;
- birth in the Barrens without registration, now a growing source;
- deliberate data erasure by an intelligence agency, government, major corporation, or someone with comparable resources.

Singer concluded that Sugar was likely deliberately erased because she was too educated and well situated to have simply lacked an identity. He made geographic guesses based on her voice—Topeka, western Oklahoma, northern Texas, or Fort Worth—but explicitly instructed her not to confirm, deny, explain, or investigate. Partial restoration of an erased identity would create danger without useful benefit.

### Civilian Persona and Street Name

Singer established that he will know her as Sugar.

Hermione is a civilian persona that must be performed for people who need that identity. It protects the person from the street. Sugar is the street identity.

Street identity does not depend on legal biography. It depends on reputation: how someone behaves, whether they can be trusted, whether they earned their standing, and whether they keep their word.

Singer described reputation as part Dharma and part Karma. The group has only a small amount of public runner reputation. Singer's former reputation belongs to a person who is dead and gone. His current public reputation began when he released Vey's independents. That reputation was created under three apparent personas—a decker, a samurai, and a face—and Singer wants to prevent those reputations from blending into a single identifiable public figure.

Public reputation may help a recognized samurai intimidate a gang boss, but the same recognition can turn a minor police contact into serious heat. Random recognition of Sugar is currently around one chance in a thousand; Hermione and Sandy are more likely to be recognized inside Renraku.

## Singer and Sugar: Professional Roles

Singer told Sugar to learn these lessons well enough to teach them to others.

He identified possible professional foundations for her:

- face, most reliably;
- computer specialist/decker as a strong developing base;
- courier or intrusion specialist as another possible base.

He warned against choosing a primary role she would always perform poorly compared with a properly augmented specialist. Leon has spent at least as much on his vehicle control rig as Sugar has on her headware. Sugar can be a good driver, but she will not have the agility and ease of a rigger, even a mediocre one.

Singer asked Sugar what her best skill was, then redirected her to evaluate the two skills into which she had invested the most of herself. She identified computers and people. She had initially used the word decker, but Singer challenged that label by asking which hosts she had hacked and which datasteals she had run. She admitted she had done neither.

Established correction:

- Sugar has substantial computer study and practice.
- She has not performed decking during played sessions.
- Her cyberdeck is a recent addition.
- She is training toward decking but has not yet earned the professional or street identity of a decker.

Singer also observed more stealth in Sugar's movement than she gives herself credit for.
