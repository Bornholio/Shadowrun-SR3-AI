## Identity Acquisition Requirements

These are established instructions and should not need to be restated by Singer:

- Renraku may provide introductions to independent identity craftsmen or services, but Renraku must not issue, build, own, or control the identities.
- Sandy Alonzo remains Singer's genuine corporate identity for Renraku Orbital employment and the Renraku-rented residence.
- New civilian identities are fake identities, not casual aliases.
- Use Silver or Gold credsticks only. No Platinum or Ebony.
- The identities are for ordinary civilian life: housing, hotels, groceries, routine purchases, and similar activity.
- Do not route dirty money, shadow income, or illegal activity through them.
- Use the minimum authentication necessary:
  - passcode by default;
  - fingerprint only where the needed transaction level justifies it;
  - voiceprint only when genuinely required;
  - avoid retinal and cellular enrollment unless unavoidable.
- Synthetic fingerprints are contingency equipment only, not routine practice.
- Full-time gloves and high-end anti-identification voice masker disks are standard precautions for Sugar, Leon, and Rina; Singer does not require one.
- Voice maskers obstruct automated voice identification but do not change the voice enough to defeat skilled human listeners. Acting, cadence control, and language use remain necessary.
- Separate permits and licenses should exist only where actually needed.
- Do not permit disposable weapons to civilian false identities.
- Keep legends modest, geographically plausible, and supported by enough transaction, licensing, education, and local-history data to survive ordinary verification.
- Every extra database or claim is another possible contradiction. Only establish identity where there is a reason to exist.

## Identity and Reputation Doctrine

- A SIN is a cryptographic identity anchor backed by government and financial databases.
- Banks provide much of the practical verification infrastructure.
- Credsticks may carry licenses and permits; routine checks return confirmation or a flag rather than relying on a visible photograph.
- Older people may plausibly have missing records because of the Crash of 2029.
- Modern SINlessness more often comes from illegal immigration, birth in the Barrens, or deliberate erasure.
- Deliberate erasure requires major resources and should not be casually investigated or partially restored.
- Sugar's prior identity appears to have been deliberately erased. Singer does not want her to confirm, deny, or investigate geographic guesses about her past.
- Hermione is a civilian persona to be performed.
- Sugar is the street identity Singer recognizes.
- Street reputation is earned through conduct, reliability, competence, and trust rather than government records.
- Public reputation can help or create heat. Keep decker, samurai, face, corporate, and civilian reputations compartmentalized where practical.
- Random recognition of Sugar is currently very low. Hermione and Sandy are more likely to be known inside Renraku.

## Sugar's Professional Development

- Sugar has invested most deeply in computers and people.
- She has practiced computer skills extensively, but she has not yet hacked hosts or completed datasteals in play.
- Her deck is a recent acquisition.
- She may be training toward becoming a decker, but she has not yet earned that professional reputation.
- Singer sees more natural stealth and controlled movement in Sugar than she credits herself for.
- Sugar should test physical capabilities in safe, non-pressured environments before deciding what roles she can support.
- Sugar is a good driver but not a rigger. Leon's vehicle control rig gives him a qualitative advantage she should not try to duplicate casually.
- Build from proven strengths rather than filling a base role that will remain weak.

## GM Constraints

- The user controls Singer. Do not invent Singer's internal reasoning, motives, or conclusions beyond what he states and what is logically necessary.
- The GM may freely develop Sugar's thoughts, reasons, and reactions.
- Do not call Sugar a proven decker until she has actually performed decking work in play.
- Preserve the distinction between studied skill, practiced skill, professional role, and earned street reputation.
