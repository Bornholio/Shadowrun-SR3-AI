He did not present it as payment, ownership, a test, or a reason she owed him loyalty.

The deck becomes part of her confidence because it confirms something she has wanted to believe about herself: she is not only someone worth protecting. She is someone worth equipping.

## Debt Losing Its Shape

Singer's refusal to treat the deck as a balancing payment changed something important for Sugar.

She has spent years reading favors, gifts, shelter, introductions, and protection for the hook hidden inside them. Even when she trusted Singer, some part of her still expected generosity to become a ledger.

This session weakened that expectation.

Sugar now has a clearer emotional distinction between:

- being given something because someone wants leverage;
- being given something because someone wants her to grow;
- being loved without having to produce equal value on command.

She may still notice old instincts when they surface, but she is less likely to obey them automatically.
