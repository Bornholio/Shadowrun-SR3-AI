# Sugar Campaign Historical Summary — Sessions 1–11

This is a compact historical continuity file. It records what changed across the campaign without replacing the current Header, character narrative files, interaction files, property files, or dedicated plot files.

## Authority and Supersession

- The newest dedicated file overrides an older Header when both cover the same subject.
- The current Header controls active time, location, immediate pressure, and next-session opening.
- `CampaignRules.md`, `Sugar_Narrative.md`, and `Singer_Narrative.md` control standing portrayal and authority.
- The newest `Player_Interactions_##.md` controls detailed relationship continuity.
- Dedicated property, loot, contact, location, and plot files control their own contents. Do not rebuild inventories or full dossiers from old Headers.
- Revised Headers supersede unrevised versions for the same session unless a later file explicitly preserves an omitted fact.
- GM corrections written into older Headers are rules to apply, not unresolved fiction.

## Session 1 — Escape, Recovery, and the First Sale

Sugar found Singer injured and impossible, then helped move both of them into a dead hotel room while his body recovered. The Piedmont apartment was stripped of important material and left as a boring, compromised fallback with Mr. Grumbles watching the building.

Deke's tracking charm and public humiliation at Black Calf exposed the basic predator economy around Sugar. Sugar built and sold a sanitized MOTH packet, earning her first independent certified nuyen and learning that information, proof, and market position matter more than revenge fantasies.

The relationship shifted from rescue and suspicion into chosen partnership, attraction, consent-conscious intimacy, and practical trust.

## Session 2 — Marv Shop Extraction

Sugar recruited Cal Rusk as a driver through Mrs. Voss. Singer, Sugar, and Cal entered Marv's tracker operation, removed selected equipment, data, tools, and inventory, and left a deliberately crude burglary story behind.

The team escaped in Cal's Bulldog with the good rotodrone still supporting exfiltration. The full recovered inventory moved into dedicated loot files and should not be reconstructed from the Header.

Sugar proved useful inside a real operation rather than serving as baggage. Cal finished the job cautious but impressed enough to remain a bounded logistics contact.

## Session 3 — Marv Residence, MOTH Validation, and Professional Growth

Singer searched Marv's residence and recovered additional evidence, property, and data showing that Marv knowingly supported stalking and tracker sales. Sugar and Singer kept victim identities out of sale material.

MOTH paid 8,000¥ for a validation package. Sugar gained Shadowland access, began HoloLisp and utility-design work with Plato, and started building professional technical competence rather than merely using inherited headware.

Sugar contacted Auntie Nettie under safety rules. Singer disclosed important medical and body-handling facts to Sugar. Their intimacy deepened while remaining chosen and direct.

## Session 4 — Venn, Hermione/Sandy, and the Voss Safety Intervention

Singer and Sugar established the Sandy/Hermione cover and acquired the Mary Avenue house under that identity lane. Sugar handled the Venn fencing contact, and a hardware-only sale meeting was scheduled.

Singer and Sugar intervened remotely when Lena and Mara Voss were endangered by a tracker-related client lane. Police contact and spirit pressure kept Mara alive and put the immediate attacker into custody. KITE-GLASS remained only a buyer or broker handle, not a complete conspiracy.

The campaign's operating correction became explicit: spend established pressure, advance changed states, and do not let evidence or safety work become an endless detective procedural.

## Session 5 — Sales Closed and the House Became a Base

MOTH bought the remaining sanitized packages and that tracker-data sale lane closed. Venn completed the physical-goods sale at 40 percent of actual value after Sugar won the negotiation. Cal's share and practical role were settled enough for him to remain a stronger contact.

Singer and Sugar moved into the Sandy/Hermione house, cleaned and placed recovered household and shop material, and established heavy mundane and astral security. The house became a real lifestyle base under cover rather than a temporary dead room.

Sugar's professional discipline and confidence increased. The relationship remained warm, sexual, playful, and adult without replacing operational agency.

## Session 6 — Old Threats Closed and New Work Appeared

Marv was confirmed dead. Deke's active lane was closed offscreen; later continuity establishes him dead at the bottom of the bay. Neither remains an active pressure source unless the GM deliberately reintroduces downstream consequences.

Sugar maintained the old apartment as a paid fallback address, contacted Nettie safely, trained with firearms, built corporate-compatible cover presentation, and developed medical and communication support.

A new fixer lane and the Bitter Hills target began to replace the failed Deke/Marv economy.

## Session 7 — Quill, Northline, and Bitter Hills Preparation

Maribel “Quill” Quist became the proper professional fixer route for Sugar. Singer destroyed Northline Civic Response Coordination's functioning coercion infrastructure, leaving only people, habits, and possible grudges rather than a working service racket.

Sugar's portrayal was corrected firmly: she is high-proof, competent in her rated skills, and capable of acting as a Johnson. The Bitter Hills operation became the primary lane, with the Argent Room meeting used to recruit Leon Vale and Rina Koss.

## Session 8 — Bitter Hills Resolved

Sugar successfully acted as Johnson at the Argent Room. Leon handled transport and vehicle control; Rina handled security and extraction-lane protection.

The Bitter Hills site was neutralized. Five living captives were extracted. Thomas Grady was already dead. The active crew was defeated, the site was stripped of useful criminal infrastructure, and the Lakeview Royals were directed toward the empty shell rather than a working exploitation operation.

Victim data and personal effects remained separate from loot. Leon and Rina became bounded professional contacts with clear future limits: no wetwork, no abductions, and extraction only when the target wants out.

## Session 9 — Sugar's Deck, Juan Herrero, and Malibu Tower

The house gained organized work areas and a low-grade clinic. Sugar received the rebuilt CMT Avatar cyberdeck as her property without debt or ownership claim. Quill became a proper direct fixer contact rather than a one-job intermediary.

The hidden money and coercion lane behind Bitter Hills led to Juan Herrero, a former prime runner and behind-the-scenes Renraku contract specialist assigned to Mark De'soir's Viz-Roll acquisition.

Singer killed Juan inside Malibu Tower, removed his body and extensive property, copied his data, and left an empty-suite disappearance story supported by moving-crew implications. A silent alarm triggered late, creating a real Renraku and tower-security wake rather than a clean vanishing.

Sugar controlled the timed information release outside the physical operation and proved that remaining outside a lethal room did not make her peripheral.

## Session 10 — Mark De'soir and the Viz-Roll Acquisition

Singer replaced Juan's negotiation function and met Mark De'soir under the Sandy/Hermione cover. Mark accepted a compartmentalized communication protocol and disclosed the acquisition's real weaknesses: fragmented ownership, seller trust, inadequate capital, and Graham Vey's effort to absorb success or leave Mark holding failure.

Singer demonstrated Viz-Roll's severe source-recovery flaw. Sugar independently worked seller and minority-holder mapping. Naomi Kincaid-Hayashi became the cultural doorway; Daniel Kincaid the operational doorway; the Vancouver trust the institutional doorway; the Brazilian block the easiest spoiler point.

Singer copied Graham Vey's files and identified a supporting network including Elias Trent, Mariko Senda, Paul Danner, Celeste Rourke, and Javier Mendes. This established selective middle-layer protection, not proof that Vey controlled Renraku security.

Juan's four vehicles were moved into a disposal lane under Leon's authority. The Nightsky was recognized as the highest-risk object. The older Thursday 4:00 p.m. LapisLazuli meeting was later superseded by the Rainier Exchange plan.

## Session 11 / Out-of-Chat Continuity — Rainier Exchange Preparation

By Wednesday, March 9, 2050, Singer and Sugar had completed additional productive planning with Mark. The seller-side event moved to the Rainier Exchange, Suite 10-C, on Thursday, March 10, with room access beginning in the morning and the substantive meeting at 10:00 a.m.

The meeting's purpose narrowed to trust, credible capitalization and operating commitments, technical manageability, and a small working group. Mark would invite Haruko Ishida and Takeshi Mori voluntarily for cultural-continuity and operational-delivery expertise.

Singer and Sugar planned independent prescreening, no-recording rules, bounded cyberware and transmission checks, neutral catering, disclosed astral security, and quiet emergency support. Leon and Rina were offered first-stage preparation and prescreening work with roles, authority, routes, and abort conditions to be established before arrival.

The vehicle-disposal lane largely resolved: the Nightsky attracted too much attention and was broken down. Leon was asked to look for a real limousine as a separate future transport asset, prioritizing sound systems and rebuildable value over empty prestige.

A higher sponsor behind Juan remained possible but unproven. Mark was specifically not to investigate upward. Evidence of tasking, funding, protection, reporting, cleanup, or recovery must reveal that layer through existing pressure rather than a manufactured investigation.

Sugar now operates as a professional partner, decker, social specialist, and emerging Johnson with her own fixer relationship. Singer remains wholly player-controlled. Their marriage cover and real relationship reinforce rather than erase Sugar's independent agency.

The campaign should continue to use changed states, bounded knowledge, SR3-era technology, human NPC voices, and established pressure that acts instead of waiting indefinitely.
