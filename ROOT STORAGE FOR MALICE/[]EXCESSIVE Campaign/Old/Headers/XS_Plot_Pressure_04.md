# XS Plot Pressure 04 - Character Wants, Capabilities, and Emotional Drivers

Session use: GM-side plot pressure and contact development reference for Sugar/Singer Session 04. This is not a narration script and not a decision tree. Use it to keep NPC motion coherent, bounded, and useful without stealing Singer's agency.

Active time base: 2050, Day 4 morning after the Bitter Lake hotel night. The player starts the session by describing Singer's actions. Do not open with extended narration unless the player asks for scene framing.

Primary player goal for the near future: fence/sell the recovered physical goods and separated useful packages without selling raw victim lists or uncontrolled hot evidence.

Fence clock: the GM's 2D6 complication roll says complications arrive on Day 9 if the team has not sold or moved the relevant loot by then. From Day 4 morning, this leaves roughly 7 days of operational room. If the data and victim/buyer/evidence material is separated cleanly, the remaining physical gear trends toward high-tech loot rather than hot loot. Raw trackers, victim lists, buyer trails, active serials, Marv/Deke material, and unsorted devices remain hot.

## Table-use boundaries

- Keep responses shorter than prior mode.
- Do not roll past player decision points.
- Use prose narration with visible actionable facts, not bullet-list choices in play.
- Outside narration, GM analysis can use bullets and tables.
- Player starts scenes by declaring actions when possible.
- Cal can speak by message to Sugar unless physically in-scene.
- Contacts should stop feeling like punishments for Sugar having purchased or earned them. At least one legitimate useful contact in the fixer/broker/fence category should become available by the end of this session.
- Deke is a threat and failed contact, not the only available gateway into the street economy.

## Immediate pressure spine

The next few days are about converting a dangerous haul into money, tools, favors, and cleaner options before Marv's ecosystem catches up.

Marv's panic and customer/supplier fallout create the broad external timer. Deke's emotional/control pressure creates the personal timer. MOTH/Skein can become more than a simple data buyer if developed as a cold but useful broker. If MOTH is not expanded into that role, a replacement contact should enter through Cal, MOTH, Auntie Nettie, Shadowland, Cairn, or the Black Calf trio.

## Contact replacement principle

Deke's category was aspiring fixer/broker/street introduction. Replacing him should not mean giving Sugar a perfect patron or safe parent. It should mean giving her access to a real, useful, bounded professional who can route goods, names, and opportunities without treating her as property.

Good replacement qualities:
- Wants money, reputation, clean handling, or leverage, not ownership of Sugar.
- Can say no without punishment.
- Can offer a fence lane, buyer lane, clinic lane, safe inspection lane, or introduction lane.
- Knows only what they have a route to know.
- Does not demand raw victim lists as the price of admission.
- Can be cautious, transactional, funny, or cold while still being legitimate.

## Cal Rusk

What he wants:
- His 3,000¥ first validation cut settled in the cleanest way available.
- To keep his van, drones, name, and work out of Marv's blast radius.
- First look at drone-useful repair gear, electronics tools, boards, dampers, testers, and clean duplicate tool rolls against his eventual share.
- A boring plan for moving or selling the physical haul that does not require him to babysit hot evidence forever.

What he can do:
- Provide practical logistics, van movement, short-term storage reality, drone repair perspective, and grounded caution.
- Act as a message-only plot voice to Sugar when offscreen.
- Introduce or point toward a practical buyer/fixer lane if the team keeps him insulated from the weirdest truth.
- Confirm which physical lots are boring enough for electronics/tool resale after identifiers and data are stripped.

Emotion driving him:
- Wary respect. Singer looks like a military ghost story, but Sugar handled herself and paid him.
- He is allergic to hot stupidity, not hostile.
- His main fear is being left holding a van full of someone else's supernatural mess.

Likely Cal message voice:
- Short, practical, dry.
- To Sugar rather than to the room.
- Example tone: "Certified transfer is fine if you split it across two blanks. I don't need choir music about it. Also, tell your tall problem I found two buyers for clean tools, not trackers. Clean means clean."

Possible information he has:
- A low-glam electronics salvage buyer can take stripped tools and benches if the story is a failed shop clear-out.
- A drone hobbyist/repair circle may pay better for test gear and boards, but not if anything screams stalking hardware.
- He may know a real fence by reputation but will not vouch hard without seeing the cleanup plan.

## MOTH / Skein

What she wants:
- Validated, packaged, deniable information she can resell or leverage.
- Proof that Sugar/Singer can deliver clean packets without drama.
- Control over exposure, timing, and silence.
- To avoid becoming responsible for raw victims, messy physical product, or supernatural strangeness.

What she can do:
- Buy curated data packages: technical tracker defeat/build implications, buyer-chain structure, supplier-chain traces, Marv residence index summaries, sanitized proof of malice.
- Route to inspection space, specialist readers, or safe review resources if paid or if it improves her future margin.
- Introduce a non-Deke broker/fence if she sees money in becoming the team's quiet routing layer.
- Verify whether a buyer/fence lane is an obvious law-enforcement or corp-security trap.

Emotion driving her:
- Cold curiosity becoming cautious professional interest.
- She is not friendly, but she respects clean execution.
- Her fear is contamination: raw names, victims, magic, police attention, amateur revenge, and clients who confuse payment with friendship.

Expansion option:
- MOTH can become Sugar's legitimate replacement for Deke in the information-broker lane, but only if framed as paid access and procedural trust. She should not become warm quickly. She can become useful quickly.
- She should expand from "data contact" into a route-maker: buyer vetting, dead drops, price benchmarking, inspection space, escrow, and introductions.

Limits:
- She does not want raw physical trackers dumped into her lap.
- She will discount value for risk, dirty pickup, cleanup work, and silence.
- She may refuse anything victim-list-shaped unless sanitized.

## New replacement contact candidate: Rina Vale / "Switchback"

Role:
- Street broker and fence-router for electronics, tools, small technical shops, clean grey-market components, and boring logistics.
- Not a glamorous fixer. Useful because she moves goods without needing a myth around it.

Introduction routes:
- Cal can mention her as someone who buys clean electronics lots and does not ask stupid questions if the paperwork story is plausible.
- MOTH can route to her as a physical-goods handler separate from data.
- Auntie Nettie can know her as a clinic-adjacent procurement person, but that risks bringing medical lanes into loot lanes.

What she wants:
- Clean margin.
- A story she can repeat without sounding like she is lying.
- No raw stalking devices, no victim data, no corp-marked prototypes, no magic explanation.
- A reason to believe Sugar is not Deke's property and Singer is not going to scare away buyers.

What she can do:
- Buy or route stripped electronics/computer shop lots, tool kits, benches, diagnostic gear, generic chips, generic components, and safe resale items.
- Split sales into multiple smaller buyers to lower heat.
- Introduce a second buyer for chip encoders or specialized programming hardware if those are scrubbed and the team decides to sell instead of keep.
- Explain what must be sanitized before it stops being hot.

Emotion driving her:
- Competent suspicion.
- Irritation at Deke-style ownership games.
- Practical sympathy for Sugar only after Sugar demonstrates boundaries and payment discipline.

Bounded knowledge:
- She knows Cal by reputation or one prior van/drone job.
- She may know Deke as a nuisance wannabe, not as a complete villain dossier.
- She does not know Marv house details, MOTH, spirits, metaplanar storage, or Singer's real capabilities.

Use by end of session:
- She should at least introduce herself by message, call, or controlled meet if MOTH is not taking the broader broker role.
- Her first useful offer should be real but bounded: inspect a sanitized sample list, quote a lot, or provide a safe low-value first sale.

## Cairn, Black Calf fixer

What he wants:
- Decide whether Singer is an opportunity, a liability, or both.
- Potentially sell software from Singer's expensive optimized catalog of sneak-optioned wares to his clients.
- Make money without getting eaten by whatever Singer is.

What he can do:
- Offer a software resale lane that could inject significant cash into Singer's hands.
- Provide a separate fixer contact path outside Deke/Sugar's street dependency mess.
- Filter buyers if Singer retains the right to reject clients.

Emotion driving him:
- Greed restrained by survival instinct.
- Curiosity about Singer's catalog and standards.
- Concern that Singer may be too serious, too strange, or too connected to approach casually.

Limits:
- He should not know Singer's location or deep truth unless given a route.
- He does not automatically care about Sugar beyond what the interaction shows.
- This lane risks money and attention; it should not replace the immediate fencing goal unless the player leans into it.

## Trio in Black Calf

Use as lesser pressure/contact texture unless brought into scene.

What they want:
- To understand whether Singer is a profitable mystery, a threat, a future patron, or trouble to avoid.
- To preserve their own standing inside the Black Calf ecosystem.
- To be near opportunity without becoming the first people blamed for it.

What they can do:
- Carry rumor, soft introductions, observation, and social temperature.
- Nudge Cairn's impression.
- Provide small local knowledge, buyer warnings, or a message channel.

Emotion driving them:
- Fascination, caution, opportunism, and social hunger.
- They should not act as a single three-headed exposition device. Give each one a different reason to care if they appear.

Bounded knowledge:
- They know only what happened in/around Black Calf and what Cairn or visible behavior gave them.
- They do not know MOTH, Marv's house, Sugar's old pad details, or Singer's hidden systems.

## Deke

What he wants:
- Reassert control over Sugar or punish the loss of control.
- Find out who helped her, where she is, and whether his Marv/tracker lane is burning.
- Preserve his self-image as a fixer with reach.
- Avoid being the obvious loser if police/admin attention starts sticking to him.

What he can do:
- Pressure Sugar's old pad, old routes, weak acquaintances, club-adjacent people, and anyone he thinks owes him.
- Try to message, guilt, threaten, or love-bomb through old channels.
- Reach for another broker/contact if Marv goes silent or explodes.
- Make mistakes if angry, especially if official attention makes him feel cornered.

Emotion driving him:
- Ownership panic.
- Humiliation.
- Fear disguised as entitlement.

Limits:
- He does not know the Bitter Lake room, Cal's role, MOTH details, or current loot state unless he discovers them in play.
- He should not be the only route to street economy.
- Do not let his existence make every contact a trap. The point is to replace him, not make Sugar pay forever for needing someone.

## Marv

What he wants:
- Understand what happened to his shop, house, devices, contacts, car, and security.
- Survive customer/supplier anger.
- Rebuild communications and decide whether to flee, blame someone, hire help, or beg protection.
- Recover enough records to identify the thief or reduce his losses.

What he can do:
- Wake into panic and create noise.
- Contact customers/suppliers through whatever backup route remains outside stolen/reset devices.
- Blame Deke, Sugar, local burglars, competitors, or an unknown professional depending on what evidence remains.
- Trigger broader market awareness that tracker goods are moving.

Emotion driving him:
- Shock, violation, fury, terror.
- He is not a mastermind in this moment. He is a stripped operator realizing someone reached into both business and home.

Limits:
- He does not automatically know Singer did it.
- He does not automatically know metaplanar storage or magic method.
- His reaction should create pressure and opportunities: panic sale, bad calls, supplier exposure, customer fear, or a buyer-chain lead.

## Auntie Nettie / Mrs. Voss

What she wants:
- Keep Sugar alive and out of the old apartment corridor.
- Assess Sugar's body/headware without creating a text trail or inviting Deke to her door.
- Decide how much of her shadow-clinic identity to reveal.

What she can do:
- Provide medical/headware triage, clinic referral, quiet checkup, and grounded adult warning.
- Potentially introduce a clinic-adjacent practical contact later, but she should not become the default fence.
- Warn Sugar if Deke or strangers move near the old pad.

Emotion driving her:
- Worried affection restrained by experience.
- Protective anger under calm instructions.

Limits:
- Contact tomorrow afternoon from somewhere different. No names, no boys, no drama in text.
- Do not pull her into every problem. She has boundaries and risk.

## Fencing plan implications

Near-term sellable if sanitized:
- Electronics/computer shop lots.
- Duplicate electronics kits and computer kit if not kept.
- Diagnostic boxes and signal test gear after tracker identifiers/data are removed.
- Generic optical memory chips.
- Some scrubbed components, casings, packaging, bulk consumables, tool rolls, benches.
- Household resale goods only as low-value moving/estate lots.

Hold/keep or treat as hot:
- Sony Encoder II unless the team chooses to sell later.
- Technician workstation/programming computer until fully imaged and scrubbed.
- External storage brick until fully handled.
- Finished tracker units, prototypes, micro-transceivers, customer/order archives, supplier fragments, Deke-adjacent material, hidden partitions, Marv house evidence, victim photos/lists.

Operationally useful distinction:
- Physical goods without data/serial/victim linkage can become high-tech loot and easier to fence.
- Anything that proves stalking, buyers, victims, supplier chains, Deke, or Marv's intent remains hot and should be sold only as curated paydata or used as leverage.

## First-session response guidance

At the table, after the player describes Singer's morning actions:
- Keep the response timestamped.
- Acknowledge only visible room facts, Sugar's immediate emotional response, and any messages/results that action triggers.
- Let Cal's reply arrive in message voice if useful.
- Let MOTH, Cairn, or Switchback move only when the player's actions open that lane or when a concise pressure ping is needed.
- End on the next meaningful decision point.
