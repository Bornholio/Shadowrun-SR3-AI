# Scumbag — Bitter Lake / Bitter Hills Chop House
*GM EYES ONLY*

Excessive Campaign notes, this is external GM Facing and he will place plot into chats hands by peices, ignore plot drivers below if they conflict, do not choose paths

## Plot Overview
A crew of organ leggers has converted a low-rent residential-service property in Bitter Lake into a members-only vice house. The local pocket is called Bitter Hills by landlords, residents, clients, and street traffic, but official records should use Bitter Lake. The clients are small-time fixers, corp mid-managers, gang lieutenants, and other men with enough money or position to believe the house will protect their secrets.

The house's real business is recording clients and workers into raw Better Than Life sex chips, then using the footage for blackmail, resale, and leverage. The basement side is a low-grade organ-legging and cyberware-stripping operation. It is not a charnel house or fantasy murder den: bodies do not stay long, dead or discarded people are processed quickly, bleach and soap cover the medical smell, and secure locks protect the working rooms.

The family gets pulled in through one of the threads below: a missing contact, a blackmailed target reaching out, a chip turning up on the street with a recognizable face on it, or a body-recovery job gone through the wrong fixer. This file covers the crew, the house, and the gear. Play out the hook separately.

## Hooks (pick or combine)

## The House
Unassuming metroplex-era rental in Bitter Lake, in the local Bitter Hills pocket, chosen for privacy and weak practical patrol pressure rather than defensibility. Two above-ground floors run the visible service side: lounge, bar, kitchen, private rooms, editing room, and office. The basement is not accessible to casual lease or utility research and is reached through a reinforced door behind the bar, disguised as a supply closet.

**Ground floor:** entry lounge, bar, kitchen, one heavy's bunk/ready room, service hall, front entry, rear exit.  
**Second floor:** four service rooms with concealed simsense pickup rigs, locked editing room, madam's office, bathroom, linen/storage.  
**Basement:** BTL production bay, low-grade medical bay, recovery/holding room, cold storage, cleaning/laundry, locked supply storage, rear loading path through the normal building service route.

Goods move in and out through useful reality: rear alley, back door, kitchen/service hall, behind-bar basement door, covered bins, handtruck, laundry bags, and a van staged near the service end of the block. No dank sewer route, no secret tunnel, no impossible disposal path.

Security is practical rather than impressive. The public-facing security is weak enough to fit the cover: one cheap camera on the front door and normal rental-grade locks. The private rooms and basement working spaces have better locks, controlled keys, reinforced interior doors, and people who know who belongs where. The crew relies on familiarity, payoffs, fear, timing, and the three main threats more than visible hardware.

## Gear
**Editing room:** three work terminals, a low grade, cobbled together simsense/editing terminal, chip blanks, optical chip encoders, external storage, hardline feeds from the service rooms, and local file protection.  
**Service rooms:** each has a hidden simsense pickup rig feeding the editing room by hardline.  
**Basement medical bay:** low-grade surgical and cyberware-removal gear, sterile containers, medkit support, cleaning stock, body bags, laundry bins, and locked supply cabinets. It can strip usable parts and stabilize a cooperative or restrained person briefly; it is not a real hospital and not a safe clinic.  
**House security gear:** handheld comms, cheap cameras, interior locks, basic counter-surveillance tools, restraints, flashlights, and practical street weapons.

## The Crew — 8 Men

### Main Three
Use `Scumbag_06_Main_Three_Heavy_Hitters.md` for full stat blocks.

**Deacon — House Enforcer / Dirty Street Samurai**  
PR 5. The visible hard stop in the house. Deacon controls bodies, corridors, doorways, and short violent timing. He is not a thug with chrome; he is a failed professional dangerous enough to make a normal runner team slow down.

**The Editor — Simsense Technician / Data Controller**  
PR 4. The house's technical spine. He controls recordings, feeds, storage, chip burning, local systems, and file movement. He is most dangerous when he is destroying, moving, hiding, or protecting data while the house is under pressure.

**Church — House Mage / Astral and Perception Control**  
PR 4. The magical reason the house has lasted longer than its hardware deserves. He controls perception, fear, silence, astral checking, barriers, and delay. He should not know Singer's hidden capabilities by default; he can recognize danger and react to it.

## Running It
The three main threats are the wall between the family and the operation. Deacon buys physical seconds, Church complicates perception and movement, and the Editor moves or burns evidence the moment things go loud. The lesser crew are not civilians with pistols; they are low-grade operational criminals with roles, habits, and fear.

The real danger to leave loose is the drive and storage chain full of blackmail chips, unsold BTLs, and names. Victim data is not loot. Evidence and victims should be handled as evidence and victims, not as sale inventory.

Consider what happens to the living occupants after the raid; that is a consequence thread, not a loose end to hand-wave.
