*GM-facing SR3 equipment file for the five lesser criminal occupants. These are practical working loadouts, not full personal inventories and not additional plot hooks.*

The house has shared spare pistols, armor jackets, ammunition, restraints, handsets, and work equipment in the bunk room, bar, editing room, and basement. The entries below identify what each man normally has on him or immediately assigned to him. They do not duplicate every removable item already listed in the floor inventory files.

## Use in Play

- Marcus is the most visibly armed of the five and the most likely to stand his ground briefly.
- Owen is strongest when warned and positioned near the rear shotgun, but his morale is ordinary.
- Daniel protects the vehicle, keys, route records, and cargo before he protects the building.
- Victor's firearm is mostly status and fear management; separation and pressure degrade him quickly.
- Kevin reaches for drugs, restraints, locked doors, or a hostage before he tries to win a gunfight.

None of these loadouts turns a PR 1–2 criminal into a professional combatant. The gear helps each man perform his job and makes him dangerous to civilians, captives, and distracted intruders without competing with the three heavy hitters.
