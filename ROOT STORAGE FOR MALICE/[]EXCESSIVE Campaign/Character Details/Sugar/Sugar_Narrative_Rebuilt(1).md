## Core Identity

**Street name:** Sugar  
**Japanese tag:** 糖度過剰警報  
**Legal name:** Evangeline Mae Kincaid  
**Civilian/corporate persona:** Hermione Alonzo  
**Era / place:** Seattle Metroplex, 2050  
**Age:** 18  
**Metatype:** Human  
**Magic:** Mundane

Sugar is a runaway from Oklahoma oil wealth and a hard-edged Catholic family culture built around money, obedience, reputation, and inherited control. The Kincaids gave her expensive alphaware headware as an educational investment and preparation for family business. She was expected to understand contracts, maps, drilling rights, numbers, etiquette, institutional behavior, and how to present herself without surrendering information.

She left without the trust, accounts, lawyers, household protection, or a safe legal identity. She kept the education, the headware, the instincts, and the ability.

In Seattle she became Sugar. The name and the longer tag are chosen identity: loud, artificial, memorable, and hers. The pink hair, goggles, gum, cheap chains, and aggressive sweetness are not evidence of incompetence. They are presentation, misdirection, style, and street identity.

**Hermione Alonzo** is a civilian and corporate persona performed by Sugar. Hermione is not a separate personality, not an adult mask hiding a lesser self, and not a replacement for Sugar. Sugar changes register, clothing, vocabulary, posture, and presentation because she is skilled at doing so.
