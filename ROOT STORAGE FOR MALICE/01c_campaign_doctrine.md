## Purpose

This file records GM-level tone and continuity doctrine for future Malice Family SR3 sessions.

Load after `01_chat_control.md` and before active session files when context is uncertain or when cleanup/generation risks reactivating retired material.

---

## Core Doctrine

The campaign is grounded Shadowrun 3rd Edition Seattle.

Preferred tone: corporate intrigue, city mechanics, pop culture, money pressure, gangs, local services, clinics, shelters, venues, runner etiquette, neighborhood trust, police background texture, and corporate liability management.

Avoid: cosmic horror, eternal-magic destiny plots, world-saving stakes, global Matrix catastrophe, artifact-driven plot, chosen-family metaplot, or prior-campaign archaeology.

---

## Prior History

Do not use prior campaign history as plot fuel.

The memory loss exists to break twelve years of accumulated play infrastructure: hundreds of contacts, extreme asset growth, and old campaign sprawl. Prior history is not a contact source, asset source, enemy source, ally source, memory-restoration engine, or narrative driver.

The active campaign is about powerful prime runners without their old infrastructure.

---

## Retired Material

Aldric is dead to the campaign. Do not use him on screen, off screen, as explanation, as contact, as protector, or as a future reveal source.

GT and related pursuit threads retreat after the defeat of their final team. The corporate high-ups bury the matter by absorbing it into a medical subsidiary, including Taetzel Building history. Do not continue GT as an active hunter chain.

RAVN is retired except for physical items the family already holds. Do not generate new RAVN pursuit, builder mystery, surveillance network escalation, or conspiracy layers from it.

Dragons appear only at the planned apex of a large-scale event. Do not use dragons as cleanup, casual allies, casual enemies, offscreen problem-solvers, or narrative spice.

The Astral Sextant is a fade-out item. Items are tools, resources, loot, evidence, or things to pass onward. They are not plot engines or destiny keys.

---

## Contacts

Contacts are SR3 hard rules elements and player investment. They are GM purview.

Each character has two true sacrosanct contacts. Other interpersonal relationships can exist as ordinary NPC relationships, but do not treat them as mechanical Contacts unless the GM establishes them.

Do not revive old contact networks from prior history.

---

## SR3 Technology Tone

This is futuristic SR3, not 1990s technology and not SR4/SR5 smartphone culture.

Use SR3-appropriate language: phone, handset, pager, radio, transceiver, telecom, commcode, LTG, jackpoint, deck, host, frame, agent, utility, IC, tactical link, BattleTac, headware communications device.

Do not use the later-edition generic consumer-device meaning of the forbidden term. In SR3 it is a rare headware device for multiplexing communications equipment mentally, not a universal pocket computer.

Matrix plots should be local and institutional: hosts, LTGs, telecom traces, data hygiene, broker queues, dead drops, HR systems, security tally, IC, sysadmins, and corporate liability.

No global Matrix-collapse plot.

---

## Security Ratings

Security ratings color response, not constant pressure.

Use ratings for response time, response quality, backup availability, procedural professionalism, jurisdiction, and force level once something becomes visible.

Do not imply constant camera coverage, citywide acoustic surveillance, automatic harassment, universal SIN enforcement, or that SINless people cannot move through high-rated zones.

The city is diverse, crowded, and socially mixed. Police and private security are overstretched. Beat cops, patrol cars, EMTs, venue security, gang lookouts, mall guards, and neighborhood watchers are usually background tone unless player action escalates the scene.

Assume the team has strong police-channel and network awareness as baseline. Routine policing is background color, not a default plot complication.

---

## City Tone

Seattle changes by time of day but is never empty.

Downtown and Belltown should feel active: workers, SINless, couriers, venues, food, service staff, off-shift corp employees, gangers, cops, EMTs, clinic traffic, shelter movement, delivery traffic, and night-economy residue.

Friction should come from actions, favors, money, logistics, trust, reputation, and incompatible interests, not from forcing surveillance or metaplot pursuit.
