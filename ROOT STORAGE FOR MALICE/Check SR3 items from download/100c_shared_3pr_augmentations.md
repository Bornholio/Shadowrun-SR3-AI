# Shared Augmentations - 3PR
*Singer, Banshee, Carpenter. Chat-use quick reference only.*

Purpose: load before 3PR tests to catch modifiers **not already folded into character-sheet attributes/skills**. Character sheets hold the name-only inventory. Do not re-add character-creation plumbing, combat-only devices, router/topology detail, or full cyberware rules here.

## Use Rule
- Sheet values already include most augmentation dice/ratings. Do not add them again.
- Apply only the situational items below.
- For unusual cyberware legality, scan, install, Essence, or generic rules, load the cyberware/bioware/nanoware skill instead of expanding this file.

## Universal Flags
- Delta cyberware / cultured bioware / nanoware / geneware are highly concealed but still suspicious if identified.
- Cyberscanner baseline: most cyberware TN ~14-15; induction datajacks are tactile/very hard to spot. Bioware scan TN ~14.
- Legality matters only after detection or search.
- Signature 7 normally; 6 while nanites are actively healing.

## Roll Modifiers Not Baked Into Sheets

### Any Skill
- **Aptitude:** -1TN only for the sheet-marked skill.
- **Cognition:** may Center on INT tests when penalties matter.

### Perception / Senses
- **Perceptive:** -1TN Perception and astral perception.
- **Sound Discrimination 5:** +5 hearing dice; counts as Rating 10 vs white noise.
- **Spatial Recognizer + Hearing Amplification:** -5TN sound location; -2TN ordinary hearing perception.
- **Wide-band Hearing:** -2TN when ultrasound/extended-frequency hearing matters.
- **Eagle Eyes 2:** offsets up to -4TN range/vision penalties.
- **Nictitating Membranes:** -1TN smoke, glare, flash, water eye penalties.
- **Microscopic Vision:** -2TN microscopic/fine-size vision penalties.
- **Improved Scent/Taste:** obvious odors/tastes -4TN; scent can identify/track.

### Movement / Physical Utility
- **Augmented Balance:** -2TN balance, climbing, jumping, falling, knockdown; ignore difficult ground.
- **Extended Volume 3 + Abnormal Oxygen Adaptation:** long breath hold; -3TN fatigue; oxygen toxicity reduced.
- **Double Jointed / Muscle Toner 4:** Escape Artist -1TN / -2TN when relevant.
- **Retractable Climbing Claws:** -2TN climbing.
- **Calcitonin:** bones rarely break; bones Barrier 6.
- **Cold/Heat Adaptation:** -2TN Survival in matching environment.
- **Improved Glucagon:** fatigue accrues at half rate.

### Social
- **Good Reputation 2:** -2TN social tests; cancels +2 suspicion/wariness.
- **Tailored Pheromones 2:** effective CHA +4 within 20-30m physical proximity only; no astral/remote effect.
- **At Ease:** use highest Etiquette specialization; locals may read them as local; Friendly attitude -1TN.
- **Bravery:** -1TN fear/intimidation resistance.
- **Clean Metabolism:** reduced scent trail/body odor; pheromone detection TN 10.

### Magic / Astral
- **Focused Concentration:** sustaining is +1TN; max sustained spells = INT+1.
- **Spirit Affinity:** elemental spirits may be curious, helpful, or reluctant to attack.
- **Astral Chameleon:** others +2TN to assense aura; signatures -1F.
- **Perceptive:** also applies to astral perception.

### Matrix / Data / Knowledge
- **Encephalon 2:** Task Pool for INT-linked mundane skills when applicable; do not apply to Perception or magical ability.
- **Cracker:** +2 dice Access tests.
- **Chipjack Expert Driver:** Slot 1 currently Cracking 6; Task Pool 6 for that chip-based skill.
- **Mnemonic Enhancer 3:** +3 dice memory-related INT tests; Knowledge default TN +3 instead of +4.
- **College / Tech School Education:** -1TN Academic / Background Knowledge defaults.
- **Photographic Memory:** exact recall of seen/heard material the character had access to.

### Toxin / Disease / Pathogen
- **Toxin Extractor 2:** -4 Power injection/ingestion; also attack-vector patches by house rule.
- **Tracheal Filter 2:** -4 Power air-vector toxins/gases.
- **Nephritic Screen:** -1 Power pathogen/blood-vector toxin; +1 Body die.
- **Pathogenic Defense 4:** -4 disease/pathogen Power; +4 dice disease resistance.
- **Resistance to Poison 3:** +3 dice toxins/poisons/drugs.
- **Weak Immune System 1:** -1 Body die vs disease.
- **Implant penalty:** Poison/Toxin/Disease/Drug Power +1 when applicable.

### Healing / Stabilization
- **Guardian Angel:** automatic R6 medkit; -2TN Biotech First Aid; auto-stabilizes Deadly; auto-revives unconscious; doubles overflow time on failed stabilization.
- **Nanosymbiotes:** heal much faster than normal; active nanites reduce Signature by 1.
- **Regenerative Healer + Quick Healer:** -4TN Healing Tests total; magician-doctoring penalty ignored; +2 magic-loss roll bonus.
- **Implant penalty:** +1TN Healing Tests.
- **Angiotensin:** blood loss is much slower; out-of-combat wound loss about Body x12 seconds per box.
