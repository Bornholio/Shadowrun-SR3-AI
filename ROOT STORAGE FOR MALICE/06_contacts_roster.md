## Contact Authority

Contacts are hard SR3 rules elements and player investment, not casual interpersonal relationships. Contact creation, retirement, rating changes, and activation consequences are GM purview.

Only the listed fourteen retained contacts are mechanical contacts by default: two true contacts per family member. Other NPCs may exist in play as acquaintances, clients, neighbors, vendors, cops, fixers, gangers, or local color, but they are not Contacts unless the GM explicitly makes them Contacts.

All retained contacts were established within the last five years. The runners have no memory of these relationships unless current play establishes recognition or context.

## Contact Rules (SR3)

- Single Contact Rating: 1–3
- Activated via Etiquette test
- Each character has two sacrosanct true contacts
- Contacts are player-facing investment and should not be used casually as plot complication engines
