## Tone And Plot Hygiene

- Fade prior metaplot and cosmic material. Do not generate new plot from old destiny, ancient magic, world-saving, cosmic horror, or prior-career callbacks.
- Preferred tone: corporate intrigue, medical/corporate liability, pop culture, city mechanics, gangs, venues, SINless logistics, neighborhood trust, money, favors, and local reputation.

- Use SR3 communications vocabulary: phone, handset, radio, transceiver, pager, telecom, commcode, LTG, jackpoint, cyberdeck, frame, utility, BattleTac, tactical link.
