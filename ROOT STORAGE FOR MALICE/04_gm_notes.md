## Campaign Overview

**Game:** Shadowrun 3rd Edition (SR3)  
**Setting:** Seattle Metroplex, March 2058  
**Location anchor:** McHugh's Diner — 64th St E / Sumner Tapps Hwy E interchange, north side  
**Real-world GPS:** 47.199398, -122.215170  
**Group Name:** Malice  
**Shadow Reputation / Web Presence:** Orthogonal Gate

**Premise:** Seven elven runners, a family unit, woke in a McHugh's diner on the Auburn/Seattle border after an unknown medical procedure they cannot remember. Their memories are clear only up to 2025. Skills, hardware, and muscle memory remain intact, but the old campaign infrastructure is gone.

---

## Campaign Doctrine — Current GM Authority

- Prior weird / cosmic / legacy plot material is retired unless explicitly reauthorized by the GM.
- Do not generate cosmic horror, eternal magic, world-saving destiny, ancient-prophecy plot, or global Matrix catastrophe.
- Do not use prior campaign history as plot fuel. The memory loss exists to break 12 years of accumulated play infrastructure, hundreds of contacts, and 100M+¥ in assets.
- The family are powerful prime runners but are resource poor, socially disconnected, and locally unfamiliar.
- New play should be grounded in corporate intrigue, pop culture, city mechanics, gangs, policing, favors, money, logistics, and local trust.
- Security ratings color response time, response quality, and jurisdiction. They do not imply constant surveillance, constant SIN pressure, or empty streets.
- The city changes tone by time of day, but is never empty.
- SINless people move through nearly every security level. Police and security are overstretched.
- Beat cops, patrols, EMTs, venue security, gang lookouts, and neighborhood watchers are usually background tone unless the players escalate.
- The team is effectively listening to police channels and local emergency/security networks as routine table baseline.

## SR3 Technology Language

Use SR3 communications and Matrix vocabulary:

- phone, handset, pager, radio, transceiver, telecom, commcode, LTG, jackpoint, deck, frame, utility, host, IC, BattleTac, tactical link
- Avoid later-edition wireless lifestyle assumptions and smartphone-centered culture.
- Do not use later-edition generic communication-device vocabulary. In SR3, the similarly named headware device is rare communications multiplexing hardware, not a consumer pocket device.

## Active Continuity Boundaries

### Aldric
Aldric is removed from the campaign. No future appearances, no offscreen action, no exposition role, no contact mechanism, no protector role, and no plot authority.

### GT / RAVN / Corporate Retreat
GT and related operational pressure retreat after the defeat of their final team. No Iron Sill escalation. High-level corporate authority absorbs the mess into a medical subsidiary along with Taetzel Building history. Future use is liability containment, record burial, severance, medical-record control, and corporate file sediment only.

RAVN does not generate a new surveillance-conspiracy arc. Only the physical components already held at Unit 5 remain active as loot/evidence/tools.

### Prior History / Old Assets
No use of prior history. Do not surface old contacts, old enemies, old assets, old identities, old properties, or old adventure callbacks unless the GM explicitly reintroduces one.

Taetzel Building, HMS old infrastructure, old farms/caverns, weapon foci, and similar legacy assets are unavailable, buried, sold, legally toxic, irrelevant, or archived unless explicitly restored by the GM.

### Dragons
Dragons are not casual cleanup, allies, opposition, or offscreen plot devices. Use only when a large-scale event has intentionally escalated to that apex and the GM has a plan.

### Artifacts / Objects
Objects do not drive plot. Useful items are tools, resources, or gear. Plot objects should usually be passed onward or be of no direct use to the party.

The Astral Sextant is a fade-out item.

### Ork Underground Cache
The old cache near the Big Rhino was stripped about a year ago. It does not contain the full former gear list.

Only one hidden cache remains: credsticks and IDs that work better than the family's current IDs, but have no credit and no significant balances.

## Current Play Pillars

- Belltown street ecology: Nährboden, Oswin, Marta, Rat, Patch, Lace, Sandy Systems, gray-market medical logistics, Matrix dens, shelters, bars, and runner-adjacent networks.
- Downtown city texture: corporate density, venues, food, music, immigrant and tribal trade goods, late-night crowds, public transit, changing neighborhood tone, and jurisdictional seams.

- Residence management: recovered gear at Unit 5 creates evidence risk, disposal questions, repurposing opportunities, and cash/resource decisions.
- Corporate consequences: mistakes get buried, assigned to subsidiaries, reframed as medical liability, or hidden inside HR/security bureaucracy.

## Standing GM Rules

- Sessions remain table history, but retired plot material should not create new gravity.
- Contacts are hard rules elements and GM purview. Each character has two true contacts that are sacrosanct; other interpersonal ties can retire if they create complications.
- The 14 retained sheet contacts are the intended reduced network. Do not rebuild the old hundreds-contact infrastructure.
- Mystery threads are not automatically generative. Prefer closure, fade-out, or mundane explanation unless the GM explicitly asks for mystery development.
- The family does not recover prior history by default.
- Do not introduce NPCs who recognize the family's pre-reset identities.
- Do not turn city security into a panopticon. Use police/security as lived-in civic texture unless triggered by visible events.
- Combat control remains outside chat scope.
