## Elven Aging

Most elves who Awakened in 2011 or later may expect to see their 400th birthdays, barring disease or disaster. Elves mature physically and mentally at the same rate as humans; around age 25 their metabolic balance effectively freezes, and they cease showing physical and mental signs of aging. Senility is expected during the last century of life, per Tir schooling.
