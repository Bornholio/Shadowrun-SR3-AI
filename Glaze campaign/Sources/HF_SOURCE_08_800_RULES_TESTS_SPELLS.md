## HF-801 — Roll Display Rules

<!-- HF-SOURCE-BEGIN HF-801 -->

# SR3 Roll Display Rules

- Use an actual random d6 method for every GM-controlled roll.
- Resolve the entire roll before displaying it.
- Show each die only as its **final SR3 value**.
- When the Rule of Six applies(ALWAYS), explode each 6 until complete and show only the accumulated result: `8`, `13`, `20`, etc.
- Do not show intermediate notation such as `6+2` or `6→2` unless the GM asks for it.
- If a TN above 6 requires exploding dice and the displayed roll does not show resolved values above 6 where applicable, treat the roll as invalid.
- Report: **dice pool, TN, final dice, successes**.

Example:

`Glaze INT 13, TN 8: 2, 4, 9, 3, 1, 8, 5, 2, 15, 4, 3, 1, 5 — 3 successes.`

Each die is compared independently to the TN. A high exploded result still counts as only one success.

For Open Tests, show the completed dice and report only the highest final value. For Opposed or Success Contest tests, show both sides separately before the result. For complementary tests, show the primary and complementary rolls separately.

## HF-820 — Clean Element Spell

<!-- HF-SOURCE-BEGIN HF-820 -->

**Clean (Element)**
Type: P · Target: 6 · Duration: P · Drain: +1(S)
This area spell clears all impurities out of a volume of air, earth, or water, leaving it completely clean. Each element requires a separate spell. For example, Clean Air could be used to cleanse a room of toxic fumes, or Clean Water could purify a drinking supply. Successes determine how thoroughly the element is cleaned; muddy water would require only 1 or 2 successes to clean, but contaminated runoff could require as many as 5 successes to make it drinkable.
*Manipulation (Transformation)*

## HF-821 — Fashion Spell

<!-- HF-SOURCE-BEGIN HF-821 -->

**Fashion**
Type: P · Target: 6 · Duration: P · Drain: +1(M)
This spell instantly tailors clothing, transforming garments into any fashion the caster wishes. The successes measure the degree of style in the tailoring. The spell cannot change clothing's protective value, only its cut, color and fit. The weight of the clothing does not change, and it must cover approximately the same amount of area (a jump suit can't be converted into a bikini). This spell cannot change clothing with a total Armor Rating (ballistic and impact) higher than Force (+1 per 2 successes). The caster must touch the clothing.
*Manipulation (Transformation)*

## HF-822 — Fix Spells

<!-- HF-SOURCE-BEGIN HF-822 -->

**Fix**
Type: P · Target: OR · Duration: P · Drain: (S)
The Fix spell repairs damage to non-living materials, including vehicles. The caster must touch the object. Fix can repair any item with a weight equal to the caster's Magic in kilograms or less. It can only repair broken items when all the pieces are present. Each success repairs 1 point of Barrier Rating (or box of damage), up to a maximum equal to the Force of the spell. The Fix spell can only be used once to repair any set of damage.
*Manipulation (Transformation)*

**Fix Vehicle**
Type: P · Target: OR · Duration: P · Drain: +1(D)
The Fix spell repairs damage to vehicles. The caster must touch the vehicle. Fix Vehicle can repair any vehicle with a body equal to the caster's Magic or less. It can only repair broken vehicles when all the pieces are present. Each success repairs 1 point of box of damage, up to a maximum equal to the Force of the spell. The Fix Vehicle spell can only be used once to repair any set of damage.
*Manipulation (Transformation)*

## HF-823 — Invisibility Spells

# Invisibility spells

Invisibility
Type: M • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: M
This spell makes the subject invisible to normal vision. The subject is completely tangible and detectable by the other senses. Their aura is still visible to astral perception.  Attacks against invisible targets suffer the Blind Fire modifier if the attacker is unable to see or otherwise sense the target of the spell. Invisibility affects the minds of viewers. 

Improved Invisibility
Type: P • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: +1(M)
This spell makes the subject invisible to normal vision. The subject is completely tangible and detectable by the other senses. Their aura is still visible to astral perception.  Attacks against invisible targets suffer the Blind Fire modifier if the attacker is unable to see or otherwise sense the target of the spell. Improved invisibility affects technological sensors as well.

Vehicle Invisibility
Type: P • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: +1(S)
This spell makes the subject invisible to normal vision. The subject is completely tangible and detectable by the other senses. Their aura is still visible to astral perception.  Attacks against invisible targets suffer the Blind Fire modifier if the attacker is unable to see or otherwise sense the target of the spell. Vehicle invisibility affects technological sensors as well. Signature effects can combine with mask spells, and concealment power.

## HF-824 — Mask Spells

<!-- HF-SOURCE-BEGIN HF-824 -->

# Mask spells

Mask
Type: M • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: M
The mask spell alters the target’s voice, scent and other physical characteristics. The target assumes a physical appearance (of the same basic size and shape), chosen by the caster.  Observers can make a Resistance Test to attempt to overcome the illusion. Mask affects the minds of viewers. 

Physical Mask
Type: P • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: +1(M)
The mask spell alters the target’s voice, scent and other physical characteristics. The target assumes a physical appearance (of the same basic size and shape), chosen by the caster.  Observers can make a Resistance Test to attempt to overcome the illusion, this creates an illusion that affects technological sensors as well.

Vehicle Mask
Type: P • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: +1(S)
The mask spell alters the target vehicles signature, shape, color scent and other physical characteristics. The target assumes a physical appearance (of the same basic size and shape), chosen by the caster.  Observers can make a Resistance Test to attempt to overcome the illusion. Physical Mask creates an illusion that affects technological sensors as well.

## HF-825 — Shape Earth Spell

<!-- HF-SOURCE-BEGIN HF-825 -->

**Shape Earth**
Type: P · Target: 4 · Duration: S · Drain: +2(M)
This spell moves and shapes a number of cubic meters of earth equal to the caster's Magic. The material can be moved and reshaped in any way the caster desires, at a Movement Rate equal to 1 meter per success (up to a maximum equal to the spell's Force) per Combat Turn. This allows the caster to rapidly dig or fill in holes or tunnels, dig trenches, or push over earthen barricades. The spell only works on soil and rock, not processed mineral materials such as glass, metal, or concrete, and the spell's Force must at least equal the Barrier Rating of the material affected (1 for loose soil, 2 for packed earth, 4 for gravel or loose rock and 6 or more for stone). Material reshaped by the caster remains in its current form when the spell ends. If that form cannot be supported by the material, it will collapse.
*Manipulation (Telekinetic)*
