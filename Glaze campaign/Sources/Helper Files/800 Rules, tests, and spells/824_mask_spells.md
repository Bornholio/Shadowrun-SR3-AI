# Mask spells

Mask
Type: M • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: M
The mask spell alters the target’s voice, scent and other physical characteristics. The target assumes a physical appearance (of the same basic size and shape), chosen by the caster.  Observers can make a Resistance Test to attempt to overcome the illusion. Mask affects the minds of viewers. 

Physical Mask
Type: P • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: +1(M)
The mask spell alters the target’s voice, scent and other physical characteristics. The target assumes a physical appearance (of the same basic size and shape), chosen by the caster.  Observers can make a Resistance Test to attempt to overcome the illusion, this creates an illusion that affects technological sensors as well.

Vehicle Mask
Type: P • Target: 4 • Duration: S • Drain: +1(S)
The mask spell alters the target vehicles signature, shape, color scent and other physical characteristics. The target assumes a physical appearance (of the same basic size and shape), chosen by the caster.  Observers can make a Resistance Test to attempt to overcome the illusion. Physical Mask creates an illusion that affects technological sensors as well.
