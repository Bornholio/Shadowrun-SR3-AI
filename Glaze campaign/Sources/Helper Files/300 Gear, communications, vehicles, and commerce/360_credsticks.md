## Acquisition Time
Standard availability procedure: **Etiquette Test vs. the Availability TN.** Divide the base time by the number of successes. Failure means the item can't be found through that contact within the time frame.

Base time is the second half of the Availability entry (24 hours, 72 hours, 14 days, 30 days); the TN is the first number (the Rating).
