# Grenades

- Note most grenades are listed as J (Military Legality) and police do not use them except for Concussion in SWAT situations demanding them
- Grenades may come in `Air-Timed` variants for use with grenade launchers and smart linked users with *range finders* 
- Defensive means large radius inapropriate for offensive use not a general class of various types
- Offensive means low radius, used for advancing in close use not a general class of various types
