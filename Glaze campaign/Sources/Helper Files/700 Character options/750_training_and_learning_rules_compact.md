# SR3 Skill Improvement, Learning & Training Rules

## Learning New Skills
New skill: Rating 1 for flat 1 Karma (Active, Knowledge, or Language).

## Skill Improvement Good Karma Cost Table
**Base Skill**
| New Rating Is… | Active | Knowledge/Language |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ Attribute Rating | 1.5 | 1 |
| ≤ (2 × Attribute Rating) | 2 | 1.5 |
| > (2 × Attribute Rating) | 2.5 | 2 |
**Specializations**
| New Rating Is… | Active | Knowledge/Language |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ Attribute Rating | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| ≤ (2 × Attribute Rating) | 1 | 1 |
| > (2 × Attribute Rating) | 1.5 | 1.5 |

## Training
Test using the current skill or INT, TN = 2 × Skill Rating sought. Good Karma spendable only after test succeeds.

## Learning a New Skill
Two methods: self-directed study (DIY) or instructor. DIY normally limited to skills related to ones already possessed (GM discretion).

### Do-It-Yourself
Final training time = base training time ÷ successes. Zero successes → base training time × 1.5. New skills start at Rating 1.

### With an Instructor
Instructor must know the skill at rating 3+ (or ≥ target rating if improving), and should have Instruction Skill.
Instructor Test: Instruction (4), as complementary roll
Student gets −1 target modifier on Skill Tests for learning new skills.
Instructor must stay for full training time; leaving early doubles remaining time.

## Skill Improvement Table (Training Time)
| Skill Type | Base Training Time (hours) |
|---|---|
| Active Skills | Karma spent × 28 |
| Knowledge/Language Skills | Karma spent × 60 |

## Training hours
1 training day = 4 hours. Daily training limit = [Willpower/2] + 4.
Training beyond limit: declare extra hours, make Willpower (10) Test; result = extra effective hours (capped at declared amount).
Learning a language by living in its normal environment as a non-native speakers always counts as 4 hour in addition to formal training
