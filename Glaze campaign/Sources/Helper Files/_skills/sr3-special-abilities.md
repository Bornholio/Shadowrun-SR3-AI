## ADEPT POWERS

### Multi-Tasking
**Cost:** 0.5 Power Points

The adept may use **two Free Actions per Initiative Phase**.

> **When to call:** Any time a player wants to declare two Free Actions in the same initiative phase. Confirm PP cost on sheet before ruling.

---

### Nimble Fingers
**Cost:** 0.5 Power Points

- **+1 die** to Stealth or Quickness Tests involving sleight-of-hand
- The following become **Free Actions:**
  - Insert Clip
  - Pick Up / Put Down Object
  - Remove Clip
  - Use Simple Object

> **When to call:** Any time a player performs one of the listed actions and wants FA timing, or rolls Stealth/Quickness for a sleight-of-hand task. Confirm PP cost on sheet before ruling.
