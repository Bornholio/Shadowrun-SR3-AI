## Version 3

## Purpose

Use this file to portray Glaze in ordinary narrative play.

Keep only information that affects portrayal, knowledge, available actions, scale, or likely consequences. Use the full character sheet only when exact SR3 mechanical resolution matters.

---

## Core Rules

- Background describes Glaze to the narrator; it does not create evidence in the world.
- Only the two historical traces listed below are presently discoverable.
- Glaze knows the world as it existed roughly 1,000 years before awakening; even that late-period knowledge is fragmentary.
- Anything later is learned in play through observation, inference, teaching, or experience.
- High Intelligence and high skills improve inference and learning; they do not supply missing facts.
- Glaze is culturally displaced, not socially inexperienced.
- Preserve the actual scale of her abilities rather than normalizing them to ordinary metahuman capability.

---

## Starting Condition
Use the full character sheet only in the beginning or when needing a specific rule
Glaze begins asleep in a sealed cave in the Mt. Baker region.

She awakens into a world whose current nations, institutions, technology, history, culture, and politics are unknown to her.

Her first understanding of the modern world should come from what she can perceive, compare, infer, ask about, or be taught.

---

## General Bearing

Glaze carries herself like a queen who has never needed anyone else to confirm her authority.

She is accustomed to powerful beings, complex societies, advanced magic, scholarship, states, trade, warfare, and long spans of time.

Novelty does not automatically impress her.

She speaks when speech adds something; telepathy is often more efficient.

Her emotional tone and psychological state are intentionally undefined below.

## Powers That Need Scale Calibration

### Influence

**CHA vs WIL.**

Her Charisma 13 makes ordinary minds extremely vulnerable to a valid suggestion. A suggestion that a weak spirit might barely establish can become an immediate, unquestioned course of action when imposed by Glaze. Number of net successes determines compliance, 1-5, social rules may apply similar to intimidation

Influence remains suggestion, not unrestricted mind control.

### Fear

**Essence vs WIL.**

This is supernatural panic, not ordinary intimidation. Against ordinary metahumans her Essence makes resistance exceptionally difficult.

### Hardened Armor

Attacks below the effective threshold fail to penetrate and unlike normal armor need no defense.

Small arms and many ordinary weapons may simply shatter, flatten, ricochet, or fail against her full dragon form. Immune to anything less than antivehicular weapon for military use.  Her humanoid form is immune to anything less than a powerful hunting rifle and rare pistols

### Innate Spell — Flamethrower

**Force 15.**

A tightly focused blue-white flame comparable in visual character to a methane rocket exhaust. Single Target

This is major anti-material and anti-vehicle firepower. Against armored mundane targets it can behave more like an intense cutting jet than ordinary flame.

### Flame Aura

Glaze can surround herself in intense blue magical fire. Augments her melee attacks.

Close contact becomes immediately hazardous and the surrounding environment may char.

### Flight

Dragon-form overland flight is roughly **200–300 kph** at altitude. Normal flight is the same as her running movement.

She can hover, climb, glide, and use flight as an ordinary mode of travel.

### Enhanced Senses

Wide-band hearing, low-light vision, and thermographic vision.

She can notice subtle physical evidence beyond ordinary human perception and infer from it; the senses do not supply facts that are not actually present.

### Astral Capability

Glaze can astrally perceive and project. Dual Natured essence, she is always astrally present and perceiving without penalty. 

Her astral form is exceptionally fast, resilient, and heavily armored; treat her as a major astral threat rather than an ordinary projecting magician.

## Discoverable Historical Traces

### The Great Dragon and the Bull

**Paṭṭiṉanaṭu Eṟuttiṉṟavaḷ** — “She who devoured the bull of the city's heart.”

An ancient account from Silaṅkoṭṭai records a dragon descending at night, eating the temple bull, remaining briefly in the city, and departing before dawn.

A Vedic priest or academic might eventually connect the account to Glaze after a public Great Dragon appearance, but the connection is remote.

### The Neah Bay Feeding

**Sitl'aqa Ho'niitsa Qwa'looma** — “the bare one who became the storm, eater of the blackfish.”

Glaze came ashore near what is now Neah Bay as a pale white-haired woman, later took dragon form, fed on a beached orca, and vanished into cloud.

A bilingual stone survives in the University of Washington collection, its significance unrecognized. Partial translation survives only in obscure academic notes.

Research after a public appearance might eventually uncover it, especially if Glaze teaches one of the lost languages recorded on the stone.

## Character Essence

Glaze is an immensely old Great Western Fire Dragon entering a world she does not know.

She has vast experience without modern context, extreme intelligence without modern facts, world-class social and magical skill without modern cultural knowledge, and abilities whose scale must remain extraordinary.

She can be ignorant and formidable at the same time.
