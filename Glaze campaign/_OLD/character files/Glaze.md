# Glaze
Great Fire Dragon — Second Age relic, Initiate Grade 3
## Description
### Full Dragon Form
She doesn't read as a heraldic beast — no spiked, ponderous mass of horn and scale. Fifty tons of her move like she weighs nothing: sleek, fluid, unmistakably feminine even at full size, built for speed and grace rather than bulk. Head and body run some thirty meters, standing over four meters at the shoulder, tail trailing twenty-five meters behind her, wings spanning forty-five when spread. Her hide is a deep, light-swallowing obsidian black — matte enough that from below, against open sky, she all but vanishes into the blue — broken by veins and highlights of true sapphire along the ridgeline, where the color pools deep and saturated rather than catching a glossy shine. What look like spines running the length of her tail aren't spines at all: smooth, bright blue projections, finlike rather than bony, tapering the tail's whole run before terminating in a fringe of fine blue fiber at the tip. Her horns sweep back from her brow in a wavering, blade-edged curve — closer to a kris dagger than a ram's horn, dark and faceted along their length. Her eyes burn amber-orange, and that color isn't decoration — it's her own fire, visible even banked. At her throat, a second light glows steady blue, the likely source of the pale cast running through the rest of her coloration, hair included. She is a fire dragon in the truest sense: her Flame Aura and breath don't billow orange like a young dragon's — they burn narrow, blue-white, and violent, closer to a rocket engine's methane flame or a cutting torch than a bonfire, hot enough to slag ceramic and melt steel rather than simply set things alight.
### Partial (Hybrid) Form
A humanoid frame roughly human-scaled but unmistakably not human: pale skin carrying that same faint blue undersheen, waist-length hair the color of glacier ice, and vestiges of the dragon retained rather than hidden — a three-meter tail, the same black curved horns, faceted amber eyes. The same obsidian hide and sapphire veining persist over her torso, shoulders, and limbs in smooth, armor-like segments. The glowing blue core at her throat is visible here too, worn openly rather than concealed. This is the form she wears when she wants to be recognized as what she is without the full scale of the dragon.
### Human Form
No tail, no horns, no scale — but nothing about her reads as ordinary. She carries herself like a queen who has never once doubted the room was hers. Her outfit is glossy black, form-fitting, seamed everywhere with fine copper fittings and banding — not decoration, but living metalwork, the copper carrying threads of her own magic woven through the fabric so the garment survives the transformation with her rather than being shed or torn. The same pale blue-white hair and ice-pale skin carry over from her other forms, and that lingering amber warmth in her eyes that a careful observer might find unsettling on a human face. The glowing core at her throat is banked low but never fully dark, a soft blue glint just visible beneath the collar of the outfit.
