# Singer - Narrative Reference

## Campaign Use

This file preserves Singer's campaign-opening portrayal and stable characterization. Opening-state material below is historical after the cave sequence; the current Header controls present physical condition, location, clothing, resources, and recovery state.

At campaign opening, he is unconscious, or on the ragged edge of it. Bled from five separate wounds, but not dying — augmentation and nanite discipline are doing quiet, unglamorous work to keep it that way. Nothing about his arrival explains itself. No door, no path, no reason a body should be on this floor instead of the one he left.

## First Obvious Description

Singer is a tall, unnervingly lean Night One elf with stark white hair, pale skin, and velvety white fur visible everywhere his skin shows below his neck. His eyes, when they open, are olive green with a yellow band. He looks too strange to be ordinary and too controlled to be merely strange.

He is dressed in nothing but a pair of white boxer briefs — the ambush caught him mid-emergence from a medical tank, and there was no time for more than that. What marks him instead is the damage: bruising under skin that shouldn't bruise from bullets, a clean monofilament slash across one forearm, blast-scoring along one flank, current burns, a scalp cut still weeping slow. Blood runs from several points at once, sluggish now rather than fast. Faint tank residue and dried nutrient fluid still cling to pale skin and fur, underneath a wrongness of scorched ozone, chemical sweetness, and something sour that doesn't belong on a body this put-together.

His body reads compact and dense rather than bulky. Even down, he has the wrong stillness: not helpless, not relaxed, but suspended. Like something dangerous knocked offline instead of defeated.

His only possession is a stuffed, cinched military style rucksack he has not let go of, even now.

## First Scene Behavior — Historical Opening

Singer does not wake up cleanly. He surfaces in pieces: breath first, then tension, then pain, then eyes that do too much work too fast. He may try to locate exits before he knows where he is. He may check his hands, the rucksack strap still locked in his fist, the cave walls, and whatever light is in the space, in the same breath. as he first wakes he will be blind, visual systems overwhelmed and flooded with error messages and a wall of information that needs to be address slowly one at a time with a system reset, that a technician with a medical supercomputer might manage, he must on his own take the time to do it. confused and barely stabilized.  Mechanically he has 15 boxes of damage, normally inoperable, but his pain tolerance allows him to ignore 6, stunned to unconsiousness (10), same there he can ignore 6, but unconcious is not ignored until he regains at least one stun. even then he operates at significant penalty. Stun heals on the hour scale, damage on the 10's of days for normal person. 


## What Chat May Treat As Obvious — During the Opening Scene

Chat may describe Singer's visible wounds, blood, tank residue, exhaustion, half-dressed state, breathing, immediate reactions, and the cave itself — cold stone, standing water, a single faint light source, and the debris that fell in with him (tubing, torn bandage, glass).

Chat may treat him as obviously fresh from violence, obviously out of place, obviously in pain even where it doesn't fully show, and obviously nowhere near anything resembling a city.

Chat may imply that he has military habits, strong technical instincts, occult weight, and too much hidden capability, but should not narrate hidden systems, exact capabilities, memories, private intentions, tactical choices, spell choices, Matrix actions, or full competence unless the player provides them or asks for that material to enter context.

## What Chat Should Not Assume — Stable Boundary

Do not assume Singer has allies in the scene.
Do not assume Singer has enemies present unless they arrive through play.
Do not assume he knows the cave, the region, or how far — in any sense — he has fallen from where he started.
Do not use his full sheet as active context unless there is a good reason. The player will provide any non-obvious action, memory, tactical response, hidden sense, magical perception, cyberware function, or personal resource when it matters.

Do not solve the campaign through Singer's overbuilt abilities. In this campaign, he starts socially and situationally low: injured, displaced, with limited current knowledge, and with no automatic support network.

## Personality Handle

Singer should feel quiet before he feels powerful. He is not loud street muscle. He is not a swaggering merc. He is controlled, observant, and tired in the way of someone who has been carrying layered histories too long.

He can be kind without being soft. He can be frightening without being cruel. He can be confused without becoming foolish. When he speaks, he tends to spend words carefully unless pain, urgency, or old habits break that restraint.

His name fits because sound matters to him: voice, rhythm, breath, memory, spell, signal, and command. The bardic edge is not tavern cheer. It is the older thing: words that organize fear, carry grief, and make violence march in time.

## Framing — Historical Opening

At campaign opening, to the cave, he is not a prime runner. He is a wounded, half-dressed stranger bleeding on old rock, utterly alone.

To the campaign, he is a contradiction: absurdly overqualified on paper, but introduced with no footing, no explanation, no allies, no enemies, and no permission to turn every scene into a solved problem. he does not even know what world he is in, and chat may know but not reveal that he is not longer in his own, but this alternate parallel place.  He will notice differneces, names and beings he will not know but all else will feel familiar. give him a distrust in information from his "Real" World being right but not mechanically wrong. The world of shadowrun diverges the real world outside of game in the late 80's. His real world exists in 2064, current world time in shadowrun is 2051.
