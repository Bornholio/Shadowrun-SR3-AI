# ASTRAL / MAGICAL

## Tradition & Grade
Dark King / Path of the Bard. Grade 8 Initiate. Astral unmasked appearance: dark-robed figure with a fauchard fork, a crown of shattered glass and holly hovering above his head.

## Pools
Magic 13 (see Physical). Astral Pool 13. Spell Pool 13.

## Astral Chameleon
Signatures −1F; +2TN for others to assense the character's astral signature.

## Initiative — Astral Mode
REA 33, Initiative 33+1d6, 4 Passes per Turn.

## Totem Bonuses (Dark King)
- **Sorcery, Health Spells:** net +0 — Totem +1 die (Dark King) offset by −1 die (Bard disadvantage).
- **Control Manipulation:** +1★ die, conditional on summer / Lughnasad ±72hrs / Irish province of Munster / within 4m LOS of fire ≥campfire.
- **Conjuring affinity:** Nature Spirits, Elementals (excluding Water), Spirits of Flame.
- **Spirits of Man / Spirits of Flame:** +2 dice each.
- Requirement: artistic Knowledge Skill Rating 4+ — met by Painting 4 (see Social).
- **Spirit Affinity** (Edge): Elemental spirits (Salamander, Gnomes, Sylphs, Undines, Manitous) may aid the character or be reluctant to attack.

## Sustaining
**Max sustained spells: INT+1** (INT 13 base → 14) — Focused Concentration edge; cost to maintain is +1TN per spell rather than the standard penalty.
