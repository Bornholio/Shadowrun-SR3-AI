# Interpersonal 15 19

Chronological interpersonal history from Sessions 15–19. Repeated state summaries, portrayal instructions, future-facing guidance, and material already established in earlier session ranges have been removed. Older relationship state is retained only where the change itself is part of the history.

## Wednesday, May 31, 2051 — night

### Full-dragon intimacy

After the Urdesh aftermath, Singer asked what Glaze wanted. Glaze chose food, time on the roof in full dragon form, intimacy, and later returning to the Urdesh recording and the Alachia question.

On the roof, Singer spent hours touching and attending to Glaze in full dragon form—flanks, neck, horn bases, wing roots, face, and heartbeat—while singing and talking to her. Glaze actively directed the contact, changed pressure and pace when she wanted, asked him to look at her and talk to her, joked, teased, and enjoyed the attention.

Singer correctly spoke Glaze's dragon name in Dragon Tongue. Her reaction was strongly positive. Eventually she returned to human form, kissed him, told him that **no one had ever sung like that for her**, and led him downstairs. Sexual intimacy followed.

This was a substantial change from the earlier period when Singer was still learning how to approach her body and forms: Glaze was now openly directing affection in her dragon form rather than merely permitting contact.

---

### Reproductive uncertainty becomes shared planning

After sex, Singer raised the possibility that they might accidentally discover whether pregnancy between them was possible.

Glaze agreed that accident was not how she wanted that question answered. Neither knew whether Singer's heavily altered metahuman biology and Glaze's dragon-origin human form were reproductively compatible.

Singer's history with his late wife made the subject emotionally complicated, but neither used that history to pressure the other toward or away from children. They agreed that understanding the possibility should come before chance made the decision for them.

---

### Tir, Alachia, and Horrors

Singer and Glaze continued investigating Tir history, Alachia, immortal elves, Horrors, and the conflict between Singer's parallel-history memories and evidence in this world.

Singer supplied modern search capacity and his remembered future history. Glaze tested contradictions, dates, publication histories, language, foreign reporting, and curated public narratives rather than accepting either source automatically.

Singer said he believed Alachia was immortal and described language slips that suggested anachronistic knowledge. Glaze treated those as evidence rather than proof.

Their Horror discussion remained unresolved. Singer described the possibility that moral weakness creates intrusion points, that Alachia may have discovered some form of ward, that Mother Earth can function as an opaque barrier similar to Glaze's cave, and that future humanity may eventually need to fight Horrors rather than only hide from them.

Singer eventually said he had had enough negativity and ended the discussion, noting that Glaze had centuries while he hoped for at least two or three. Glaze accepted the stop.

---

### Firearms lesson

Singer asked whether Glaze wanted to shoot guns in the front lot. She agreed.

He physically adjusted her grip, arms, hips, legs, and stance without awkwardness between them. Glaze began as a firearm novice, learned quickly, and particularly liked the rifle. Singer emphasized movement, cover, concealment, barriers, deception, and less-lethal ammunition rather than simply exchanging fire.

The lesson was ordinary shared activity between lovers. It only happened to immediately precede the Aztechnology attack.

## Thursday, June 1, 2051 — Aztechnology attack and settlement

When Singer detected the incoming military force, he gave Glaze immediate tactical direction while he moved toward the threat. Glaze followed because he had the relevant information and seconds mattered.

After the fighting, Glaze's first significant question was:

> **"Are you hurt?"**

Singer said no, explained that negotiators were coming, and admitted he was too angry to negotiate cleanly.

Glaze answered:

> **"Yes. Ask me."**

Singer then gave her substantially more than an advisory role:

> **"You can decide what you want out of them."**

He gave broad preferences and deliberately handed Glaze authority over the settlement with Aztechnology.

Glaze rejected vague community-relations money and pushed for concrete restitution and durable benefit: neighborhood repair, food and shelter support, water infrastructure, a real clinic with continuing support, and non-retaliation toward the household, tenants, workers, and neighbors. She was willing to let Aztechnology attach its name and public story to the projects if it actually performed.

Singer remained present but did not take the negotiation back from her. This was a major expansion of the earlier **"You let me have the room"** dynamic: Glaze was now trusted with consequences at megacorporate scale.

Singer also screened incoming negotiators for recording and intelligence collection before letting them meet Glaze. The first pair violated the no-recording condition and were sent away; the second pair complied sufficiently to be admitted. Astral investigation was also made an explicit boundary during the settlement, and Glaze backed that boundary rather than allowing semantic workarounds.

After the crisis, Singer again shifted rapidly from extreme violence toward treatment, release, explanation, and concern once the threat was controlled. His later **"Well that was exciting"** returned them to their familiar understatement after danger.

The attack also reinforced that Singer's late wife and son remained emotionally real to him. Glaze treated that old family as part of Singer's life rather than as competition with their relationship.

## Thursday, June 1, 2051 — afternoon and evening

Singer noticed that the attack had destroyed Glaze's earlier plans and made room for her to recover the day.

Glaze chose to leave the poison, corporate conflict, and old catastrophe behind for a while. They took a recreational motorcycle trip toward Whidbey/SSC. Glaze repeatedly chose direction and pace. On a private coastal stretch she took full dragon form to swim; Singer used Oxygenate so they could remain underwater together and later used spirit assistance to race and play around her. Glaze explicitly regarded the outing as a good date.

Back at Taetzel, a shared shower became intimate and sexual. Singer improvised poetry about Glaze's different forms and wrote it down when she asked him to preserve it. A private voice recording of the poem also remained on Glaze's pocket secretary.

The day demonstrated that a major crisis did not require a prolonged repair scene before they could return to recreation, sex, humor, and affection.

## Friday, June 2, 2051 — early morning

Singer and Glaze went out hungry, encountered the Stuffer Shack violence, and afterward returned naturally to food, washing, study, joking, and affection.

### Tactical language

Glaze acted independently during the incident rather than waiting for Singer to assign every move. Afterward Singer apologized for not defining **LETHAL** earlier and explained that he meant lethal force was authorized or appropriate, not that Glaze surrendered her choice of method.

They clarified their preferred communication layers: line-of-sight telepathy for fast nuanced exchange, radio when separated, and sign language for silent basic direction and status. Singer began the sign-language lesson with the sign for **"poop."**

The same conversation also explicitly identified **"that is what she said," "in my pants,"** and **"for tax purposes"** as recurring joke structures rather than literal instructions. Glaze was learning Singer's timing and interference humor alongside vocabulary.

---

### Independent technical access

Singer gave Glaze useful access to his research environment and search tools while still keeping sensitive material compartmentalized. Her growing independence no longer required Singer to be the only path into modern information systems.

While reviewing Mitsuhama material, Singer opened his eyes crying and said:

> **"Bastards killed Coin-spinner and David."**

He then explicitly separated the personal memory from this world's established history:

> **"Not this world."**

Glaze now knew Coinspinner and David were emotionally important people from Singer's parallel history and saw him distinguish grief and remembered history from present-world certainty even while upset.

---

### Dragon-interface work becomes genuinely collaborative

When a form-change/interface problem emerged, Singer abruptly asked Glaze:

> **"Can you enchant?"**

Her answer changed the project. The work stopped being a device Singer was simply engineering for Glaze and became a division of expert domains in which Glaze's enchanting knowledge exceeded his for part of the problem.

Singer also said they needed priorities because he wanted time to **sleep, eat, and do other things with Glaze** instead of allowing every solvable technical problem to consume their lives.

Glaze chose a concrete personal goal: become independently competent with Matrix and digital systems so she could research, communicate, and act without Singer permanently mediating the tools.

---

### "Aggressive" acquires a shared meaning

Glaze had understood **aggressive** as stronger effort or firmer scheduling. Singer corrected the scale:

> **"Mine is closer to military conquest than strong effort."**

He explained that a bottleneck might lead him toward burglary, astral intrusion, Matrix conflict, or raid-like action even when he still regarded those methods as morally extreme.

He then distinguished attacking an external problem from overriding Glaze:

> **"I don't want to do that to you, but I am willing to do the part that clones me."**

And regarding public exposure:

> **"I'm not willing to risk your exposure until you desire it."**

By the end of the exchange, they shared a much more precise understanding of what Singer meant when he proposed being aggressive about a problem.

## Saturday, June 3, 2051

### Reproductive research becomes an outside collaboration

The reproductive question moved from private discussion into an actual medical/research relationship with **Dr. Miriam Okafor**.

Glaze conducted much of the substantive conversation herself while Singer repeatedly left the room or shifted to domestic tasks. When Singer asked whether she wanted to specify her status, Glaze personally chose to tell Okafor that she was a **female Western Great Dragon**.

Glaze respected Okafor because she separated curiosity from entitlement, could say **"I don't know,"** and continued thinking carefully after she understood how sensitive the work could become.

The research remained an attempt to understand compatibility and preserve future choice, not a decision to conceive.

---

### Singer shares more family and biological history

During the consultation, Singer told Glaze and Okafor more about himself. Glaze heard directly that Singer was **95**, had been born human male, had passed through multiple expressions including a later Night One state, and possessed genetic modifications so extensive that even he did not claim to understand every causal layer.

He also described the fertility history with his late wife: years of treatment and failed IUI attempts before their son was born in **2010**, expressed immediately, and required surgery to survive. Singer and his wife later underwent further expression changes themselves.

Singer became visibly gloomy while talking about his wife and son and stopped when he could no longer articulate more. Glaze stayed with the conversation without forcing him to continue.

---

### Ordinary domestic life

The rest of June 3 contained comparatively little crisis. Singer and Glaze made a rural food trip, Glaze handled purchases and ordinary public interaction herself, they spent relaxed time around the university, shared dinner with Okafor, and returned to Taetzel for domestic work.

Food storage, laundry, guests, research, technical design, humor, and karaoke were becoming ordinary parts of their home life rather than pauses between emergencies.

## Sunday, June 4, 2051

### Singer's recovery becomes physically obvious

Singer woke during the night, kissed Glaze, said he intended to spend about an hour checking Cole's work, and went to treat contaminated water magically.

When he returned, he told Glaze that for the first time since she had met him he was **not cold** because his internal factories were no longer replacing nanites. He returned to bed and slept beside her.

Glaze had known Singer throughout a period when his body was still performing enormous repair work after his arrival. His warmth gave her a direct physical marker that this phase of recovery had substantially changed.

---

### Glaze runs the morning herself

Singer slept while Glaze handled the morning. She met Cole, decided when Singer should or should not be disturbed, asked her own questions, answered Sound Interface Works about her horn/body interface requirements, and told Okafor that the future laboratory should allow her to perform routine measurements herself.

Unlike earlier milestones where Singer deliberately stepped back during a specific conversation or outing, Glaze simply operated the household and active projects because they were also hers.

---

### Blue Mountain Holdings becomes explicitly equal ownership

After the Aztechnology implementation meeting, Singer explained that their holding company was extraterritorial and that Glaze owned exactly **50%** of it. Neither could unilaterally bind the corporation into debt where a majority shareholder vote was required.

Glaze also identified a possible institutional trap in the proposed clinic and separated ownership of the physical space from control of medical operations: BMH could provide the facility while an independent clinic organization ran the medicine and Aztechnology met its funding and equipment obligations.

By this point Glaze was not merely being consulted about Singer's projects; she was acting as an equal owner with her own judgment about shared obligations.

## Monday, June 5, 2051

### Glaze takes Singer out

When Singer asked what Glaze wanted to do, she chose a day specifically **not attached to one of Singer's errands**. She wanted to decide where they went, handle ordinary arrangements, decide when they left, and later have a computer problem of her own.

Glaze chose the Westwind. Singer initially assumed he would drive, then explained the controls and put her in the driver's seat.

She used autonav as assistance, corrected a bad parking attempt, and drove them home later with noticeably more ease. Singer retained technical awareness of the vehicle but did not secretly take control; at one point he closed his eyes and trusted her to drive.

Glaze chose the Aquarium, Pacific Science Center, Pioneer Square/Seattle Underground, and coffee. She handled admissions, payments, ordering, bargaining, purchases, and directions herself. She bought small things because she wanted them, including a plush octopus, scarves, books or guide material, and a carved bird.

Glaze later said that she had initially wanted to prove she could take Singer somewhere, but by lunch proving it mattered less than simply having a good day together.

### Joy at Taetzel

Singer came home visibly happy. While cooking he sang loudly when Glaze was not nearby and dropped back to humming when she entered. Glaze noticed, caught him doing it, and teased him.

At dinner Singer used the entertainment system to build a cheerful environment around the songs he had been singing and then sang directly to Glaze. She began humming back and did not stop when he noticed.

His more complete astral presentation emerged, Cruit and Tiompán sounded, invoked spirits moved around the building, and the Taetzel hearth spirit beat time nearby. The home's magical resonance carried Singer's joy rather than grief or threat, and Glaze joined him in it.

Singer ended by kissing her and asking whether they should work on **her** computer problem.

### Glaze begins practical Matrix independence

Glaze clarified that she wanted independence from needing Singer for ordinary digital tasks, not independence from him as a partner. Her immediate goal was ordinary legal competence: communications, research, records, purchases, travel, money, routine computer use, and enough understanding to recognize when something unusual or dangerous was happening.

Singer built a safe training environment and gave her concrete tasks rather than a lecture. Glaze had to find conversations and information, distinguish public directory information from routing truth, work with Null Operations, and locate/decrypt a protected file titled **SONGS ABOUT GLAZE**.

She enjoyed being able to make inexpensive mistakes and revise her model without real-world consequences.

Singer then connected her through his **hitcher jack** so she could observe his own Matrix work. She experienced the enormous difference between physical and Matrix time and watched identity, authority, communications, storage, concealment, pursuit, and departure become one continuous expert act in his hands.

Singer deliberately made clear that his own scale was abnormal rather than a reasonable standard for ordinary competence. Glaze consequently chose legal everyday competence first and treated illegal decking as optional rather than necessary.

Singer provided **Computer Basics for the New Employee — Fuchi Corporate**, a 54 MP expert-system training utility, as her routine training path. Glaze also wanted visibility into any later educational version Singer produced from it because, in her words and reasoning, whoever designs the teacher determines what ignorance remains.

The evening ended with Glaze studying while sitting on Singer's lap, Singer resting against her and listening to her heartbeat, and the two eventually going to bed.
