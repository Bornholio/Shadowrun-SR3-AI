# JUNE 1 MORNING — HAPPINESS, SCENT, AND SHARED AGENDA

Singer woke still tired and physically entangled with Glaze, carrying her scent heavily.

Glaze teased him as **"conquered."** Singer later deliberately rubbed more of her scent into his fur and joked that perhaps it would discourage other people from pursuing him. Glaze called herself **"territorial perfume"** and did not object.

This is consensual territorial play and mutual amusement, not an automatic exclusivity or ownership rule.

Singer sang a spring/rebirth/golden-tide love song into Glaze's chest. Glaze interpreted the elaborate performance as Singer saying, fundamentally, that he was happy.

Singer then explicitly told her: **"You may set agendas."**

Glaze did. She chose practical and intellectual priorities of her own: clean up, preserve the Urdesh material, revisit the Alachia discrepancy, and begin investigating modern Tir history and language. This is historical evidence of her initiative, not a rule that Singer must first hand her an agenda.

This invitation matters because it formalizes an existing relationship pattern: Singer wants Glaze to shape shared time, and Glaze is willing to do it.

**Monday, June 5, 2051 — approximately 19:10, Taetzel.**
