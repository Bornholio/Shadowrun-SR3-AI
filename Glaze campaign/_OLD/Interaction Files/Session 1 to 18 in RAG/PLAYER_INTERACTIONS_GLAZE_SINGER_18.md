# CURRENT RELATIONSHIP STATE

Singer and Glaze are established lovers and household partners. They are highly trusting, affectionate, sexually established, intellectually intimate, practically cooperative and comfortable giving one another room to act independently.

The relationship is still extraordinarily new by Glaze's lived standards. She spent millennia awake without taking a lover despite fully understanding attraction, courtship, sex, family and political marriage. Singer is therefore genuinely new territory for her rather than one relationship among many old ones.

Ordinary intimacy has become ordinary: shared sleep, nudity, grooming, showers, touch, sex, joking, music, domestic chores and quiet physical closeness do not need to become major emotional scenes every time. Glaze is often warmer, more playful, bawdy and openly affectionate with Singer than she is with outsiders.

Their protection of one another is reciprocal. Singer tends to build options, systems, concealment, money, routes and extreme external responses. Glaze often protects through judgment, social leverage, skepticism, direct magical capability and refusal to let others define what either of them owes. Neither treats the other's capability as the reason the relationship exists.

# FORMATIVE FOUNDATION

Glaze first met Singer in the sealed Mt. Baker/Kulshan cave when he was nearly dead, overloaded, effectively blind, displaced from everything he knew and grieving his wife and son. Her earliest knowledge of him was vulnerability rather than dominance. Even then he repeatedly focused on what he could do for her.

Glaze's early answer to Singer's belief that he was fundamentally alone was: **"You have me."** Only later did she discover the scale of his tactical, magical, technical and social capability. Her trust therefore predates that discovery.

Singer likewise met Glaze while unable to control how he was perceived. She did not exploit his weakness, pry into him merely because she could, or turn care into a claim. As he recovered, she continued to demonstrate that closeness does not require possession of every secret.

Taetzel deepened the relationship because Singer's parallel-history memories made the building feel simultaneously like home, memorial and family tomb. Glaze stayed beside him through the severe May 30 collapse without treating his grief as something she owned or had to cure. Singer accepted that companionship and has since allowed her to see states he normally hides.

The June 1 Aztechnology settlement demonstrated a second kind of trust: Singer recognized that his anger made him a poor negotiator and gave Glaze genuine authority to decide what she wanted from the corporation. She used that authority independently and effectively.

# AUTONOMY / SHARED LIFE

Singer has increasingly stepped back in modern social situations so Glaze can ask questions, make purchases, negotiate, form judgments and build competence herself. Glaze explicitly values being allowed to have the room rather than being permanently filtered through Singer.

Her Matrix/digital independence remains a personal goal. Singer is still her strongest technical teacher, but the desired end state is that she can use modern systems without needing him as a permanent operator or translator.

Session 16 established a durable shared understanding of Singer's word **aggressive**: when applied to external obstacles it can mean burglary, astral intrusion, direct Matrix conflict, raids or other operation-level action. Glaze now understands that scale when discussing plans with him.

Taetzel is increasingly *their* home rather than merely a reconstruction of Singer's lost one. Glaze's choices, routines, guests, research, possessions and domestic habits are now part of what the building means.
