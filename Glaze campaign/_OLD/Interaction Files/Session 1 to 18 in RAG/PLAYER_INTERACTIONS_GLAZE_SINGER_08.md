She said yes, naming fixers, dancing, DJs, English, Singer singing at her, and Singer letting her lead. The first bed still needed fresh linens so they moved to the second; Singer settled against her and fell asleep almost immediately when she closed her eyes.

## May 27
**Glaze chooses the day.** Singer handed her the agenda. She handled housekeeping and breakfast herself in English, selected the Space Needle, made the reservation, and later chose how long to stay at the carnival and the Rhino. She enjoyed discovering ordinary systems herself; Singer used leading questions and demonstrations rather than reflexively solving everything.

**Introversion and time.** At the carnival Singer told her he is normally introverted off work. Glaze believed him and separated ease of conversation from needing strangers or noise. He then described his old near-continuous work pattern and said he would rather give his time to her.

**Visibility.** He told her that until recently he seldom traveled without some combination of disguise, Mask, invisibility, spirit Concealment, computer obscuration, camera destruction or drone hijacking. With Glaze he has hidden far less: **"If you are not hidden, neither am I."** She liked the intimacy of the choice but explicitly refused to make it an obligation — he does not owe the world his face merely because he stands beside her, and hiding for good reason is not withdrawal. What matters is that he spent years preventing outsiders from assembling all the pieces of him and is voluntarily letting her see them.

**Family and support.** He clarified that supporting her means **like family** — if she does not work, he will. His present ambition is not money for its own sake, but for her he would "move mountains, even if slowly." Glaze accepted the model directly and set out her own terms on work (Part 1).

**Housing.** Singer taught her how security rating, surrounding infrastructure, schools, transit, space, comfort, entertainment and location affect housing cost. The Queen Anne examples let her understand why an expensive family house can be rational even where she personally would not buy. Her refined requirements are in Part 1. When she made a joking hypothetical about future children and Singer becoming unbearable about schools, the exchange was affectionate and deliberately hypothetical — no plan for children exists.

**Prejudice without fear.** At the Big Rhino she watched Singer answer an actually prejudiced troll with humor and an arm-wrestling wager, win, then waive the promised payment. She understood that the troll stayed anti-elf while developing personal respect for Singer, reinforcing that individuals and group prejudice need not be flattened into one reaction. She enjoyed the Rhino precisely because it centered ork and troll physical scale and informal customs rather than merely tolerating them.

**Karaoke.** She discovered an activity where competence is not the point — people sing because everyone is allowed to be loud together. Singer encouraged rather than corrected her English performance, and she deliberately asked not to be rescued from pronunciation unless she accidentally insulted someone's mother. They left their seats voluntarily as the Rhino filled. The experience strengthened her sense that modern leisure can be communal without being transactional or educational.

**Dillon's reciprocity.** Singer told Glaze that Dillon's successful information work made him more reliable, and that they should ensure Dillon profits so the relationship stays reciprocal. Glaze agreed with the principle while rejecting invented debt — the better model is using him for work where his professional role genuinely earns money. That happened the same night: Singer's Grackle software sale paid Dillon substantially, and Singer later authorized him to resell ordinary catalog software at his own street markup. A practical example of maintaining a fixer through mutual utility.

**VR.** Singer brought Glaze into his forest-glade VR meeting space and taught her virtual movement and information manipulation in Sperethiel — intimate partly because it exposed another environment in which he has long functioned naturally while appearing physically still. She learned that VR space is not physical geography; walking is often only a metaphor and movement can be discontinuous; virtual architecture can organize information rather than represent distance; and immersion leaves the meat body vulnerable, like astral projection in the practical sense of attention being elsewhere. He mitigates that with spirits, automated conditions and tactical-computer sensory watch — which explains why he can appear motionless while working intensely.

He also demonstrated a high-end competitive game and deliberately understated his hardware as a Fairlight-class deck rather than exposing the far more expensive cyberware truth. Glaze saw his cybernetic response advantage become so extreme that the game read it as cheating. She wants to try trodes; he wants to verify they are safe for her first.

**End of night.** After VR they returned fully to the physical hotel room. Singer moved toward intimacy and Glaze reciprocated; they ended the night together and settled to sleep. Intimacy can now follow an ordinary shared day without requiring a special declaration.

## May 28 — morning and early afternoon
**Overnight comfort.** Singer became cold again during the night, pulled the second bed's covers onto the shared bed, and continued sleeping wrapped around Glaze. In the morning he asked whether he had made her too hot. They now adjust around each other's temperature, sleep and physical presence as ordinary bodily comfort.

**Medical disclosure.** He showed her the character-like medical display visible beneath the skin of his forearm and explained its functions, including his current nanite and nanosymbiote recovery state (Part 1).

**Fertility becomes a research priority.** He explicitly connected their sexual relationship to the unresolved compatibility question, described the risk as a possible "moment of weakness," and said he intends to find a parazoologist. He then stated his attraction plainly: Glaze is "irresistible." Her established answer stands — understand the consequences before accident decides them.

**Shared planning.** He laid out current practical pressures aloud (identity, rental car, bike, hotel, Taetzal, shell corporation). Glaze chose **morning lessons** before those complications. Domestic and operational coordination without Singer becoming her manager or Glaze becoming passive.

**English training.** Roughly a half-day of intensive VR/AR goggle training, then continued practical immersion while moving through waterfront shops, using signs, labels, objects and ordinary transactions as language material.

**Waterfront and lunch.** They watched fishing boats unload, bought fresh fish and crab and minimal cooking equipment, and cooked lunch at Seacrest Park — ordinary shared errands, Singer using real environments as teaching material, and a relaxed outdoor meal.

## Present point
Lovers with an increasingly ordinary domestic rhythm layered over the campaign's active practical problems. Newest developments: sleeping wrapped together and adjusting bedding and temperature has become mundane comfort; the fertility question is now an active research concern with a parazoologist as the intended route; Singer expresses strong sexual attraction openly while prioritizing informed consequences and protection; they coordinate near-term priorities together, with Glaze choosing morning lessons; intensive VR/AR-assisted English plus real-world immersion continues her independence trajectory; the waterfront outing and lunch were ordinary shared time. The immediate tone is affectionate, sexually comfortable, practical, playful and intellectually reciprocal.
