# Player Interaction Carry-Forward

Use this file for continuing relationship, roleplay, and interpersonal state involving primary chat-controlled player characters and other recurring characters.

This is an end-of-session carry-forward record, not a transcript. Preserve interaction details that may change how characters speak, react, trust, cooperate, argue, avoid, assist, or set boundaries in later sessions.

Do not put inventory, Karma, combat mechanics, general plot summaries, or unrelated bookkeeping here.

## Carry-Forward Reminder

At session close, preserve enough interpersonal detail that future dialogue and reactions remain continuous.

Do not flatten a developed relationship back to generic friendliness, hostility, attraction, professionalism, or suspicion simply because the prior scene is no longer in context.

Do not lose small but meaningful interaction details when they establish trust, boundaries, recurring humor, private knowledge, emotional pressure, or expectations.

Primary chat-controlled player characters retain control of their own decisions, thoughts, motives, and undeclared actions. This file records established interaction state; it does not authorize Chat to decide those characters' future behavior.
