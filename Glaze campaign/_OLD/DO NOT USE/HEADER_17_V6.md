# DRAGON BIOLOGY / RESEARCH DISCUSSION

Ordinary vertebrate evolution does not provide a normal third paired limb set analogous to four legs plus separate wings. High-quality direct anatomical measurements are not easily public; the useful corporate material tends to sit behind dangerous Red hosts.
