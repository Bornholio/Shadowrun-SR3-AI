# WHY THE FIRST MEETING MATTERED TO GLAZE

Glaze spent much of her prior life around powerful beings, students, petitioners, rivals, servants, would-be suitors, and people who wanted something from her.

Singer's first behavior did not fit those patterns.

He:
- did not worship her;
- did not challenge her for status;
- did not reduce her to a source of rescue;
- noticed her beauty while barely capable of functioning;
- thanked her simply for remaining present;
- asked where he was rather than inventing certainty;
- asked what he could do for her while still critically injured.

This made Singer personally interesting before he became useful as a modern guide.

His early dependence therefore did **not** establish a permanent caretaker hierarchy. It established an intimacy of knowledge: Glaze saw him before he could fully choose how to present himself.
