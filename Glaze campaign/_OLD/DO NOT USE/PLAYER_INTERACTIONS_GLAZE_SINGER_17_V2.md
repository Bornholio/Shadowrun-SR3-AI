# CURRENT RELATIONSHIP EFFECT OF SESSION 16

The strongest new relationship fact is:

**Glaze now understands that Singer's ordinary operating intensity is much more severe than phrases such as "work hard" or "be aggressive" suggest, while Singer has made explicit that he does not apply that intensity to overriding her autonomy.**

Singer may attack external obstacles with extreme methods. Glaze's learning, visibility, risk acceptance, and choices remain hers.

# TACTICAL TRUST — INFORMATION IS NOT CONTROL

The Stuffer Shack fight gave Glaze a clearer direct experience of Singer's decision speed under civilian danger. The relationship consequence is not the combat result itself; it is that **Singer's tactical calls give Glaze information without making her an extension of his initiative or judgment**.

Glaze acted on her own assessment rather than waiting for Singer to assign each action. Singer likewise trusted her to handle parts of the aftermath without micromanaging her.

Afterward Singer apologized for not previously defining one tactical word:

**"LETHAL" means lethal force is authorized/appropriate.**

Glaze now understands the intended meaning. It does not create a standing command hierarchy and does not remove her right to choose a different method.

# COMMUNICATION LAYERS

Singer and Glaze established a shared communications model.

- **Telepathy when Glaze can see Singer.** Singer wants it used as fast conversational speech rather than isolated dramatic words. He estimates they can often exchange roughly **three to four short turns between physical actions**.
- **Radio when line of sight is broken.**
- **Sign language for silent direction, attention, and simple status**, while recognizing that it carries less nuance than telepathy or speech. Singer recommended **American Sign Language** and began the informal lesson with the sign for **"poop."**
- Military-style information overlays can be added later when Glaze has suitable user hardware.

This is a shared protocol, not a command structure.

# GLAZE'S DIGITAL-INDEPENDENCE GOAL

Singer asked Glaze to choose a middle-weight personal goal in one telepathic sentence. She chose:

This matters relationally because the intended endpoint is **less dependence on Singer as translator/operator**, not a permanent teacher-student structure.

2. become fluent with the interfaces she will actually use, including 'trodes, displays, menus, and DNI-style controls;
3. search, sort, cross-reference, and judge digital information without Singer building every query;

The first part is complete when Glaze can explain the basic architecture, identify the roles of common components/security, and follow an ordinary technical conversation without Singer translating it.

# THE AUTONOMY BOUNDARY

Singer then made the most important interpersonal distinction of the session:

**"I don't want to do that to you, but I am willing to do the part that clones me."**

He is willing to acquire resources, remove external barriers, duplicate his own labor, and take risks that multiply what he can contribute. He cannot use those methods to make Glaze understand something, choose something, or become ready before she is ready.

**"Our time together shows that I'm not willing to risk your exposure until you desire it."**

This is now an explicit current relationship boundary. Singer's protectiveness is not permission to decide Glaze's visibility forever, and his preference for efficient solutions is not permission to expose her because an operation would become easier.

When Glaze uses **aggressive** about a shared objective in the future, she understands the scale of external action Singer may hear in the word. It still does **not** authorize him to decide for her, compel her learning, or expose her without her acceptance.

Singer explicitly pointed to the prior morning and this night as evidence of how hard he operates under normal conditions.

Glaze's new insight is not merely that Singer is capable. She now has a better experiential scale for what **casual effort** means to him and how quickly he can move between domestic affection, extreme action, care, technical work, grief, and humor.

That knowledge should improve her ability to judge what she is asking for when she asks Singer to remove an obstacle. It should not make her passive or turn his capability into authority over her.
