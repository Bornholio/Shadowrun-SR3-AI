Excluding the two primary pressures—Aztechnology settlement performance and the Taetzel contamination/remediation—the unresolved secondary field is fairly broad. The ones I would be most concerned about losing are the genuinely old threads that already have an established route back into play. 

1. **OLD — Mt. Baker investigators.** Probably the strongest buried adventure thread. Multiple astral investigators were probing Glaze’s former cave, including an initiated/Masked magician and an artificial watcher. The watcher was expelled and the entrance reclosed, but **who they were, who sent them, what they learned, and why they were interested remain completely unresolved**. The Great Mountain Spirit is free now, so this has to advance through the investigators themselves rather than convenient spirit surveillance. 

2. **OLD — Organlegger network / Darnell Washington.** Darnell’s kidnapping was clearly part of something more organized than a random street crew: fake medical presentation, drugs, target material and pickup procedure. Singer dumped fragmented evidence toward law enforcement, but the larger organization was never identified or destroyed. Darnell memorized Singer’s number and can re-enter naturally by calling “Doc”; Singer has no direct route back to him. This is a good secondary threat because it can move itself without becoming a police investigation campaign. 

3. **OLD — Morgan / Perianwyr delayed residue.** The immediate Morgan estate mystery is substantially resolved and should **not** be resurrected as another generic investigation. However, the Header explicitly preserves a GM-only delayed beat: after Aztechnology fallout, Perianwyr and Morgan are intended to break with Aztechnology/disappear, material Singer had passed to his friend goes with them, and **one identity later becomes active in a recognizable way**. That is an actual future thread, not merely historical color. 

4. **OLD — Lin Hwang’s untouched residue.** The Taetzel building reference still preserves **one locked personal container in Lin Hwang’s quarters**, unopened, along with ordinary surviving electronics/chips. It is one of the few literal old unopened objects remaining on the 28th floor. It does not have to contain plot, but it remains unresolved until opened or deliberately ignored. 

5. **OLD — Tomas’s May 31 supply run.** Singer prepaid Tomas to source potable water/bleach, groceries, about 100 gallons of fuel, a roughly 900+ gallon tank if available, and a route to Happy Farms. No later continuity establishes completion. This is old enough that I would **close it rather than preserve it indefinitely**—delivery, partial delivery, inability to source one item, or a phone call explaining the remainder. 

6. **OLD — Marisol / protective poncho.** The materials and production-space relationship exist, but Singer still has to finish the poncho and show it to Marisol before she will allow unattended use of the expensive production equipment. Small thread, but it has a concrete completion condition. 

7. **Reproductive compatibility / Dr. Okafor.** The questions remain entirely unanswered: Singer-specific compatibility, altered biology, offspring/embryonic risk, and what transformation during gestation would mean. Okafor has offered the research route, but **no sample has been sent**. This is a good character/research thread but not something that should spontaneously become a pregnancy plot. 

8. **Glaze does not know she owns part of BMH.** She knows “we own a building,” but does not know she legally owns BMH shares, how shares work, or that her interest is held through Debbie Harry. That is an unresolved knowledge/reveal issue rather than a crisis, but it could matter substantially the first time ownership, voting, inheritance, liability, or corporate paperwork becomes concrete. 

9. **Dragon archive / Alachia / Tir research.** Singer assembled roughly **2,300 MP** of dragon research, but Singer and Glaze have never done the full systematic joint review. Their specific Tir/Alachia investigation is also unfinished: Jenna Ni’Fairra’s family line, possible Alachia connections, curated records, and the difference between Tír Tairngire and Tír na nÓg remain research questions. This is already tied to Glaze remembering Alachia without thorns and Singer’s unreliable Fourth-Age/parallel-history memories. 

10. **Horror preparedness / Awakened children.** This is intentionally long-range. Singer and Glaze have identified the eventual Horror problem and the question of whether modern magical society could ever resist rather than merely hide. Separately, Singer has raised neglected Awakened children as a concrete social problem Glaze may care about. **No particular child or project currently exists**, so this needs an external route before it becomes adventure rather than philosophy. 

11. **Glaze finding work/projects that are actually hers.** She has explicitly wanted something personally meaningful—teaching, languages, magic, enchanting, consultation, or something else—but has not chosen a modern role. This is a potentially useful secondary plot generator because it gives Glaze independent contacts and consequences rather than making everything originate with Singer. 

12. **Software business through Dillon.** The Grackle sale completed, so this is not itself unfinished. What remains is the standing commercial mechanism: Dillon can sell ordinary Singer software, while strange/high-rating/custom/suspicious requests come back to Singer. Best treated as an **event trigger**, not a scene that needs servicing every session. 

13. **Varga / “Channeling” and “the slow path.”** Singer gave Elena Varga a very narrow magical lead. She remembers those terms but has not acquired impossible future knowledge from them. This is a planted future professional/magical contact thread if her own investigation eventually produces something. 

14. **Taetzel secondary development.** Beyond remediation, there are several unfinished but nonurgent property directions: find a salvage/rebuilder source for broken appliances/tools/pumps; fuller CCSS/elevator/industrial restoration; possible 26th/27th-floor dragon-scale conversion; and general furnishing/repair acquisition. None yet has a named active contractor or deadline. 

15. **Personal travel/history threads.** Still hanging are the postponed Olympic Peninsula trip where Glaze could actually fly, a possible Vancouver Island visit to Singer’s grandparents’ graves, and Singer’s father’s North Dakota farmland as something he might eventually take over and rent. These are genuine future hooks, but currently voluntary personal material rather than pressure. 

16. **Trodes for Glaze.** Explicitly preserved as a future safety/technology question, never followed through. Very small, but still unresolved. 

There are also two things I would **not** count as unresolved despite their age: the old covenant/New Horizons access issue is finished, and the Urdesh name-loss puzzle itself is finished. Morgan/Hale/Northline is also essentially closed except for the specific delayed Perianwyr/Morgan future beat above. 

For actual adventure generation, the three most underused old threads are **Mt. Baker investigators**, **the organlegger organization**, and **the delayed Morgan/Perianwyr consequence**. Tomas should probably be resolved quickly rather than promoted into plot. ★
