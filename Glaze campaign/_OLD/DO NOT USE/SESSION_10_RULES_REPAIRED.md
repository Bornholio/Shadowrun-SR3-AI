# SESSION 10 — RULES / CAMPAIGN PROCEDURES
Shadowrun Third Edition.

This file preserves only durable rulings, conventions, and mechanical precedents from the Struggle session. It excludes chat-process failures and one-off narration mistakes that do not need to become campaign rules.

# DICE DISPLAY — RULE OF SIX
Every natural **6 always expands**.

When displaying meaningful rolls, show:
- dice pool;
- target number;
- final accumulated die values only;
- success count.

Do not leave an unexpanded natural 6 visible as a finished result.

# PURCHASING / AVAILABILITY
For grouped purchases, do **not** stack multiple base times by default.

Normally the longest relevant base time controls the group.

If compared items have materially different combinations of high TN/low base time versus low TN/high base time, use an actual roll or more detailed adjudication to determine which item controls completion.

# WEATHER CONVENTION
When the GM asks for Seattle-area in-game weather, use the **same calendar date in 1994** as the default analogue for 2051, unless another method/date/location is specified.

Translate the historical weather into practical SR3 scene conditions such as temperature, precipitation, wind, visibility, and street effects.

# ARCOLOGY PUBLIC ENTRY
Public/commercial areas of the Renraku Arcology do **not** automatically require every visitor to present identification at entry.

Restricted, security-sensitive, or controlled areas may still impose ID checks, screening, and access-control requirements.

# NPC NAMING CONVENTION
Future NPC names should normally be plausible common-use real-world names appropriate to background, ethnicity, region, and class.

Avoid conspicuously fantasy/roleplaying-style names or over-themed names created mainly to advertise archetype.

Handles may remain stylized when there is an in-world reason.

# MATRIX RESULT CONTINUITY
When the player/GM explicitly declares a completed Matrix result, treat that result as established campaign fact unless the GM later revises it.

Do not retroactively re-roll or reinterpret a declared completed run merely because a fuller procedure could have been used.

This session's established Matrix outcomes include:
- Westwind rental extension by two weeks;
- validated super-user/passcode route for return handling;
- Renraku-side New Horizons/Yakuza intelligence recovered;
- Blue Mountain Holdings recommendation letter planted/sent through a validated corporate route;
- library/interlibrary dragon archive of approximately 2,300 MP acquired;
- the associated stated tally/exit outcomes.

# KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARIES
Old corporate intelligence is not proof of present legal ownership or current organizational control.

Specifically, Renraku records describing the old New Horizons/Yakuza situation as a total loss and showing the original gumi as defunct do **not** establish who currently owns or controls the Taetzal access covenant.

Likewise, the New Horizons covenant-assignment trail overlapping Dillon's Kobayashi/former-Shigeda lead creates a negotiation path, not proof of ownership.

# ANCIENT MAGIC CLASSIFICATION
Ancient magical objects are not required to fit modern Sixth World focus categories.

Modern classifications may be used descriptively, but failure to fit a current category is not itself evidence that the object is invalid, impossible, or misunderstood.

Singer's Infinite Warehouse is established in play as:

- likely metaplanar or otherwise outside ordinary physical space;
