# Session Carry-Forward Header

Use this file as the primary end-of-session continuity record for the next session.

It should preserve enough detail that the next session can resume without reconstructing the previous one. Record changed state, unresolved plot information, commitments, consequences, NPC attitudes, and facts that may matter later. Do not compress away details merely because their importance is not yet obvious.

Do not use this file as a transcript, inventory sheet, character sheet, or rules reference. Keep bookkeeping and large mechanical records in their dedicated files.

## Next-Session Continuity Check

Before starting the next session:

- preserve the exact resume point;
- retain every significant unresolved plot thread;
- retain consequences that have not yet paid off;
- retain relevant NPC/faction knowledge and attitudes;
- retain commitments, deadlines, and promised future actions;
- preserve distinctions between confirmed information and speculation;
- do not silently discard small details that may connect to later plot;
- do not duplicate full bookkeeping or character mechanics into this file.

The purpose of this Header is continuity, not compression for its own sake. Losing a plot detail between sessions is worse than carrying a few extra lines forward.
