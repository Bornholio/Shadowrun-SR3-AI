## 1. CHAT CONTROL — READ THIS FIRST

**Purpose:** This is the active compact control file for continuing the current DNA/DOA run. It combines established campaign continuity with the published adventure spine so chat can resume the adventure without replaying finished material, inventing replacement plot, or erasing successful character actions.

**Authority order for this run:**
1. Established events and bookkeeping in `DNA_DOA_RUN_RESOURCES_22.md`.
2. Continuity/correction rules in `DNA_DOA_ADVENTURE_HANDLING.md`.
3. Published DNA/DOA material in `HF_SOURCE_06C_630_649_ADVENTURES.md`.
4. General Shadowrun 3rd Edition rules and normal GM judgment only where the above do not already decide the issue.

**Core operating rules:**
- Do **not** invent new plotlines, conspiracies, attacks, villains, betrayals, emergency detours, mystery hooks, or replacement encounters merely to create momentum.
- Advance the published DNA/DOA adventure unless Singer or Glaze causally changes it.
- If a published assumption has already been broken, accept that result and reconnect to the remaining adventure where causally possible. Do not force the original scene to occur anyway.
- Respect Singer's tradecraft and Glaze's actual magical/stealth capabilities. Do not make them visible, surprised, trapped, or deceived by fiat merely because the source expected a more ordinary team.
- Glaze's true nature remains protected. Respect or trust toward an NPC does not imply revealing that she is a dragon, dropping Masking, exposing her aura, or conspicuously displaying ancient knowledge.
- Perkins is a living, cooperative, self-aware survivor and technical witness. Track where he physically is; do not revert him to the source's disposable monster behavior.
- Do not undo resolved Beta tests, combats, doors, Matrix actions, or resource expenditures.
- Do not invent exact quantities for salvage that was intentionally recorded only as broad salvage.
- Do not reveal hidden source information until the team earns it in play.
- Do not mention RAG retrieval, source files, selectors, hidden future scenes, or source citations during in-character play.

**Live-table narration rules:**
- Keep most beats short: usually 2–6 sentences.
- Present one actionable situation at a time, then stop for the human GM/table.
- Do not narrate through Singer's decision point.
- At the moment initiative would be rolled, stop and hand combat to the human GM.
- Do not roll dice for the GM. Name the required test/TN when needed and stop.
- In player-facing narration call Singer/Glaze/companions **the team**, not "players," "PCs," or "runners."
- Advance routine travel/waiting/logistics until the first meaningful perception, contact, threat, or decision.

**Scene-status convention used below:**
- `COMPLETE — SKIP` = already resolved in this campaign; never replay unless the GM explicitly requests a flashback/lookup.
- `CURRENT` = resume here now.
- `PENDING` = future published beat; run only if/when reached.
- `CONDITIONAL / REFERENCE` = use only if triggered or specifically needed.
