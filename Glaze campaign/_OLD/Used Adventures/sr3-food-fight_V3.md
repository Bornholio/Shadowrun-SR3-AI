# FOOD FIGHT — SR3 Scenario Reference
Intro scenario, Shadowrun 3rd Ed. Purpose: teach the Combat Turn sequence. **Not meant to be do-or-die.** 

Taetzel is at 16801 Taetzel St. (92nd St.), Redmond, WA, UCAS.

## WHAT'S UP, CHUMMER?

Singer and Glaze are up late and hungry. There is exactly one option at this hour — the 24-hour Stuffer Shack down on the corner of 92nd and Pepper ave 24 blocks west and on the edge of Touristville and the industrial zone beside it . But nothing stays quiet for long on Seattle's streets. The moment the two of them are inside and foraging, the Stuffer Shack gets stripped of everything that isn't bolted down.

The thieves are a thrill-gang called the Chiller Thrillers. They have a serious case of the munchies and will load up on stuffers, but they are just as interested in whatever the customers are carrying. They will kill or maim to get loot. They will also kill for no reason at all. They fight until they are dead or incapacitated — they do not back down.

Singer and Glaze walk in and split up toward whatever aisles interest them. As soon as they've separated, the three Chillers already inside — SlicerDicer, Static, and Zany — signal Catcher that the place is ripe for plucking.

The rest of the gang piles out of their van in the parking lot. As they saunter in, Spike smashes the exterior PanicButton™ box so it can't be used. Anyone loitering near the entrance gets herded inside at gunpoint. When Spike kicks the door open, the three already inside draw and order everyone in sight to get their hands up.

## PREMISE

Glaze and singer are the PC's in this situation, glaze is being told basic facts that may be mentioned in this adventure.  

PCs are up late and hungry; the only option is the 24-hour Stuffer Shack on the corner. While they're inside foraging, a thrill-gang called the **Chiller Thrillers** robs the place. The gang wants stuffers, but also anything of value the customers are carrying. They will kill or maim for loot, or for fun. **They fight until killed or incapacitated — they never back down.**

Tone target: half a dozen chimpanzees going berserk in a supermarket, with big guns. Loud, shocking, and disorienting even for experienced runners.

Gang symbol: a skull pierced through the left eye socket with a bloody icicle.

## SCENE FLOW / TRIGGER SEQUENCE
1. **Approach.** Raining. Parking lot holds ~6 cars and vans, 2 bikes, an electro-scoot. Nobody loitering outside. If the team posts a lookout, that's fine — note it.
2. **Inside.** Ask each PC where they're headed. Let them shop. Runners would know Stuffer Shacks have average security and that carrying a weapon with **Concealability under 5** will clear the store and get Mr. Nick to hit the PanicButton for Lone Star — the *players* may not know, so mention it discreetly before they go in.
3. **Separation.** Three Chillers are already inside: **SlicerDicer** (walking down Aisle 1), **Zany** (sitting on the counter between Areas E and F, sipping a drink), **Static** (Aisle 8, in animated conversation with a toaster-oven display). Once the PCs scatter to their aisles, the three signal Catcher that the place is ripe.
4. **The signal** is SlicerDicer walking to the counter to flirt with Wanda. The gang can enter any time after that — let the PCs go about their business first. Build noise: Mrs. Needles screeching, Louis asking questions, Timmy staring at Wanda, Mr. Nick stocking.
5. **Entry.** The rest of the gang piles out of their van. **Spike smashes the exterior PanicButton** so it can't be used, then kicks the front door open. Anyone loitering near the entrance is herded inside at gunpoint. The three already inside draw and order everyone's hands up. Catcher fires a shotgun blast into the display behind the counter holding the camera monitors and announces the shakedown.
6. **Initiative.** The gang has Initiative at scene start. Players do **not** roll until after the door crashes in and Catcher makes his announcement — then they roll.
7. **Escalation.** Until a firefight starts, the gang is casually brutal: they take what they want, shoot objectors, and actively goad people into fighting. The longer they go unopposed the more wanton they get. Zany and Wiley body-search for valuables, starting with someone *other* than a PC. Once they've collected the valuables, they start smashing the place up.

## BYSTANDERS
All use **Average Metahuman** (SR3 GM Screen, *Critters* p.19) unless noted. PCs can make a **Perception Success Test (TN 8)** to spot concealed weapons on any of them — if the target notices, expect an aggressive reaction.

- **Wanda** — checkout clerk (Area A). Elf, bright orange hair cemented into a single spike. Reacts to events a few seconds late. Stays behind the counter. **She forgets to hit the PanicButton.**
- **Mr. Nick** — manager. Dwarf, practically lives at the store. Defiance shotgun behind his desk (Skill: Shotgun 2). Will hide the NutraSoy Energy Cakes™ boxes, then use his size to move behind displays and shelving toward his office; if he makes it, he tries to ambush the robbers from there. Starts at the Aisle 11 display.
- **Mrs. Needles** — obese human woman shopping with a small child. Shoves her cart down Aisle 5 like an Urban Brawl player, hides behind it, screams.
- **Louis Needles** — streetwise urchin, talks nonstop. Asks everyone whether they're shadowrunners (including the Chillers). Any encouragement and he escalates to dragons, ghouls, vampires, spirits.
- **Johnny** — stockboy, gangly and pimpled, stocking soup in Aisle 16. Goes flat on the floor immediately and stays out of sight.
- **Timmy Thinners** — scrawny elf in tattered black leathers, scoping the audio players in Area D, working up nerve to talk to Wanda.
- **Jack & Angie Scatman** — Aisle 7, rocker-royalty wannabes. Jack: white leather pants, sleeveless shirt, fringed vest, FauxGold™ jewelry, **Walther Palm-Pistol** (Pistol 1). Angie: black leather jumpsuit, zippers and mesh chain, **Beretta Model 101T** in her shoulder bag (Pistol 1). Both dive for the floor and will not fire except in self-defense.
- Also present: an obese woman with a cart of NutraSoy Energy Cakes™, and an equally obese child who wants one of everything, repeating "Stop that! / Drop that! / Put that down! / Get over here!"

## SUPERMARKET SWEEP — ENVIRONMENTAL RULES
The store is a dynamic battlefield. Things break, stuff spills, colorful substances go everywhere.

**Every time anyone fires a gun, roll 1D6 and consult the Food Fight Table.**
- Burst Fire: **+1** to the roll
- Full Auto: **+2** to the roll
- When a PC takes a shot, let the player roll their own result
- Then roll **2D6** on each mess column to describe what it looks like

**Results are cumulative** — the target suffers the result rolled *and* every result below it. A 6 also inflicts the 4–5 and 2–3 effects.

### Food Fight Table (1D6)
| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Nothing breaks. |
| 2–3 | Glop splashes over the target and everyone within 2 meters. That patch of floor becomes **Difficult Terrain**. |
| 4–5 | So much glop covers the target's face and arms that they suffer a **+2 modifier to all actions** from impaired visibility. |
| 6 | **Pyrotechnics!** Stuff explodes over the target and everyone nearby, and the damage causes secondary effects — falling cans, exploding light fixtures, chemical reactions, whatever you like. Target rolls **Body (4)** to resist **Light Stun**. |

## DEBUGGING
Runners are usually more professional than gangers, so the team should come out on top — but this can still kill PCs. If they look like they're about to be slaughtered, use one or more of these:
- Gangers stop shooting any character who is down and out (has taken a Serious or Deadly wound)
- The gang splits after grabbing Wanda, a bunch of goodies, and whatever cash and credsticks they've collected
- Invent your own out — the scene is a teaching tool, not a meat grinder

## AFTERMATH
The runners may have stopped the robbery, but the property damage far outweighs any gratitude Stuffer Shack Inc. might feel. **Lone Star is on the way** and the runners almost certainly don't want to explain their hardware. Police statements create a permanent record — "Just what is your SIN, citizen?" — which means the neighborhood is going to be far too well-lit for anyone who prefers the shadows.

If Johnny, Mr. Nick, or Wanda survived, they thank the characters and suggest they clear out before Lone Star arrives with questions the team won't want to answer. Mr. Nick tells them to help themselves to anything still edible on the way out.

## KARMA
Events like this are fairly routine in 2060, so surviving is worth **1 point**. The Chiller Thrillers are nasty opposition, so facing them down is worth **1 more**. Every character gets **2 Karma**, plus any individual awards you consider appropriate per SR3 p.244.

## RUNNING NOTES
- Do **not** let players roll Initiative until Catcher's shotgun blast — the gang acts first by design.
- Track which Chiller is where; the three inside are already positioned when the PCs walk in.
- Static and SlicerDicer both have behavioral triggers that can be exploited by clever players — reward that.
- The Food Fight roll happens on **every** gunshot, PCs and NPCs alike. It's the whole point of the scene.
- Concealability matters on entry (under 5 clears the store). Perception TN 8 for spotting concealed weapons on bystanders.
- Catcher and Zany carry the only real comm gear; jamming or dropping either one leaves the gang uncoordinated.
- The van is unattended and holds a Lone Star scanner.
- Weapon damage codes, ranges and Concealability are not listed here by design — look them up in `sr3-firearms` or the SR3 firearms table at the moment of the roll.
