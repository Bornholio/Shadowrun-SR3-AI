# DNA/DOA — Adventure Handling and Restart Control

## Purpose
This file is a compact GM control document for continuing DNA/DOA after the Beta facility. It records established divergences, likely player-caused breakpoints, and how to preserve the published adventure without railroading.

## HARD RULE — DO NOT INVENT PLOT TANGENTS
**Do not invent new plotlines, conspiracies, attacks, villains, betrayals, emergencies, detours, mystery hooks, unrelated NPC schemes, or replacement encounters whole cloth.**

The published DNA/DOA adventure and already-established campaign events are the active plot. Let that plot advance normally unless Singer or Glaze causally change it through their actions.

World/NPC action may expand only from facts already established by the adventure, campaign continuity, or a clearly motivated consequence of player action. Do not create an unrelated complication merely to make something happen. Do not substitute an invented event for the next published beat.

If Singer and Glaze bypass, expose, neutralize, or alter a published encounter, follow the consequences of that change and then reconnect to the remaining adventure wherever causally possible. Do not force the original scene to happen anyway, and do not replace it with a new invented plot.

## Baseline Adventure Continuation
Assuming Singer and Glaze continue to the S.S. Misha, the adventure should continue along its existing major trajectory unless their actions change it:

1. Sewer withdrawal and approach to Wharf 114 / S.S. Misha.
2. False Biogene contact / Alamos 20,000 confrontation.
3. Independent Aztechnology arrival at the wharf.
4. Warehouse / Jenny Hernandez / route toward Wilhem Park.
5. Bronston and **"Into the Flames."**
6. Wilhem Park problem and possible Alpha Section rescue.
7. Owens / kidnapped Orks / evidence resolution.
8. Return to Wilhem Park.
9. Biogene safe-house sequence, subject to Singer/Glaze detecting or altering the trap.
10. Adventure aftermath recalculated from actual events.

Do not skip these beats merely because Singer and Glaze are unusually capable. Their capabilities determine how the beats resolve, not whether the source plot is allowed to exist.

## Established Changes from Beta
- Singer and Glaze were at all relevant times either physically disguised as Orks or physically invisible. Their true physical identities were not exposed.
- Glaze has been deliberately protecting any evidence of her true identity as a dragon.
- Blount did not visually identify who attacked/disrupted him. His assensing observations cannot become an immediate report because he remains unconscious for hours.
- Singer removed relevant pre-mission and early-mission host/camera information and falsified later camera evidence.
- The stripping of Beta appears consistent with a much larger team operating for substantially longer than Singer and Glaze actually did. Physical forensics and falsified/missing camera data conflict, making surviving recordings unreliable.
- Singer rebuilt/recatalyzed the sewer-side breach enough to slow recognition of the actual ingress/egress route.
- Singer intentionally left alive infected subjects exhibiting the worst symptom profile while burning potential viable sample sources. The biological loss is greater than the facility damage alone.
- Beta lost substantial equipment, research continuity, data, sample material, and physical infrastructure.
- James Taylor and Debbie Harry currently have false GridGuide movement continuing elsewhere through the motorcycle.
- Singer's present tradecraft is restrained by his historical standards. Past Singer could have used far more extensive spirits, drones, vehicles, theft, deception, Matrix operations, thermite, explosives, and evidence destruction. Do not portray the current operation as his maximum capability.

## Wharf / S.S. Misha Handling
Aztechnology's arrival at the Misha **does not require Singer or Glaze to have been tracked from Beta.** The published timing must stand on an independent causal route.

Use the established explanation: Aztechnology is already tracking the Biogene side of the transaction and has information from penetrations/insiders associated with the Alamos 20,000 operation. Singer and Glaze can change timing or circumstances through their actions, but they are not the necessary cause of Aztechnology knowing about Wharf 114.

The false Biogene contact remains part of the adventure. Do not assume Singer and Glaze automatically fall for him, approach visibly, or remain exposed once the confrontation begins.

The wharf firefight may still occur between Alamos and Aztechnology even if Singer and Glaze are hidden, bypass the impostor, or decline to participate openly.

## Glaze — Capabilities That Must Not Be Forgotten
Glaze is not an ordinary runner with generic magic. When resolving scenes, remember that she may have access to capabilities including:

- Physical Mask.
- Improved Invisibility.
- Masking metamagic.
- Very high Stealth.
- Very high Assensing.
- Detect Life / Detect Enemies.
- Clairvoyance and other magical reconnaissance.
- Exceptional mundane and draconic senses.
- Conjuring and spirit services such as Concealment, Guard, Search, Confusion, and scouting where appropriate to SR3/domain rules.
- High intelligence and strong independent judgment.

Do not automatically activate these abilities for her, but do not write scenes as though they do not exist. Published "forced" visibility, surprise, pursuit, or confinement may fail if she chooses to use them.

Glaze has been working hard to conceal her identity. Respecting or liking an NPC does not automatically make her reveal that she is a dragon, expose her aura, use ancient knowledge conspicuously, or abandon her cover identity.

## Perkins
Perkins is now a living, cooperative, self-aware survivor and technical witness rather than a disposable monster encounter. His existence can affect later interpretation of the project, but do not force him into every scene. Track where he physically is and whether he is protected, concealed, present, or separated when scenes become dangerous.

## Bronston / Wilhem Park
Singer is already predisposed to admire Bronston. Do not manufacture hostility merely to create friction.

**"Into the Flames" is a world-defining speech and should be allowed to land as written in spirit and importance.** Do not rush past it, replace it with exposition, or interrupt it with an invented crisis.

Glaze is likely to take Bronston seriously for reasons independent of Singer:
- He survived the Night of Rage and turned catastrophe into durable community.
- He is preserving history, literacy, education, and cultural continuity for people with short generations and little institutional protection.
- He protects children while still intending them to become autonomous adults.
- He functions as a leader and teacher rather than merely a victim or gang chief.

Likely Glaze response: substantial respect, intellectual attention, curiosity, and willingness to engage directly. She may strongly appreciate what Wilhem Park represents. She remains autonomous and may disagree with Bronston, challenge coercion, or reject manipulative treatment of Singer, Owens, or others.

Do **not** equate respect with submission or identity disclosure. Glaze can value Bronston deeply while remaining under an Ork/other cover and withholding her true nature.

If Singer and/or Glaze choose to materially support Bronston or Wilhem Park, that can become a genuine continuing campaign relationship. It must arise from their decision, not GM invention.

## Singer — Likely Adventure Breakpoints to Evaluate in Advance
Do not assume Singer will do any of these, but be prepared for them because each can invalidate a published assumption:

- Authenticate or investigate the Misha contact before exposing himself.
- Inspect Matrix, communications, surveillance, traffic, or logistics around Wharf 114.
- Approach the dock indirectly or remain physically absent/invisible.
- Capture and interrogate Alamos personnel rather than simply fight them.
- Investigate how Alamos learned about the handoff.
- Give Bronston substantially more evidence about Peterhoff/Owens than the book expects.
- Offer Bronston/Wilhem Park money, equipment, communications, education, medical resources, or continuing support.
- Treat Alpha as a security system/problem to defeat rather than a room-by-room dungeon.
- Use Matrix, magic, social engineering, spirits, surveillance, or tradecraft to shorten or bypass Alpha obstacles.
- Contact Walker/Biogene directly rather than trusting an intermediary.
- Investigate the safe-house address, communications, surveillance, personnel, or surrounding traffic before walking inside.
- Send expendable reconnaissance, agents, spirits, drones, or false traffic ahead of himself.
- Detect Holmes/Honnicker/Alamos involvement before the safe-house ambush can form normally.

When Singer breaks a published assumption, evaluate the actual consequence and continue from there. Do not negate his competence to protect the scripted encounter.

## Alpha Section
Treat Alpha as a functioning secured facility with occupants, alarms, motives, response times, and geography — not as a mandatory linear room sequence.

The published objectives and NPCs remain real. Singer and Glaze may reach them by different means. Their possession of Beta data/sample material changes what Owens needs from them and what can already be established about Peterhoff's work.

Do not invent extra defenses merely because Singer and Glaze solve the published ones efficiently.

## Safe-House Sequence
This is the most fragile remaining scripted encounter.

Singer's normal verification and Glaze's perception/magic may expose the trap before they enter. If they do, let that success stand. Do not invent a second hidden ambush solely to restore the published scene.

Honnicker, Alamos, Holmes, Aztechnology, Walker, and Biogene should act only from their established knowledge, motive, resources, and timing. If the ambush fails, continue with the consequences of its failure rather than forcing another version of it.

## Narration / Pacing Rule
Advance routine travel, waiting, and logistics until the first meaningful event, perception, contact, or decision that could change Singer's action, then stop.

Do not dead-stop before enough of the triggered event has occurred to make the situation actionable. Conversely, do not narrate through Singer's decision point.

Most importantly: **advance the existing adventure. Do not replace missing momentum with invention.**

## Restart Principle
The restart should resume from established continuity after Beta withdrawal and move toward the S.S. Misha unless Singer changes course. The next meaningful developments should come from DNA/DOA's existing actors and circumstances, not newly invented plot material.
