## TR-TAETZELBUILD-002 - STATUS


# STATUS

**Owner:** Blue Mountain Holdings (BMH), legally separate from Singer personally.

**Ownership:** Singer and Glaze own all BMH shares. Glaze currently understands the property as something they own together; the corporate share mechanism has not been a current play issue.

**Address:** 16801 Taetzel St. / 92nd St., Redmond, WA, UCAS.

**Building:** 28 stories plus subfloor, roughly **70m x 70m**, late-2020s Festung construction. Floors 1-20 use the massive opaque reinforced shell; floors 21-28 use mirrored ballistic glass around large open plates.

The 28th floor is the occupied private home. Riku's motorcycle shop occupies part of the first floor. Major household power, communications, access control, cameras, service systems, rooftop solar and the Red host are functioning.
