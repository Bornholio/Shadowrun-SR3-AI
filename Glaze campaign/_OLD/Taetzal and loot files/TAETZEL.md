# SHELL / STRUCTURE

- **Footprint:** approximately 70m x 70m.
- **Floor height:** ordinary floors about **5m floor-to-floor**.
- **First floor:** about **9m**, double height.
- **Exterior floors 1–20:** structural opaque reinforced shell, **Barrier 32**. Intended to withstand extreme abuse and vehicle-scale threats.
- **Exterior floors 21–28:** continuous mirrored heavy ballistic-glass laminate, **Barrier 8 minimum**, with stronger points possible only where specifically established.

- **Upper ceilings:** drop-ceiling system; about 12 ft clear on the detailed 26th floor, roughly 2 ft above drop ceiling, then about a 1 ft insulated floor assembly.

- Singer has discussed eventually obtaining about a **9 m3 stainless tank** for toxic separation/segregation, on a roughly month-scale acquisition horizon rather than immediately;
- rain collection may supplement water needs but is not established as enough for full remediation.

Singer's intended remediation approach:

- create a separation/segregation tank or staging system;

He remembers the equivalent job taking more than two months in his parallel history and estimates perhaps about one month now. This is Singer's estimate, not a completed schedule.
