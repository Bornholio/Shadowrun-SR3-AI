# Taetzel Building — Full Layout

## STRUCTURE / BASIC PLAN
28 stories, roughly 30 years old, Festung style. Derelict office block in the middle of the slum.

**Walls & Barriers**
- Exterior walls, ground floor through 20th: Reinforced Concrete, **Barrier Rating 32**
- Exterior walls, 21st floor and up: Reinforced Armor Glass, **Barrier Rating 8** (top eight floors were "executive country," where a view was a perk even during the troubled twenties)
- Interior walls and doors: Normal Construction Plastic, **Barrier Rating 4**

**Core**
The building has a central core containing a bank of four passenger elevators, flanked by stairwells and lavatory facilities. At the back of the building are a freight elevator and an additional stairwell. The floor plate is broken by load-bearing pillars on a regular grid; the southern portion of each floor steps out wider than the northern portion (basic floor plan scale: 1 square = 5 meters).

**Surveillance & Automated Weapons**
Security cameras are present on **all** floors. Servoguns are installed at specific locations on the ground floor and 28th floor.

Servogun (automated shotgun):
- **Firearms "Skill" 6** — aiming mechanism only works at Short or Medium range
- **Damage:** 4M3
- **Initiative:** 20 (no roll required)
- **Armor:** Ballistic 3, Impact 0. Partially concealed by housing — attacks against them are at +2 for Partial Cover
- **Body:** 4

A decker can take over servoguns if he is in one of the appropriate nodes in the Taetzel Building computer.

## EXTERIOR GROUNDS
Surrounded by a three-meter-high chain-link fence. The fence is guarded by pressure sensors that alert the security center on the 28th floor if anyone tries to climb or cut it.
- Spotting the sensors: Unopposed Intelligence or Electronics Test, **TN 4**
- Disarming them: Unopposed Electronics Test, **TN 6**

Two agents patrol the perimeter, taking 15 minutes to complete a circuit of the grounds.

**Main Gate (A)** — South side. Locked, but can be opened from the Security or Command Terminals on the 28th floor.
**Parking Lots (B)** — Flanking the main approach. Empty, weeds growing out of cracks in the asphalt.
**Rear Gate and Loading Dock Entrance (C)** — Northeast corner, feeding the loading dock drive.

Mature trees screen the north and east sides of the building. Exterior plan scale: 1 square = 10 meters.

## GROUND FLOOR
**Lobby (1)** — Visible to the agent in the guard booth (Area 2). Equipped with a security camera that also has a servogun attached. The gun is an automatic weapon that can fire from the guard booth or the Security Terminal at any location in the room. 4M3 damage.
**Guard Booth (2)** — One guard present, viewing the lobby through an armorglass panel. He can trigger the servogun covering the lobby, and a firing port lets him shoot his FN HAR into the lobby. He can also trigger an alarm throughout the building unless his alarm control is overridden on the building computer.
**Basilisk Lair (3)** — An office converted to a lair for the basilisk, which is present with its handler. It can easily hear any weapons fire in Areas 1 or 2 and will move to cover Area 4.
**Inner Lobby (4)** — Leads to the elevators. Guarded by a security camera and two servoguns.
**Loading Dock (5)** — The cockatrice and its handler guard this area, also covered by a security camera and a servogun. Two Chrysler-Nissan Patrol-1s are parked here, modified as described in **Knock, Knock**, p. 57. Stacked against the east wall are several packing cases marked "Office Supplies" — they contain a pair of missile launchers and 8 AVMs.
**Rear Entrance (6)** — Two agents guard this entrance. The corridor is also covered by a security camera and a servogun.

## FLOORS 2–27
Empty. Perhaps some dusty office furniture, minimal lighting, no permanent occupation. Security cameras still cover every floor. These floors are the search ground when the Knights sweep downward from the 28th.

### Elevator Core (7) — The 11th Floor Trap
Unless the action is blocked on the computer, the car will freeze at the 11th floor if the shadowrunners take the elevator. After three turns the cables cut loose, the brakes do not engage, and everyone inside must resist **4D2** damage when the car plows into the bottom of the shaft.

Getting out — blow or force the doors, or smash loose the ceiling access hatch:
- Elevator doors are normal construction plastic (Barrier Rating 4). Forcing them open: Unresisted Strength Test, **TN 8** (6 with an appropriate tool). One success opens them enough for one person at a time; three or more successes force them wide open, enough for any number of people to pass through in a turn.
- Smashing the ceiling hatch: Unresisted Strength Test, **TN 4**. Clambering through it: Unresisted Quickness Test, **TN 5**. On a failure, the person who blew the hatch is stuck in it until his next action. Someone can push or pull him through with an Unresisted Strength Test, **TN 4**, at the cost of his own action. Anyone through the hatch can grab a handhold and hang on safely when the car goes, then easily climb out the 11th-floor elevator door.

If a decker is controlling the building systems, he can pull this same stunt on any bad guys who take the elevator. The agents will use the elevator to move around the building unless they suspect their system has been invaded — once they stop trusting it, they take the stairs.

## 28TH FLOOR
The Knights' operational floor, and along with the ground floor the only actively manned level. Scale: 1 square = 5 meters.

**Elevator Core (1)** — Covered by two security camera / automatic servogun combinations and two agents.
**Kyle Morgan's Quarters (2)** — Luxurious furnishings and several photos of stunning women seated with Morgan in his sports car. ("Cripes, isn't that Beauty Shannon, the simsense star?") Also an old flat photo of a gangly, teenaged Morgan leaning against the side of a Dragon; removed from its frame, the back reads "Llawrgwynedd 2022" in faded ink.
A cyberterminal sits on a desk in one corner — this is the **Command Terminal (Node 4B)**. Each attempt to crack security on the terminal and gain access takes ten minutes base time. Entry requires an Unresisted Computer Skill Test, **TN 8**; divide base time by successes, minimum one minute. The terminal displays a menu of available options.
Morgan is here during the fighting, monitoring from the Command Terminal. If his access is locked out he moves to the Security Center (Area 11). If his forces are losing, he moves up onto the roof.
**Mixacopotec's Quarters (3)** — Spartan furnishings. A heavy wooden club set with obsidian spikes hangs on the wall. Mixacopotec will not be here — he leads a squad of agents into the thick of the fight.
**Lin Hwang's Quarters (4)** — Typical modern decor, with a cyberdeck on a table next to a cyberterminal. Lin is here, monitoring system performance, and jacks in if he detects a system invasion.
**Conference Room (5)** — Large oak table seating twelve, dominated by a very expensive trid unit with a large projection tank. Its memory holds photos of Maria and Hernandez, maps of their homes and offices, and charts showing the progress of Morgan's plans to date (mostly paths terminating in dead ends). There may be photos of at least some of the shadowrunners.
**Kitchen and Dining Area (6)** — Well-stocked with food and equipment: oven, microwave, refrigerator. A securely locked, very sturdy cabinet; smashing it open reveals an array of liquor bottles.
**Computer Room (7)** — The Knights' mainframe is installed here.
**Barracks Area (8)** — Converted to bunks and footlockers, military-style, with space for 16 persons.
**Gymnasium (9)** — Exercise machines, racks of weights, and a wrestling mat in the center of the room. If Maria was captured, she is here. See **The Long Fall**, p. 65.
**Armory (10)** — Heavily reinforced door. Opening its maglock requires a valid maglock cardkey (each of the Knights has one) or a maglock passkey plus an Unresisted Electronics Skill Test, **TN 8**. Contains a rack of six FN HAR assault rifles and four Ares Predators, several cases of assorted ammo, two missile launchers and several boxes of missile types against one wall, and a locked case of grenades, explosives, timers, fuzes, and detonators.
**Security Center (11)** — The **Security Terminal** is installed here. Manned by Blackstone and an agent; Morgan may be here if the Command Terminal has been locked out or compromised.
**Blackstone's Quarters (12)** — A typical technomancer's litter of components, equipment, and tools. One corner cleared as a living area. On a table by the bed is a holo of Blackstone, a pleasantly smiling Dwarf woman, and a small Dwarf child, maybe two years old.

## THE ROOF
**Helipad (1)** — Raised about five meters off the roof. Underneath is a cozy little den — Perianwyr's lair. It contains:
- the bones of several sides of beef, gnawed very clean
- a 100-liter vat, which proves to contain beer if anyone checks
- an expensive trid unit with a voice-activated control unit; the music library holds thousands of rock and roll recordings catalogued chronologically, from the early 1950s to the latest Maria Mercurial hit

Perianwyr will be here unless Kyle Morgan is under attack on the 28th floor. If the bad guys lose the battle indoors, Morgan and Peri will be up here waiting for the final face-off.
