# Taetzel Building — Integrated Reference (Current State, +6 Months)

## TELL IT TO THEM STRAIGHT
The Taetzel Building… Almost 30 years old, this was once a mid-size office block that is now a 28-story derelict in the middle of a slum. Built in the Festung style popular in the late 2020s, it has solid, concrete walls up 20 stories or so, with mirrored armor-glass windows on the top floors where the bosses hung out.



## STATUS SUMMARY
28 stories plus a sub floor. The Dragon Knights' raid happened roughly six months ago. The building is derelict and largely stripped, on emergency power only, with a squatter bike shop operating off the emergency circuit on the first floor. Everything above the first floor except the 28th is open, empty floor plate. The 28th is sealed and no known key exists.

## SHELL & STRUCTURE
- **Exterior walls, floors 1–20:** Barrier 32. Massive; takes vehicle-scale weapons easily. Structural as well as opaque.
- **Exterior walls, floors 21–28:** Heavy Ballistic glass laminate, mirrored finish on the outside. The glazing runs continuous from the 21st through the 28th floor; isolation spacers close up the outer lip.
- **Columns:** Heavy steel-jacketed concrete, widely spaced, on every floor.
- **Floors:** Polished ground concrete, acid etched to look nice, no carpet or rugs. Floor boxes holding data and power cable are scattered liberally throughout the open floors.
- **Doors:** Security-grade doors Barrier 8. Interior office doors Barrier 4.
- **Ceilings:** Drop ceilings on the upper floors, with a foot-thick insulated floor assembly above.
- *Adventure-era text gave the lower shell as Reinforced Concrete Barrier 32*

## VERTICAL CIRCULATION — CENTRAL SPINE
The core spine carries bathrooms and elevators, with a large stairwell on each side of it:
- **2 large cargo elevators**
- **4 smaller personnel elevators**
- **2 small executive elevators** servicing along the north/back side
- Bathrooms on every floor, spine-adjacent. All of them have had the toilet paper stolen.
- **Sub floor access** is via the stairs and the cargo elevators.

Elevators currently have **no power**. Adventure-era note: the elevator machinery sits in the housing on top of the roof elevator core, which is the physical kill-point for elevator power.

## POWER & BUILDING SYSTEMS (CURRENT)
- Emergency lights in each room have power. Nothing else does.
- The bike shop on the first floor is tapped into the emergency circuit but needs more than it provides, so they run a generator and haul a few loose panels in and out when they open the doors.
- The first floor is stripped of operating items — no furniture, no computers — but the **maglocks and security system still exist, in a safety override condition**.
- All door maglocks are **flashing red and open outwards only**.
- The **security booth is wedged open with a brick**, all its displays dark.
- **External cameras** show no indicator light.
- **Fire alarm is tripped and silenced.** The triggering fire operator reads: *ROOFTOP FIRE — TEMP OVER / SENSOR ERROR.* Singer powered up the emergency fire access at the security panel and reset the open/clear condition to find it.
- Matrix host structure and system state: *(pending GM — the adventure-era A/B two-mainframe arrangement is not assumed to survive.)*
- Building security is on a CCSS system except 28th floor

## FIRST FLOOR (DOUBLE HEIGHT)
Well divided, unlike everything above it.
- **North side:** two large bays with roll-up doors.
- **South side:** the parking lots and the front door — a **Barrier 18 armored door and security trap**.
- **Rooms:** maintenance, nurse's office, visitor conference room, security entry, main front gallery entrance, two large receiving rooms, a center warehouse area accessing the cargo elevators, bathrooms as above. A few offices and store rooms scattered among them.
- The **personnel elevators face the opposite direction**, into the area behind security.
- Adventure-era ground floor (lobby with camera/servogun, guard booth with armorglass panel and firing port, basilisk lair, inner lobby with two servoguns, loading dock with the cockatrice and the Patrol-1s, rear entrance) describes the floor **as it was during the raid**. Present disposition of the servoguns and any of that hardware: mountings remain, guns gone

## FLOORS 2–20
Barren, dusty, completely open except for the widely spaced columns. **Completely dark** — the outer barrier wall is structural and opaque at these levels. Same ground and etched concrete, including the walls, with dense metal strips giving the surface a pattern. Some fallen ceiling tiles, dirt, and scattered debris.

**Electrical floors — 6, 9, 12, 15, 18:** massive electrical banks and heavy 600-volt busses for machinery.

## FLOORS 21–26
Generally identical to one another: open plate, columns, mirrored ballistic glass curtain wall, polished etched concrete, floor boxes, high drop ceiling. Daylight reaches these floors where 2–20 have none.

**23rd floor:** a rare 15x15 room, locked with a big ABUS mechanical padlock and visibly beaten on. It holds a large cache of toilet paper and other paper products.

**26th floor (as detailed):** completely open except for the columns. Drop ceiling gives about 12 feet of clear space, with 2 more feet above it and the foot-thick insulated floor segment over that. A few ceiling panels have fallen or been left stacked. Pallets are piled next to the cargo elevator.

## 27TH FLOOR
Very similar to the 26th, with a nicer drop ceiling and more sound proofing on its ceiling.

## 28TH FLOOR
**Sealed.** No key is possessed by anyone known — entry requires defeating or deconstructing the security. Assume all data was purged, either on dead-man timers or by the team on the way out.

**Post-raid disposition:** Mixacopotec and Blackstone both left under their own power and took what they could carry. Lin Hwang died; his cyberdeck and cyberterminal were stripped by whoever reached them first, but the rest of his room was left alone. Someone pulled the heavy weapons and all the explosives out of the armory. **Morgan's quarters were never recovered.**

**Lifestyle structure:** the floor ran as a communal arrangement. Morgan held a two-person High suite and Lin Hwang a single High; Mixacopotec and Blackstone were Middle; the agents lived at Low density in the barracks while drawing on Middle-grade communal amenities. The kitchen, conference room, gymnasium, and spine bathrooms are shared space and support everyone's lifestyle rather than belonging to any one room.

**(1) Elevator Core** — Arrival lobby for the four personnel cars and the two cargo cars; the two executive elevators land separately on the north side. Covered by two security camera / automatic servogun combinations and posted with two agents during the raid. Car doors are Barrier 4 like every elevator door in the building. With the elevators dead, anything coming onto this floor arrives through a stairwell door or up a shaft. Servoguns: Firearms 6 (Short/Medium only), 4M3, Initiative 20 no roll, Ballistic 3 / Impact 0, Body 4, +2 partial cover from their housings.

**(2) Kyle Morgan's Quarters — High, 2 person. Not recovered.** Luxurious furnishings, intact and undisturbed. Several photos of stunning women seated with Morgan in his sports car ("Cripes, isn't that Beauty Shannon, the simsense star?"). An old flat photo of a gangly, teenaged Morgan leaning against the side of a Dragon; pulled from its frame, the back reads **"Llawrgwynedd 2022"** in faded ink. High-lifestyle fittings: real-wood furniture, an en-suite bath, a full wardrobe of good clothing, an entertainment wall, a wet bar, and the service tech that replaces a housekeeper.
A cyberterminal sits on a desk in one corner — the **Command Terminal (Node 4B)**. Cracking it takes ten minutes base time per attempt; entry is an Unresisted Computer Skill Test, **TN 8**, base time divided by successes, minimum one minute. The terminal displays a menu of available options. Because nobody has been in here, this is the single richest intact site on the floor.

**(3) Mixacopotec's Quarters — Middle. Fled after grabbing possessions.** Furnished in spartan fashion and now emptier still. The bed frame, footlocker, and Middle-grade fittings remain; personal effects are gone. The heavy wooden club set with obsidian spikes hung on the wall here.

**(4) Lin Hwang's Quarters — High. Killed in the raid; deck and terminal stolen.** Typical modern decor, otherwise left as it was. The table that held the cyberdeck and cyberterminal is bare except for cable tails and a power tap. Everything not worth carrying is still here — clothing, personal electronics, tool kit, spare optical cable, chips. With the Command Terminal locked behind Morgan's door and the mainframe wiped, Lin's leftover personal effects are the most accessible read on who the Knights actually were.

**(5) Conference Room** — Large oak table with room to seat twelve, dominated by a very expensive trid unit with a large projection tank mounted in the center of the table. Its memory held photos of Maria and Hernandez, maps of their homes and offices, charts of Morgan's plans to date, and possibly photos of some of the shadowrunners — **all of it purged.** The unit itself is still in place and still worth money.

**(6) Kitchen and Dining — communal.** Well-stocked in its day: oven, microwave, refrigerator, autocook with a full suite of flavor faucets. Six months without power means everything perishable is long spoiled. The securely locked, very sturdy cabinet is still locked; smashing it open reveals an array of liquor bottles, which keep fine.

**(7) Computer Room** — The Knights' mainframe is installed here. **Red-7 host, subsystem ratings 10/11/9/9/9.** Data was erased or stolen and purged; the iron is still racked and running on nothing. Raised floor, cable trays, dedicated cooling, and the fiber run up the spine to the satellite-link dish in the roof comms shack that fed its SAN.

**(8) Barracks — Low space / Middle remaining.** Converted to bunks and footlockers, military-style, with space for 16 persons. Cramped by design but supported by the floor's Middle-grade communal facilities — shared latrine and showers off the central spine, laundry, and the kitchen.

**(9) Gymnasium** — Several exercise machines, racks of weights, and a wrestling mat in the center of the room. This is where Maria was held when the Knights had her, and where Morgan was about to apply BTL chips and drugs.

**(10) Armory — looted for heavy weapons; no explosives remain.** Heavily reinforced door. Opening the maglock requires a valid maglock cardkey (each of the Knights carried one) or a maglock passkey plus an Unresisted Electronics Skill Test, **TN 8**.
Still present — standard agent issue, stocked in threes to sixes:
- 6 **FN HAR** assault rifles on the rack — 5M3, Ammo 20 (clip), integral laser sight (−1 TN to hit), gas-vent recoil compensation (Rating 2)
- 4 **Ares Predators**
- 5 **Fichetti Security 500** pistols — 3M2, Ammo 10 (clip)
- 5 sets of **Partial Heavy Armor with Helmet** (Ballistic 7 / Impact 5), black and gray "urban camo" pattern, no identification or rank markings
- 6 pairs of **low-light goggles**
- Several cases of assorted ammunition and loose spare clips for both weapon types
Gone: both missile launchers and every Anti-Vehicle Missile (6D4 vs. vehicles, 12D8 vs. personnel). The locked case that held grenades, explosives, timers, fuzes, and detonators has been forced and emptied.

**(11) Security Center** — The **Security Terminal** is installed here. Camera wall, alarm control, maglock control, and servogun control for the floor; this is also the node that could reach the building's slave modules through the interface module. Manned during the raid by Blackstone and an agent, with Morgan falling back here if the Command Terminal was locked out. For anyone trying to open the sealed floor from the inside out, this is the lever.

**(12) Blackstone's Quarters — Middle. Fled with simple possessions.** A typical technomancer's litter of components, equipment, tools, and parts — bulky, low-value, and left behind. One corner had been cleared out as a living area, with a table by the bed that held a holo of Blackstone, a pleasantly smiling Dwarf woman, and a small Dwarf child, maybe two years old.

**Spine facilities** — Bathrooms in the central spine as on every floor, plus the barracks latrine and showers. Toilet paper gone here too.

**Open for GM:** whether Mixacopotec's club and Blackstone's family holo were carried out or left; whether the barracks footlockers were emptied; whether the servoguns and cameras on this floor still hold power and ammunition behind the seal.

## BEHIND THE SCENES
This is it. The shadowrunners have to hit Morgan, the Dragon Knights, and their goons here or the bad guys will keep on picking at them until they go under.

Besides these forces, Perianwyr has provided his friend with some typically draconian resources. Besides men and machines, Morgan's base is guarded by two griffins, a cockatrice, and a basilisk!

Each of these creatures was trained by Perianwyr. Each has only *one* handler among the agents. If that handler is killed or incapacitated, the creature goes wild and attacks the nearest target, whether a shadowrunner or a bad guy. The creature will continue to hunt ferociously, stalking through the building seeking prey. Only Perianwyr or Kyle Morgan can take control of a creature whose handler has been killed. Note that Perianwyr can control the cockatrice or basilisk from Astral Space, as these creatures are always actively present on the astral plane. The griffins, however, are not. This is convenient for the Dragon, who cannot fit inside the building physically unless he starts knocking over walls.

## DEBUGGING
The shadowrunners will have the advantage of surprise until they lose it. Morgan does not really expect them to attempt to take his base. Unless their decker is blocking the security cameras, as soon as they walk into an area monitored by the cameras, the bad guys will know they are there. Any guard can try to trigger an alarm, but the decker can block that, too. All the guards carry portable phones, which cannot be blocked by the computer. Therefore, the gamemaster must use his judgement. The shadowrunners have to take out the guards before they can make a call for help on the phone.

Once surprise has been lost, the guards' actions will depend on whether the Knights can track the shadowrunners on the security cameras. If they can, they will maneuver their agents in two equal groups, trying to surround them. Mixacopotec leads one of these groups.

If they cannot track the runners, they will divide the remaining agents into four equal groups. One will be stationed on the 28th floor. A second is placed on the ground floor. The other two start to search the building, working down from the 28th floor. One group descends via the central stairs, the other via the back stairs. The elevators will be disabled, either through the computer or by killing the power to the elevator on the roof. All groups of agents will be in constant communication via portable phones. Mixacopotec will be leading the group down the central stairs.

If the runners break the agents, the remaining Knights on the 28th floor will get weapons from the armory and try to shoot their way out. Note that Blackstone will NOT fight. Moreover, he will actively use the building security system to help the shadowrunners if they reveal the fate of his family to him. Kyle Morgan will join Perianwyr on the roof.

Whether the shadowrunners corner Morgan on the roof or on the 28th floor, their showdown with the chief of their enemies is dealt with separately in **The Long Fall**, p. 65. # Matrix Overview — Taetzel Building / Dragon Knights

## The Decking Angle
Several paths can lead the team into the Dragon Knights' mainframe. This system is interconnected with the Taetzel Building's own computer system. In fact, a decker who jacks in to scope out the building system may even find himself ghosting into the Knights' computer.

A decker can slash into the system, grab what he can, and run. Heck, if the shadowrunners get the unlock code from Node 3B, they can pull the information out of Maria's head and go public with it. If that happens, the Dragon Knights will be out of town within three hours, and there will be an unexplained fire at the Taetzel Building that removes all evidence of their stay.

Alternatively, the decker may try to hit the system and suppress the building's security systems while the rest of the team hits the site physically.

In the event that an external alert is triggered, Lin Hwang will jack in to deal with it. There are numerous possibilities if the system is decked, because it holds the data that define the entire situation, and in 2050, data are power.

## Two Systems in One Building
This is two systems in one. The Taetzel Building's normal control mainframe is **System A**. The Knights' own mainframe is **System B**. Nodes are marked as A or B on the map and in the key.

- An Alert on one system will not trigger Alert status on the other.
- Shutting down one system leaves the other unaffected.
- System operations are local to the system the decker is in — the CPU in the Taetzel Building system cannot give orders to the Knights' system and vice versa.
- **The one exception:** the Security Terminal on the Knights' system (Node 8B) *can* interact fully with the Slave Modules controlling building services and systems (Node 3A). Special software running on an interface module (Node 7B) makes this possible.

## What That Means Physically
System A's slave modules are the building's hardware — the physical gear the runners will run into on the floor:

- **Security cameras** on monitored areas. If the decker isn't blocking them, the Knights see the team the moment they walk into frame; if he is, the Knights are reduced to four-group sweep tactics.
- **Alarms** that any guard can try to trip, and which the decker can suppress.
- **Elevators**, which the Knights themselves will disable — either through the computer or by cutting the elevator power on the roof.
- **Lighting**, minimal on floors 2–27, and other building services (doors, locks, environmental) running off the same slave modules.

What the decker cannot touch is the guards' **portable phones**. Comms are entirely off-system, so suppressing the computer never stops a guard from calling for help — the team has to take guards out physically before they dial.

The 8B → 7B → 3A link is the reason Blackstone can turn the building against its owners: sitting at the Knights' security terminal, he has full command of the building's physical systems and can use them to help the runners if they tell him what happened to his family.
