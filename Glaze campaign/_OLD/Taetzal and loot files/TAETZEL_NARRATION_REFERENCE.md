# Taetzel Building — Narration Reference

Use this during live play. It is deliberately simpler than `TAETZEL_BUILDING_CURRENT_STATE4.md` and contains only what is useful for describing the place and preserving current-state continuity.

# Core impression

Taetzel is a 28-story, roughly 70m x 70m Festung-style office/industrial tower in the Redmond Barrens. It is almost thirty years old, massively overbuilt, mostly stripped, dirty and underpowered rather than structurally ruined.

The lower twenty floors are a dark armored block. The upper eight floors change abruptly into mirrored ballistic glass. From outside it reads as a fortress whose executives once wanted daylight only after they were high above the street.

Blue Mountain Holdings now owns it. Singer and Glaze own BMH, but Glaze does not know she is a shareholder or that her fake identity holds that interest.

# Outside / grounds

South side:
- centered main entrance;
- east and west parking lots against the perimeter fence;
- about 44 spaces total;
- old oaks, overgrown bushes, tall grass and weed-choked edges;
- heavy hedge and Barrier 18 perimeter fence;
- fortified-looking gates, spring-up bollards and wedge-ramp defenses.

North side:
- two large roll-up/loading bays;
- truck/service access.

The grounds should feel neglected but defensible, not collapsed. Do not invent extra outbuildings, tunnels or lot geometry without GM input.

# First floor

Nine-meter double-height floor and much more divided than the tower above it. It contains the main/security entrance areas, maintenance, nurse's office, visitor conference room, receiving rooms, warehouse/cargo-elevator zone, bathrooms and scattered offices/storage.

Most furniture/computers were stripped. Security hardware and maglocks remain. Old first-floor gun mountings remain but the weapons are gone.

Riku's motorcycle shop occupies part of the first floor and is now allowed to stay. It gives the building normal lights, people, bikes and traffic instead of leaving the whole tower visibly abandoned.

Singer brought the Westwind inside through a roll-up bay and said the other bay will be his garage.

# Vertical core

Central spine:
- 2 cargo elevators;
- 4 personnel elevators;
- 2 smaller executive elevators on the north/back side;
- large stairwell on each side;
- bathrooms near the core on every floor.

All elevators run ground-to-roof; cargo elevators also reach the subfloor.

Power is still limited. Singer has managed one elevator and locks as needed. Most solar breakers reset; a couple remain faulted. Singer roughly estimates 350–1,600 kW depending on restored systems and a basement battery capable of about a week of ordinary January loads; treat that as his estimate, not a completed engineering survey.

# Floors 2–20

Huge, barren, dark open plates behind opaque Barrier 32 exterior walls. Widely spaced heavy steel-jacketed concrete columns, polished acid-etched concrete, metal strip patterns, scattered debris and floor service boxes.

The 6th, 9th, 12th, 15th and 18th floors contain massive electrical banks and 600V machinery busses.

Narrative feel: industrial cathedral without windows—very large, very dark, very empty.

# Floors 21–26

Open upper plates behind continuous mirrored ballistic glass. Daylight makes them feel radically different from the lower tower.

Same heavy columns and polished etched concrete, lots of power/data floor boxes, high drop ceilings and little else.

23rd floor exception: a rare locked 15 x 15 room with a battered ABUS padlock and an absurdly large cache of toilet paper/paper goods.

26th has about twelve feet clear below the drop ceiling, another two feet above, then the thick insulated floor assembly. Some ceiling panels are fallen/stacked and pallets sit near the cargo elevator.

# 27th floor

Nearly the same as 26th, but with a nicer and more heavily soundproofed ceiling.

Singer and Glaze have discussed eventually opening 26/27 into a larger volume for Glaze's full form. That is only a future structural idea.

# 28th floor — current scene

This was the Dragon Knights' living/operational floor and remained sealed for six months.

Singer has now defeated the stair-access maglock and entered. He immediately signaled Glaze to hold and cleared the floor at extraordinary speed with weapon, UV light, Physical Mask and breath control, searching for automation, chemicals, mines and hidden threats.

After a few tens of seconds he stopped, sat on the floor and broke into severe sobbing/wailing. Glaze is nearby, initially not touching him.

**Start the next session here. Do not skip the grief beat or begin with loot accounting.**

Once play resumes, the floor can contain the established old Dragon Knights spaces: elevator/security core, Morgan's quarters, Mixacopotec's quarters, Lin Hwang's quarters, conference room, kitchen/dining, computer room, barracks, gym, armory, security center and Blackstone's quarters. Detailed contents/mechanics are in the full building file. Do not invent extra traps or surviving data.

# Roof

The roof has:
- raised 30m x 30m helipad, about five meters up;
- several large satellite dishes;
- offset radio tower;
- HVAC/cooling towers;
- elevator house/machinery;
- solar panels;
- perimeter structure/fence.

Under the helipad was Perianwyr's old den.

Six-month-old fight evidence remains obvious once noticed:
- anti-vehicle missile fragment scars on the elevator house;
- dark finger-length dragon scales;
- a blood splotch;
- bullet pocks;
- large scuffs under the helipad;
- faint Perianwyr scent that Singer can place because of the blood/scales.

No astral background count remains from the fight. Glaze gathered only loose scales.

Singer's remembered fight is **not** this fight. In his history he cut Perianwyr badly with a polearm; here Perianwyr took an anti-vehicle missile hit.

# Subfloor / real environmental problem

The subfloor contains the major contamination: a roughly **6,000 m3 tank of polluted water with about 2,500 kg of 1,4-dioxane**.

The stale area carries a faint sweet chemical smell from secondary products. Singer got himself and Glaze out quickly and turned on a small vent fan.

Future cleanup needs PPE, water handling, a segregation/separation tank and repeated magical treatment. An upper tank of roughly 4,000 gallons may be empty; Tomas can probably arrange a similar water delivery, and Singer has discussed a later ~9 m3 stainless segregation tank. Singer expects a long job even if he can do it faster than in his remembered history.

Do not turn the basement into a dungeon full of extra secrets unless the GM adds them.

# Current security / power feel

The building is not "awake" yet.

Singer has:
- restored emergency fire access;
- powered one elevator/locks when needed;
- configured passcodes at ground security;
- keyed the security room to his palm-datajack information;
- given Glaze a passcode/key on her handset;
- reset most solar breakers.

The main building has CCSS infrastructure and may eventually be riggable. The 28th-floor system is separate from ground control. Current host/software state is not established enough to improvise detailed Matrix behavior.

# Hearth spirit

Taetzel has a Force 9 hearth spirit that manifested for Singer as a powerful janitor in a jumpsuit with an appropriate cleaning tool.

Singer asked it to work there again and offered a bounded bargain: for as long as he can, not a permanent promise. The spirit accepted and grounded back into its domain.

Use it as an autonomous spirit of the place, not a mascot or servant.

# Neighborhood / occupation tone

Singer's intended security model is not "empty fortress." Riku's shop, ordinary traffic, predictable neighbors and people knowing who belongs are useful security in themselves.

Singer told Glaze eventually the whole neighborhood is security and that she can talk with neighbors. This is social integration, not an established plan for mass magical coercion.

# Singer's remembered Taetzel — emotional comparison only

In Singer's parallel history the building became a mixed home/business stronghold with clinics, cyberware surgery, shops, publishing/printing, rented security operations and weapon businesses. His late wife used the parking lot for major gang diplomacy/training arrangements. Their family/team fought through a hellish week to reach the place.

**None of those events automatically happened here.**

Taetzel is familiar enough to trigger memory but different enough to hurt. That distinction is central to narration.

# Narration guardrails

- Describe scale, emptiness, light/dark, industrial weight, dust and overbuilt security more than decorative detail.
- Keep current facts separate from Singer's parallel history.
- Do not give Glaze knowledge from GM/adventure notes she has not observed or been told.
- Do not invent exterior geometry beyond established grounds.
- Do not invent new basement contents.
- Do not assume old automated security works merely because mountings/hardware exist.
- Do not turn property maintenance into the plot unless it creates a real decision, risk, cost or discovery.
- Current emotional priority is Singer's breakdown on the 28th floor.
