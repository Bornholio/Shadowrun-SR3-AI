# AZTECHNOLOGY CONFLICT

Campaign continuity / GM reference.

This document isolates the Reynaldo Texamachach / Aztechnology / Taetzel conflict so it does not overload the general campaign header. It includes both **played facts** and a clearly separated **GM-only future/behind-scenes layer**. Do not give player characters knowledge from the GM-only layer until it becomes available in play.

# BACKGROUND — REYNALDO / PERFEKTO / MERCURIAL

Reynaldo Texamachach was an Aztechnology senior executive and prior holder/controller of **Perfekto Polymers** among other interests.

The events of *Mercurial* unfolded as established in the campaign/adventure history.

Reynaldo survived those events despite the public/operational assumption that he was dead. He spent significant time in a medical tank and his body was repaired/reconstructed with cybernetics. Aztechnology removed him from his senior position and retired him.

Later, Reynaldo took command of a small military unit in an Aztechnology corporate hold in Santa Fe. The unit was moved to a Puyallup landing field and then used to attack Taetzel.

This is not a small corporate mistake. The June 1 assault is a new multimillion-nuyen failure piled onto an older roughly **70 million ¥** loss/history tied to Mercurial/Perfekto. Aztechnology therefore has actionable institutional intelligence about Taetzel, Reynaldo, Perfekto, the Dragon Knights/Morgan connection, and the old incident. Do not portray the corporation as if it has forgotten its own property/history.

# ATTACK — THURSDAY JUNE 1, 2051

## Initial approach

During a firearms lesson in Taetzel's front lot, Singer hears four military turbine vehicles approaching low and fast with vectored thrust kicking up dust.

Singer orders Riku inside and locked down, orders Glaze to lock down in the protected upper floors, identifies the turbines as military, arms himself, and becomes invisible.

Force:
- Aztechnology Aguilar attack helicopter ×1
- Lobo APC/LAV ×1
- Lockheed Kestrel drones ×2
- Lobo assault personnel
- Aguilar pilot/copilot

The Aguilar sweeps past low and juking and enters an orbit around the building. Singer feels a wave of electronic warfare sweep the area. The Lobo executes a landing loop while both Kestrels synchronize their landing/approach. Singer's Tactical Computer computes roughly **15% odds and descending**.

## GM-resolved combat timeline

**0.6 seconds** — as the Lobo door opens and the APC settles, Singer uses the glowing white blade on his staff to hurl the nearest Kestrel through the opening into the vehicle interior.

**1.2 seconds** — Singer tears the pilot hatch off the Lobo, seriously injuring the pilot. Screams and gunfire erupt. A trooper in heavy security armor with obvious speed enhancement drives into the wrecked drone blocking the door.

**1.8 seconds** — Singer jacks into the vehicle using the pilot's cable. A heavy anti-tank guided missile strikes the Aguilar, vaporizing/destroying its tail, inflicting Serious damage and forcing it into a crash. The wired trooper continues battering through the drone wreck; the second Kestrel rises.

**2.4 seconds** — Singer slams the fast armored trooper back into the opening, knocking both him and the drone rigger behind him unconscious. Two fast special-forces troops fire heavily at Singer. A man deeper in the vehicle screams **"Forward!"** in Spanish.

**3.0 seconds** — Singer vaults up and slams the second Kestrel into the pavement, landing on the Lobo hull. The two SF troops roll out and continue firing as Singer drops/pivots.

**3.6 seconds** — Singer fires two gel rounds from his revolver, each round catching one SF trooper high in the face/head. Both are thrown prone and knocked unconscious.

**4.2 seconds** — Singer rolls into the entry. A specialist behind the leader screams over the radio: **"¡Aborten! ¡Aborten, aborten! ¡Se acabó la misión!"** The standing leader fires twice over Singer's shoulder as Singer grabs him by the throat. Over the next few seconds the man's windpipe is crushed and he goes limp. The specialist raises his hands and says **"¡Me rindo!"**

Singer drops the leader, pulls the Lobo gunner out, and orders the surrendered specialist out.

Two military jets pass overhead at about Mach 1.2 and divert south; windows across the area rattle/slam from the pressure wave. No additional allegiance/action has been established for those jets beyond the GM injection.

Singer finds restraint bands in the Lobo and cuffs the personnel except the surrendered specialist. He removes the wall-mounted medical kit and orders the specialist to perform first aid.

The Lobo tactical display is described by the GM as showing only **five vehicles and the team here** on its roster. This conflicts numerically with the four initially identified assault vehicles (Aguilar, Lobo, two Kestrels). The fifth roster vehicle was not identified in play; preserve the discrepancy rather than inventing it. No force beyond that roster is established from the display.

Singer warns the specialist to remain.

Singer runs to the damaged Aguilar. The pilot and copilot attempt to get airborne rather than surrender. Singer slams/pivots the airframe so the rotor jams harder into the ground, slices the entry lock with the white-energy staff blade, opens the aircraft, and ends combat. Both crew are led back to the parking lot.

# REYNALDO IDENTIFIED / EXECUTED

Singer asks the surrendered specialist who the leader is.

Answer: **Reynaldo Texamachach.**

Singer responds:

**"How the fuck is he still alive?"**

Singer lifts Reynaldo and fires a round through his skull, killing him definitively, then drops the body.

Singer photographs Reynaldo's corpse and the defeated/landed Lobo with Reynaldo's own handset, attaches both images, and sends the following to the handset's **entire contact list**:

**DO YOU WANT WAR?**

The message originates electronically from Reynaldo's handset, but Singer is the actual sender.

Singer later describes the event as further proof of his rule: **"no body — not dead."**

# SURVIVORS / MEDICAL TREATMENT

Singer does not massacre the defeated personnel.

He tells the surrendered specialist **"Medical first aid"** and gives him the APC medical kit.

After the immediate fight, Singer casts **Treat** on wounded Aztechnology personnel, worst first. He speaks Latin while touching each patient for approximately 10–30 seconds.

Singer later covers Reynaldo's body with a tarp, checks the wounded, tells the survivors recovery is coming, and warns:

**"If you believe that the consequences for you may be lethal, feel free to run now."**

He then snaps off every restraint band, releasing them voluntarily.

They stay.

Singer tells them:
- working for Tex was a mistake but probably not a personal failure;
- Tex cost the company enormous money;
- following orders may not protect them;
- they should talk among themselves and get their story straight;
- Singer knew enough of Texamachach's history/failures before buying Taetzel that the personnel should not assume ignorance.

Communications are restored. Recovery is inbound.

# FIRST CORPORATE CONTACT — BALTASAR

Singer waits for a response to the mass handset message.

Caller: **Baltasar Ordóñez de Villaseñor — C-PR-America, Aztechnology.**

Baltasar has seen the photographs. He states:
- Reynaldo was not acting with authorization appropriate to the event;
- Aztechnology does not desire war;
- reasonable damages can be covered;
- the company wants to recover personnel/equipment without causing another incident.

Singer imposes recovery terms:
- two trucks;
- police/security personnel and mechanics only;
- two negotiators maximum;
- **no recordings**;
- no cleanup crews harassing/dealing violently with local squatters;
- negotiators will meet Singer in person.

Singer tells Baltasar the company is good enough to recover its equipment without killing squatters for existing.

Baltasar confirms Reynaldo survived *Mercurial*, spent substantial time in a medical tank, was repaired partly with cybernetics, removed from his position, and retired. He does not yet give Singer a reliable explanation for how Reynaldo obtained/deployed the military unit.

Singer hands Reynaldo's handset to the Aguilar pilot so Aztechnology can communicate directly with its own personnel.

# DANGEROUS EQUIPMENT REMOVED BEFORE RECOVERY

Singer strips only items that remain threats to immediate safety: electronic-warfare hardware, vehicle weapon/launcher systems and missiles, the heavy machine gun, and the assault personnel's firearms/ammunition/knives/limited grenades. Ordinary supplies are returned with the force.

The exact retained inventory is intentionally maintained separately from this conflict history so later equipment bookkeeping does not duplicate or distort the event record.

# RECOVERY ARRIVAL / FIRST NEGOTIATOR FAILURE

Aztechnology sends the agreed recovery vehicles and an initial pair of negotiators.

Because of the scale/history of the loss, the company has actionable intelligence rather than approaching blind. It can know Taetzel's Perfekto history, the old Mercurial/Dragon Knights association, Reynaldo's connection, Blue Mountain Holdings/current ownership records, and the active cover identity **James Taylor** to the degree those records are available internally.

Initial negotiators:
- **Rafael Ortega**
- **Chen** (first name not established)

Singer scans them before allowing a meeting.

Resolved findings:
- Ortega is mundane but has concealed implanted visual/audio recording capability with internal storage.
- Chen carries a deliberately concealed **active micro-recorder**.
- Chen is actually **Magic 6, Grade 3 initiate**, deliberately Masked. Singer fails to pierce the Masking and therefore does not know her true awakened status from the scan.

Singer dismisses them:

**"Walk away, send professionals, render instructions understood this time."**

They leave without negotiating.

# SECOND NEGOTIATOR PAIR

Second pair:
- **Mateo Serrano** — corporate legal / settlement authority
- **Dr. Elena Varga** — magical security / risk assessment

They leave ordinary electronics outside before approaching.

Singer performs a full cyberscan, astral scan, visual examination, and professional physical pat-down. GM grants a −3 TN for taking time and allows Singer's Police Procedures as a complementary skill.

Results:
- Serrano: clean of weapons/recording/transmission gear; no cyberware detected; mundane, Essence 6.
- Varga: clean of weapons/recording/transmission gear; no cyberware detected; **Magic 6, Grade 3 initiate**, using Masking; Singer successfully pierces the Masking.

Singer privately tells Glaze:

**"I'll bring them up, one woman, Varga, is masked."**

# NEGOTIATION LOCATION / COVER PRESENTATION

Singer escorts Serrano/Varga through Taetzel personally.

He presents a deliberately ordinary business rationale:
- old Perfekto Polymers property;
- bought from New Horizons as a loss-of-value purchase;
- possible filtration-processing test/business use based on technology Singer developed;
- possible land-bank use.

He physically authenticates elevator/floor access and introduces the visitors under his **James Taylor** cover. Glaze is introduced/called **Debbie**.

The meeting occurs in the 28th-floor front common room at a table. Singer provides coffee/water and then sits off to the side with his own coffee, outwardly appearing minimally interested while continuing hidden Matrix/intelligence work.

# INFORMATION CONTROL / PR BATTLE

Singer has agents collecting news/social/media material before Aztechnology PR can remove, alter, de-index, or bury it. They retain originals, edits, timestamps, reposts, and missing items.

Observed pattern:
- broad knowledge that a violent military incident happened in Redmond remains;
- aircraft/gunfire/explosion chatter survives;
- precise attribution, exact equipment, location linkage, Perfekto/Taetzel connection, and reconstructable detail are being aggressively reduced;
- the **location** is especially being erased/de-indexed from ordinary discovery;
- some low-quality/noisy speculation is left alone while more evidentiary material becomes harder to find.

Singer displays selected surviving material on the trideo using the physical remote so the corporate visitors do not see obvious mental/headware control.

He tells "Debbie" that **John in internal security** sent the feeds and says John reports the location is mostly being erased; Singer says he sent John their security feeds to compare.

Do not establish "John" as a real independent contact, Singer uses fake team members to cover up extensive activity and abilities when revealing information.

# GLAZE / DEBBIE SETTLEMENT TERMS

Singer tells Glaze before the meeting that he is too angry to be reasonable and may ask her to negotiate. He gives her genuine authority:

**"You can decide what you want out of them."**

Singer's broad preferred direction: instead of meaningless PR expenditure, Aztechnology should create something useful locally — shelter/food support, water infrastructure, a real medical clinic — and in return may tell whatever public story it wants if it performs and stays out of their business.

Glaze turns this into concrete terms.

The first weak framing from Aztechnology includes direct damage/disruption and a vague community-relations fund. Singer visibly begins to scowl; this is not an Intimidation Test, just clear room-shaping displeasure.

Glaze rejects token placation and sets the substantive demands:

1. Repair direct property/neighborhood damage caused by the assault.
2. Establish a real food/homeless shelter within useful walking distance.
3. Provide functional potable-water capacity: tanker truck, large stainless tanks, pumps, filtration, and useful infrastructure.
4. Establish a clinic capable of supporting **eight physicians** with actual diagnostic/treatment equipment.
5. Provide **five years** operating support for the clinic.
6. Aztechnology may brand/photograph the projects and construct whatever public story it needs.
7. If Aztechnology performs, Singer/Glaze will not publicly contradict the chosen story merely to embarrass the company.
8. If Aztechnology performs, no Corporate Court filing from this incident.
9. If Aztechnology performs, no deliberate public release of the household's preserved security evidence as a centerpiece.
10. Aztechnology stays out of the household's business: no retaliation, manufactured investigation, pressure through subsidiaries, or harassment of owners/tenants/employees/contractors/neighbors.
11. **No astral scouting/investigation** of the property, residents, or associated operations; no projecting personnel, spirits, astral reconnaissance, magical "security assessment" used to evade the term, or astral tracking from incident material.
12. Reciprocal restraint: absent another intrusion, Singer/Glaze have no interest in running an intelligence campaign against Aztechnology.
13. If Aztechnology complies, Singer will avoid investigating how/why Reynaldo survived the public understanding of Mercurial.

Singer explicitly says Aztechnology can know he can cast **Treat** and that can remain the limit of what it knows about the household's magic.

The second pair understand/summarize the settlement. At session close **performance remains pending**. Do not pretend the clinic, shelter, water system, or long-term payment is already complete.

# CONTROL MANIPULATION LIE / LOBO PILOT

Singer asks that the **Lobo pilot** be apologized to because it is embarrassing to shoot down one's own forces: the Lobo was used to fire the anti-tank missile that brought down the Aguilar.

Singer gives Aztechnology a deliberately false explanation. He claims **Control Manipulation magic** made the Lobo pilot attack his own side and says the pilot probably still believes Singer took the rig under control. Singer is lying to conceal the capabilities actually used during the vehicle takeover. **Singer does not possess Control Manipulation spells.**

The false magical explanation is intended to shape Aztechnology's internal debrief without revealing Singer's broader augmentation, Matrix, or vehicle-control capabilities. Do not treat the claimed Control Manipulation spell as a real Singer ability or as established truth.

# VARGA — PARTING MAGICAL HINT

**"Varga stay away from the blood magic, take the slow path, if you are wondering about today investigate channeling if you are a summoner. Books on the topic are a few years out, but the meta planes may have answers if you can't wait."**

Varga retains "Channeling" and "the slow path." This is a narrow hint, not a full disclosure of Singer's techniques or future sourcebooks.

After Aztechnology personnel/equipment are fully gone, Singer closes Taetzel's gates and brings all retained threat equipment into the building by cargo elevator.

Glaze sees the equipment come in and watches Singer secure it in the armory.

Singer sends Riku **2,000¥** and says:

**"Well that was exciting."**

**"Beer is on me for a while."**

# CURRENT PUBLIC / CORPORATE STATE

- Reynaldo Texamachach is definitively dead.
- Aztechnology survivors are recovered alive after Singer treated/released them.
- Aztechnology recovered its remaining vehicles/equipment except for the weapons/systems Singer retained for safety.
- Aztechnology's first negotiator attempt failed due to prohibited recording.
- The second negotiation produced a workable settlement framework.
- Aztechnology PR is actively suppressing specificity/attribution/location while allowing enough generic noise that the incident can be reframed instead of impossibly erased.
- Singer's agents preserve source material and removal patterns.
- No Corporate Court action has been filed at this point.
- No open war is currently desired by either side.
- Settlement performance and long-term noninterference remain to be proven.
