# REALITY / SINGER'S ORIGIN — CURRENT WORLD FACT

Singer comes from a genuine **2064 parallel history**, not ordinary time travel. GM truth: the campaign reality is a critically branched parallel world. The crossing is not intended as a solvable riddle, and Singer does not know the full explanation.

A major divergence Singer has already verified is that in his history **Schwartzkopf manifested at Furth im Wald / Schwarzkoppe Mountain on May 21, 2051 and proclaimed sovereignty**. Contemporary records in this reality show no such event. Singer therefore treats his memories as professional/personal knowledge and comparison material, not as guaranteed prophecy about this world's future.

Singer arrived beneath **Mt. Baker / Kulshan** after escaping a lethal attack in 2064, catastrophically injured, overloaded/blind, with only his packed military rucksack and debris from the medical tank around him. Singer Cleansed the hostile background count to zero and later helped open the cave toward the surface.

Taetzel has special weight for Singer because a different Taetzel existed in his parallel history as a family/home/business stronghold. That history does not transfer into this reality, but it explains why this building can produce unusually strong recognition, memory collision, and investment. His remembered Taetzel included clinics, shops, publishing/printing, rented security operations, weapon shops/armory, and a history of gang/corporate violence around his family. None of those old tenants, arrangements, gang settlements, or family events automatically exist here.

Singer remembers personally opening a roughly three-meter wound in Perianwyr with a polearm in his own history. That did not happen in this reality; the current Taetzel roof instead records a fight in which Perianwyr took anti-vehicle missile fire. Singer's memory of Perianwyr and the current world's evidence are therefore related only through parallel-history recognition, not a shared exact past.

# TAETZEL / REDMOND — SOCIAL POSITION AND NEIGHBORHOOD REPUTATION

This section is deliberately substantial. Taetzel's social position is not a flavor note; it changes how ordinary locals, gangs, contractors, snoops and businesses plausibly approach the property.

## Redmond local baseline

Taetzel sits in an inhabited **E-rated Redmond slum**, not an empty ruin field. The surrounding area is poor, institutionally neglected and heavily informal, but still full of residents, patched apartments, shops, stalls, workshops, salvage, reused corporate infrastructure, vehicles, deliveries, electronics, small businesses, gangs, families and people trying to make a living.

Routine Lone Star protection is effectively absent for many SINless residents. Local security is therefore produced through neighbors, gangs, businesses, reputation, private arrangements and the ability to make consequences believable. Normal municipal assumptions—especially dependable police presence and routine city trash service—should not overwrite the established E-zone reality.

This matters because reputation is functional infrastructure. People do not need a formal dossier to decide whether to test Taetzel, work there, sell to it, avoid it, gossip about it, or ask it for help.

## Before Singer/Glaze: "the dragon-fight building"

Taetzel was already locally notorious before BMH occupation. Six months earlier, street people saw unmarked vans and several dozen workers, then squads of toughs brutally clearing several surrounding blocks. The building then became the site of a major fight involving **a dragon and anti-vehicle missiles**. The roof still carries evidence.

The neighborhood does not need the names Perianwyr, Kyle Morgan or Dragon Knights. The stable local fact is simpler: **this is the building where people fought a dragon with missile launchers.** It is recent local history, not an old legend.

When Singer and Glaze moved in, they were therefore not anonymous newcomers taking over a generic empty office tower. They visibly reoccupied a place that locals already associated with extreme violence and dangerous owners.

## Neighborhood reputation — occupation through Aztechnology

During the first days of occupation, Singer and Glaze established a consistent local pattern: extremely capable, private and alert, but **not predatory**. They repaired and occupied Taetzel rather than claiming territory; allowed Riku's protected motorcycle shop to remain; detected snoops without casually killing them; hired and paid local workers for bounded jobs; protected workers from the chemical hazard; and interacted directly enough that Glaze was visibly an independent authority rather than a passive companion. Marcus Bell's group also tested the property over old debts and left with the message that the new occupants were fixing their own problems, not making a territorial play. By May 31 the practical local read was **dangerous, competent, predictable, pays what is promised, wants privacy, and does not look for excuses to hurt weaker people**.

The June 1 Aztechnology assault changed the **scale**, not the moral direction, of that reputation. Nearby people do not need the technical truth of the fight: a AAA megacorporation brought military force to Taetzel, failed to simply take the property, later recovered its people and equipment under controlled conditions, and negotiated while Singer and Glaze remained in possession. Rumor will distort methods and casualties, but the visible outcome is hard to miss: **Taetzel stood its ground against a AAA corporation and was still there afterward.**

That should push local behavior toward **serious respect, wariness and curiosity**, with fewer casual tests and more care around Taetzel's boundaries. The earlier non-predatory pattern matters more now because locals have greater reason to fear what Singer and Glaze *could* do while also having evidence that they do not automatically use that capacity against neighbors, workers, retreating snoops or helpless wounded. Aztechnology's information suppression may obscure public attribution, but it cannot erase neighborhood memory of what people saw and heard. Do not convert this into worship, fealty, a protection racket or endless challengers.

If Aztechnology actually funds/builds the negotiated shelter, water infrastructure, clinic, repairs and operating support, that is likely to create a second neighborhood shift.
