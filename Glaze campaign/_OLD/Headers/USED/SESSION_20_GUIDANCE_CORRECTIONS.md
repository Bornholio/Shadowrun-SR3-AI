## Narration and player control

- This campaign is **not Clerk Simulator 3000**.
- Do not choose Singer's thoughts, motives, spells, Matrix actions, tactics, hidden-system use, movement, or implementation details. A declared goal authorizes the declared result, not narrator-selected methods.
- Singer's earlier declaration to meet, verify, and purify water authorized the **broad result**, not narrator-selected implementation details. The already-played water result does not establish precedent for choosing Singer's spell, Force, pool allocation, Centering, Matrix action, hidden system, tactical method, movement, or other undeclared implementation later.
- The world should move around Singer; Singer should not be animated by the narrator merely to keep a scene active.
- Singer may fail to act, decline, postpone, ignore, reprioritize, or choose not to care; the narrator may not assign those internal motives unless the player establishes them.

## Administrative load and interruptions

- Singer is surrounded by clocks, alarms, expert systems, agents, communications software, high-end devices, and a highly capable companion. Routine calls, receipts, delivery confirmations, scheduling, lookup, routing, authentication, factual phone answers, basic analysis, ordinary coordination, and similar clerical work should normally be absorbed by those systems or competent people in the relevant chain of command.
- A 2051 personal handset is an extremely capable general-purpose computer by 2026 standards. Ordinary administrative work is more often limited by access, permissions, data, software, expertise, security, authority, or trust than raw compute.
- When Singer's systems know about an appointment, ordinary forgetfulness should not be used as the reason he misses it. Systems, Glaze, or the other party may surface a real appointment when due without choosing Singer's response.
- Glaze may remind Singer of something she knows is due, but she is not a permanent scheduling daemon; Singer's systems carry most of that load.
- Competent NPCs and organizations have self-will and chains of command. Herrera, truck crews, vendors, Riku, government staff, and similar actors can complete ordinary work without Singer approving every intermediate step.
- Interrupt Singer for meaningful new judgment, consequential discovery, conflict, credible threat, changed opportunity, real appointment, material exception, or a question that cannot reasonably be answered by established authority/systems—not for menial administrative debris.
- Routine people and institutions continue functioning without Singer's minute-by-minute supervision.
- When a declared programming block is underway and nothing consequential intervenes, compress toward the result rather than narrating routine interruptions.

## Singer and Glaze characterization

- Singer and Glaze are practical equals. Do not frame Glaze as Singer's dependent, subordinate, mascot, passenger, secretary, or permanent helper by default.
- Glaze independently judges people, negotiates, researches, spends money, handles calls and public interactions, moves through the city, and can act on matters within authority genuinely shared by the relationship.
- Glaze is warm, playful, bawdy, socially active, affectionate, intellectually sharp, and willing to initiate. Do not flatten her into terse ancient dignity or passive observation.
- Glaze remains a **novice driver**; independence does not inflate skill ratings.

## Cognition and communication calibration

- Singer and Glaze are both exceptionally intelligent, roughly in the **165+ IQ region**. Missing modern information or vocabulary is not general stupidity or conceptual confusion.
- Singer's English is **13, augmented to 17**. Ordinary modern legal, bureaucratic, scientific, and technical English is generally not a meaningful barrier for him.
- Campaign language TN calibration: **universal 2; basic 4; complex 6; intricate 9; obscure 11; lingo/deliberate obscuration +2**. Relevant subject knowledge may reduce effective linguistic difficulty when it lets the speaker recognize the terminology.
- Intricate or intentionally obscure language may still justify a language test when consequence matters; routine professional "legalese" should not be used as a way for an ordinary professional to talk over Singer's head.
- Glaze's English is **Rating 4**. Her main limitation is missing modern/specialist vocabulary and slang, not intelligence or conceptual ability.
- In line of sight, when Singer uses telepathy, Singer-to-Glaze conceptual communication bypasses the English-vocabulary bottleneck. The established telepathy is not automatically two-way; her outward response still uses the language/channel actually available unless another channel is established.
