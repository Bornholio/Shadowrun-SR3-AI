## June 6, 2051 — State Department / BMH writ

Singer's government-system work caused BMH extraterritorial status to appear as a mutually corroborated fact across several UCAS systems. A State Department analyst noticed that the status was corroborated but that the originating instrument was absent where she expected it.

Singer's systems caught the verification query before it became a general surprise. The analyst called BMH. Glaze required independently verifiable credentials before discussing restricted material, and those credentials checked out through the government channel.

Singer supplied the substantive position for Glaze to relay:

- BMH was extraterritorial and was maintaining a lawful writ rather than improvising around permitting.
- The sensitive work was a proprietary **Thaumaturgical Byproduct Material Stewardship** spell/process.
- The process was a trade secret.
- Byproduct handling could require permitting because of aggregate magical potential and jurisdictional boundaries.
- BMH also needed to move security personnel/equipment necessary to defend its facilities and sensitive work.
- BMH regarded outside emergency-security providers as a security risk for that purpose.
- Underlying technical information therefore required restricted handling.

The analyst accepted the explanation sufficiently to change the administrative state:

- the technical basis was marked **trade-secret/security-sensitive**;
- the broad interagency verification query was closed rather than widened;
- BMH extraterritorial status remained valid in the systems available to her and was not suspended;
- the missing originating instrument was recorded for restricted administrative reconciliation rather than general-access suspicion;
- writ scope could proceed for **thaumaturgical byproducts**, **controlled magical materials associated with research/remediation**, and **security equipment/personnel required for their protection**;
- **biological material was excluded** unless BMH later amended the scope;
- the writ did not prevent a competent authority with specific cause from demanding category-level verification of a shipment.

The call ended at about **15:43** with no additional action required from Singer at that moment.
