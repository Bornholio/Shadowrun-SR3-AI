# SESSION 25 — HISTORY SUMMARY

## Opening point

**Thursday, June 8, 2051 — approximately 22:23, Redmond.** Singer and Glaze have finished their Redmond night-market business and are leaving the construction-salvage rows together. The last significant new contact was **Celia**, an independent magical environmental consultant who knows them only as James and Debbie and offered to conduct an independent magical/environmental review of Taetzel before the August EPA review. No return to Taetzel has yet been played.

The campaign has moved well beyond simple survival and adaptation. Singer and Glaze have a defended home, identities, money, contacts and increasing local standing. The important pressures going forward are therefore the consequences of what they have learned and done: Singer's parallel-history discrepancies, Aztechnology, the Tacoma DNA/DOA operation, Alamos 20,000 penetration, dangerous dragon research, and the older Horror/Fourth-Age questions behind several of those discoveries.

---

## Singer's reality problem

Singer is genuinely from a **2064 parallel history**, not ordinary time travel. The campaign world is a critically branched reality, but Singer does not know that explanation and the crossing is not meant to become a solvable cosmological riddle. His technical, magical and professional knowledge remains useful; specific history, people, organizations and later outcomes can diverge.

The first hard discrepancy was **May 21, 2051**: Singer's 2064 records say Great Western Dragon **Schwartzkopf** manifested at Furth im Wald / Schwarzkoppe Mountain and proclaimed sovereignty. Dense 2051 records in this world show that event did not happen. Singer therefore treats remembered history as evidence and comparison rather than prophecy.

Taetzel has reinforced this problem. Singer knew another Taetzel in his own history as a family/home/business stronghold, but this world's chain of events is recognizably related while materially different. The mismatch can produce powerful autobiographical collision without making Singer's memories facts about this world.

## Aztechnology: contained conflict, not peace

On **June 1**, an unauthorized Aztechnology military force attacked Taetzel with an Aguilar attack helicopter, Lobo APC/LAV, two Kestrel drones and associated personnel. Singer and Glaze defeated the attack. **Reynaldo Texamachach** was identified and killed by Singer. Surviving corporate personnel were treated and released; most corporate equipment was recovered, while dangerous systems deliberately retained by Singer were secured at Taetzel.

Aztechnology shifted from military action to information control and negotiation rather than immediately escalating. It has worked to blur public attribution, location and reconstructable timelines around the attack. Singer's systems preserved a much better evidentiary record than the corporation wants publicly available, while the Redmond neighborhood independently remembers that a AAA corporation brought military force to Taetzel and failed to remove its occupants.

Glaze led the settlement. Its important substance is that Aztechnology owes direct repair/compensation plus meaningful neighborhood restitution: potable water infrastructure, a real shelter, an eight-physician-capable clinic with five years of operating support, and related implementation. In return for performance, Singer and Glaze agreed to restraint: no Corporate Court escalation over the incident, no deliberate public evidence dump, and no intelligence campaign against Aztechnology. Aztechnology in turn agreed to noninterference, including no retaliatory investigations, subsidiary harassment, astral scouting or related intelligence work aimed at Taetzel and its people.

This is strategic containment, not reconciliation. Singer's hostility toward Aztechnology is profound, while Aztechnology remains a AAA institution with military, intelligence, magical, legal and public-relations capacity. Neither side benefits from open war unless new facts change that calculation.

By June 6 the first settlement water installation was actually functioning and drawing heavy neighborhood use. This demonstrates partial performance rather than merely promises, but the wider settlement remains something whose continued performance matters.

A separate risk exists in BMH's government posture. Singer manipulated UCAS systems so **Blue Mountain Holdings' extraterritorial status** appeared mutually corroborated. A State Department analyst noticed that the expected originating instrument was missing. After verification and discussion, the matter was moved into restricted administrative reconciliation rather than broad suspicion, and BMH obtained a sensitive-material writ covering thaumaturgical byproducts, controlled magical research/remediation material, and necessary security equipment/personnel. The anomaly was contained, not erased from history.

## Tacoma DNA/DOA operation: the largest fresh threat complex

Overnight June 7–8, Singer and Glaze entered Aztechnology's **Beta** research facility through the Tacoma sewer system. Beta contained lethal para-biological hazards, dead or consumed staff, missing personnel and advanced research. They extracted **Dr. Perkins**, a surviving scientist altered by a recombinant **Metavirus IV** process into a Tiger-Sapiens-like form. His memory and instincts were damaged but meaningful identity, choice and self-control remained. He chose escape and the possibility of treatment rather than remaining a specimen.

At **Wharf 114 / S.S. Misha**, the expected Biogene contact had already been killed and replaced by **Alamos 20,000** personnel. Singer exposed the false contact. In the resulting fight Singer killed local Alamos principals **Claude Pierce, Terry Carey and Daniel Sinclair**; Glaze used Fear against rank-and-file members. Singer retained the three leaders' bodies and later used them as forensic misdirection.

Singer and Glaze reached **Wilhem Park**, where geneticist **Dr. Allan Bronston** had credible knowledge that Aztechnology had been abducting Orks and that **Dr. Carol Owens** was being held in the separate Alpha facility. Perkins remained hidden in Wilhem Park. Singer gave Bronston Metagene material and one sample vial, but imposed strict anonymity and separation between Wilhem Park, Biogene and the assault team.

Singer and Glaze then attacked **Alpha**. They subverted major magical security, including multiple Force 8 elementals, compromised the Orange-11 host, removed the physical host assembly, cleared most personnel nonlethally, freed Ork women and children, and rescued Carol Owens. The three dead Alamos leaders were planted inside as misdirection. After evacuation Singer and Glaze withdrew separately, and a controlled fire-elemental ignition collapsed Alpha.

The important consequence is that Aztechnology faced two damaged research sites, missing people and equipment, rescued captives and confusing forensic signals. However, the history establishes **no evidence chain identifying Singer, Glaze, Taetzel or BMH as responsible for Tacoma**. The June 1 Taetzel fight and the June 8 Tacoma disaster remained separate discovery chains.

The employer side is compromised as well. **Wendell Holmes** had betrayed the later Biogene handoff to Alamos. Singer and Glaze broke the safe-house ambush, left Holmes and six Alamos/Hands associates alive but restrained, and forced **Jason Walker** to confront the compromise directly. Walker received the contracted data and vial and completed payment. He was warned that Biogene appeared penetrated and that its host forensics needed review outside the normal operations chain.

Singer also extracted a substantial but incomplete communications mesh from Alamos devices. It links the Misha and safe-house networks and contains names, aliases, photographs, messages and relationship patterns. It does **not** prove that every connected relative, employer, lover, business or correspondent is an Alamos member. Singer specifically worries that Alamos may have sympathizers or penetration inside police, political or other institutions, but no particular official has been established as compromised.

This entire Tacoma complex is fertile threat material: Aztechnology may investigate the disaster, Biogene has internal compromise, Alamos lost important people and exposed part of its network, Perkins remains vulnerable and unusual, Bronston/Owens possess dangerous scientific knowledge, and Singer/Glaze depend heavily on maintaining separation between their real identities and the operation.

## Dragon research and corporate secrets

Singer penetrated a **Mitsuhama Fort Worth** host using memorized legitimate credentials and recovered five advanced-development files concerning dragon neural interfaces, external safety limiters and Eliohann-related hardware/software. The records are real evidence that Mitsuhama possesses detailed dragon-interface research in this world.

Singer's parallel-history memories say **Eliohann** was badly injured by a botched datajack operation, aided by **Coinspinner** and **David Childers**, and eventually freed by Singer and allies. The recovered 2051 evidence does not cleanly match that remembered history. Singer suspects Coinspinner and David may have been killed as corporate loose ends, but that is not established fact.

**GM-only fact:** Eliohann disappeared willingly into Mitsuhama. Singer and Glaze do not know this.

The stolen material became the technical foundation of the external dragon-'trode project. By June 7 **Sound Interface Works** had delivered working human-form and horned-form external meshes for Glaze, and the confidential **Project SHOEMAKER** business/development relationship had been established. Singer deliberately rejected dragon datajack experimentation as too dangerous and outside the project.

On June 8 Singer physically traveled through astral space to Denver and approached **Holly Brighton** at the Bright Center for Journalism. Under reciprocal confidentiality he described the non-invasive dragon interface and privacy requirements. Holly judged the technology genuinely significant and said she had enough to pass it personally to **Nadja Daviar**. The intended Daviar/Dunkelzahn introduction therefore exists as a live route, but no response from Daviar or Dunkelzahn has yet been established in the history.

## Dr. Miriam Okafor and reproductive/genetic research

**Dr. Miriam Okafor** has become a genuine confidential research collaborator rather than a casual consultant. She knows Debbie is a female Western Great Dragon whose human, hybrid and full-dragon bodies are transformations of one individual. She also knows unusually sensitive biological history about Singer, including his deep genetic modification and prior fertility history.

The research is deliberately preserving unexplained/non-coding sequence and treating transformation state, tissue type, expression and magical environment as potentially significant rather than assuming a human baseline. Taetzel's private computing capacity may support the work. Singer is wary of outside genomic software—especially Universal Omnitech-linked systems—having undocumented communications or backdoors.

Earlier checking of hidden university HR metadata found no clean external spying trail around Okafor, but Singer encountered **three extremely heavily Scrambled files and deliberately did not open them**. Their existence is not proof of a plot, but the unknown remains.

## Fourth-Age, Alachia and Horror threads

Singer's Horror knowledge comes from direct experience. He has fought Horror-associated entities, survived a Great Ghost Dance-linked astral/metaplanar bridge event in which most companions died, and reproduced a white-light weapon effect capable of harming something mundane attacks could not penetrate. He believes the Horrors were delayed, not permanently defeated, and that future civilization may eventually have to become capable of fighting them directly rather than relying only on hiding through another Scourge.

The lost swordsman was resolved as **Urdesh the Carnivore**. Glaze's recognition of the name triggered Singer's established buried memory response and created a stronger bridge between Singer's damaged Fourth-Age recollections and Glaze's firsthand ancient knowledge.

That bridge feeds the unfinished **Alachia / Tir / immortal-elf inquiry**. Glaze personally knew Alachia in the ancient world before the later thorned condition Singer associates with Wyrm Wood/Blood Wood history. Singer and Glaze are therefore comparing damaged parallel-history memories, Glaze's firsthand ancient knowledge and curated modern records without treating any single source as complete. **Tir Tairngire and Tir na nOg remain separate research lines.** This is not yet an immediate emergency, but it is one of the campaign's deepest long-range plots.

The Mt. Baker cave-investigator thread is dormant rather than solved: a competent initiated/Masked magician and other investigators examined the former cave, and an artificial watcher was expelled by the free Great Mountain Spirit before the entrance was closed. Later checking found no renewed persistent intrusion. It should return only through new evidence rather than automatic pressure.

## GM-only delayed consequence: Morgan and Perianwyr

The Morgan/Hale estate problem itself was substantially resolved. However, after Aztechnology's internal response to the June 1 disaster, **Perianwyr told Morgan they were done with Aztechnology and both decided to disappear**, taking recovered items Singer had given to his friend. This decision was fixed by the end of June 5. One identity is intended to become active later in a way Singer can recognize.

Singer and Glaze do **not** know this until an in-fiction route reveals it.

## What matters most entering Session 25

The highest-value pressure is the **Tacoma aftermath**: Aztechnology has suffered a major covert research disaster without an established line to Singer and Glaze; Alamos 20,000 lost leadership and exposed communications; Biogene was penetrated; Perkins, Bronston and Owens all carry dangerous knowledge; and the protagonists' safety depends on their deniability remaining intact.

Behind that immediate threat sits the unresolved **Aztechnology settlement relationship**. The corporation is performing enough to support containment, but Singer and Glaze retain damaging evidence and the settlement depends on continued noninterference. Any proof that Aztechnology connects Tacoma to Taetzel, violates the settlement, or resumes covert probing would materially change that balance.

The deepest long-term plot remains the interaction between **Singer's divergent 2064 memories, Glaze's ancient firsthand knowledge, dragons, Alachia, Eliohann and the Horror cycle**. Those threads should develop through evidence and consequences rather than being forced into every scene.

Session 25 therefore begins from a superficially ordinary Redmond night with several extremely dangerous systems already in motion beneath it.
