**Attraction before sex.** Singer called Glaze beautiful from the first encounter onward and kept complimenting her in Seattle. **Darnell Washington.** A professional fake-medical organlegger crew was forcing a drugged ork, **Darnell Washington**, toward a van. Singer recognized the danger quickly, moved between Glaze and the obvious weapon threat, directed her toward the coordinator, disabled an augmented handler, rescued Darnell, contained and questioned the team, took evidence, provided competent medical triage, and continued until public EMS and Lone Star arrived. Glaze used **Influence** on the coordinator at his direction. He later told her he could have stripped the organleggers of far more than he took and chose not to.

**Puget Sound.** They took an electric sightseeing and dinner skimmer around Elliott Bay and Puget Sound — both date and classroom. Singer taught geography, ferries, boats, aircraft, measurements, time, transport and city orientation. Glaze increasingly followed the tour guide and other passengers directly, and particularly liked seeing Seattle from the water because the city's shape became easier to understand.

**Sgáan and the porpoises.** On the cruise Singer summoned a powerful sylph named **Sgáan**, politely asked whether nearby sea life might choose to visit, altered his astral presentation and sang an ultrasonic welcome. Harbor porpoises approached and responded without magical coercion. He explained that animals and paracritters usually react badly to his aura even Masked, and was grateful she had not.

**Olympic Peninsula.** They discussed flying over the peninsula; Singer complimented her wings and she promised to show him her flying when they go west. He also mentioned his grandparents' graves on Vancouver Island and became calm, distant and sad. Glaze said they could go when he wants, and did not force a larger conversation.

**Autonomy.** Across these days Glaze chose the hotel room type, handled payment, led Singer-supported negotiation, chose modern clothing, accepted or rejected his clothing and hair assistance, controlled pacing and movement during nightlife, chose whether to keep exploring, chose food and groceries out of curiosity, set her English priorities, agreed to intensive study while insisting language not replace living in the city, and began conducting ordinary exchanges without him speaking for her. **Affection before sex.** Before they were lovers he kissed her forehead and said "Thank you for making me have a good day" and "I enjoyed being with you." She answered that she enjoyed being with **him** — not the city, lessons or novelty — and rested her forehead against his. He later said so directly and joked about sleeping beside "angelic beauty."
