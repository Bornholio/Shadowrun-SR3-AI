**H17-001 — Physical / Magical State at that point**

Singer had become physically healed from the deadly injuries with which he arrived.

The progression during the session included:
- emergency stabilization by his internal medical systems,
- gradual Stun recovery,
- several uses of the Lightbearer ability Heal Believer,
- a final conventional Force 9 Heal spell after the cave's hostile background was removed.

The final Heal spell restored the remaining Physical damage completely. Singer is physically whole at session close unless the user establishes a new injury.

Singer's medical/nanite systems were heavily damaged earlier and spent many hours rebuilding. They have shown sophisticated autonomous triage behavior, including:
- emergency stabilization,
- recruiting outside aid through displays and canned language output,
- directing wound closure,
- monitoring blood pressure and other vital signs,
- microscopic repair / manufacturing activity.

Singer's vision systems were manually rebuilt/reset one subsystem at a time. His sight is functional again.

The cave's hostile **Background Count began at 4**. Singer repeatedly attempted Cleansing and ultimately pushed the remaining count from 3 to **0** using Sorcery plus Karma Pool.

Singer has only directly learned the first four Lightbearer abilities, despite having Lightbearer Rank 15. Other abilities may be latent and discovered through intuition/use. He has already sensed that additional healing-related Lightbearer functions exist, but he does not automatically know their names, activation procedures, or limits.

Singer's High Pain Tolerance covers 6 boxes for wound-modifier purposes.

Singer has professional-level libraries for:

## H17-026 — May 23, 2051

The in-world date at this historical point was:


Singer originates from his own genuine **2064** history.

Campaign cosmology / GM truth:
- Singer has **not traveled through time** in the conventional sense.
- He is in a critically branched parallel reality.
- No many-worlds theory needs to exist in-setting.
- No explanation for the boundary crossing will ever be solved as a campaign riddle.
- Singer is correct to distrust the applicability of his 2064 historical knowledge without treating his technological, magical, skill, or SR3 mechanical knowledge as false.
- His world and the campaign world share broad history and rules, but names, beings, specific events, organizations, outcomes, and later developments may differ.
- The Shadowrun world already diverged from mundane real-world history in the late 1980s; this is an additional in-setting branch.
- The campaign world remains **Shadowrun Third Edition** as history advances beyond 2064. Catastrophes whose primary setting function would be to force Fourth Edition's rules/technology paradigm do not occur.

Critical branch marker established behind the screen:
