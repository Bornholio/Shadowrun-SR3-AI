**H1519-002 — Durable incident result**

On June 1 an unauthorized Aztechnology military force attacked/attempted to seize control at Taetzel. The deployed force included an **Aguilar attack helicopter, a Lobo APC/LAV, two Kestrel drones**, and associated personnel. Singer and Glaze stood their ground. The assault failed.

`name`: **Reynaldo Texamachach**

The Aguilar, Lobo, Kestrels and remaining corporate property were recovered except for dangerous systems Singer deliberately retained because they remained threats at that moment. Those retained systems had become secured in the Taetzel armory.

The corporation did not answer the failure by immediately escalating to open war. It shifted into recovery, information control and negotiation. That outcome had become more important than the exact blow-by-blow of the fight.

**H1519-003 — Public information / evidence posture**

Aztechnology PR is actively reducing easy attribution and specificity around the event, especially **location linkage to Taetzel/Perfekto and reconstructable timelines**. Public reports may therefore become vague, contradictory, re-edited, de-indexed or detached from the exact site.

Local neighborhood memory is a separate channel. Aztechnology can make the Matrix/public story harder to trace without making nearby residents forget military aircraft, armored vehicles, shooting, recovery crews and the fact that Taetzel remained occupied afterward.

**H1519-004 — Settlement — performance awaiting resolution at that point**

Glaze, operating publicly as **Debbie**, led the substantive settlement after Singer recognized that he was too angry to negotiate reasonably. She did not merely repeat Singer's words; she turned a broad idea of local restitution into concrete demands and rejected a vague community-relations fund as inadequate.

The negotiated structure at session close is:

1. **Direct repair/compensation:** Aztechnology repairs damage it caused to Taetzel and the surrounding neighborhood and covers reasonable direct losses/disruption.
2. **Food/homeless shelter:** a real shelter within useful walking distance with actual service capacity, not a one-time publicity distribution.
3. **Water infrastructure:** a potable-water tanker truck, large stainless-steel storage tanks, pumps, filtration and supporting infrastructure sufficient to make them functional.
4. **Medical clinic:** a clinic capable of supporting **eight physicians**, with proper diagnostic/treatment equipment.
5. **Operating support:** the clinic receives **five years** of meaningful operating support so it does not become an empty photographed shell.
6. **PR freedom:** Aztechnology may attach its name, photograph the projects and construct whatever public explanation it prefers. If the agreement is honored, Singer/Glaze will not publicly contradict the story merely to embarrass the corporation.
7. **No Corporate Court escalation:** if Aztechnology performs, Singer/Glaze do not pursue Corporate Court action arising from this incident.
8. **No deliberate evidence dump:** if Aztechnology performs, Singer/Glaze do not deliberately release their security evidence as a public centerpiece.
9. **Noninterference:** no retaliation or manufactured investigations aimed at Taetzel, its owners, tenants, employees, contractors or nearby people; no harassment through subsidiaries; no continuing intelligence action designed to recover what Reynaldo lost or probe the household.
10. **No astral scouting/investigation:** no projecting personnel, spirits, astral reconnaissance, magical "security assessment" used as a synonym for investigation, or astral tracking initiated from material obtained in the incident.
11. **Reciprocal intelligence restraint:** absent another intrusion, Singer/Glaze have no interest in running an intelligence campaign against Aztechnology.
12. **Reynaldo-history restraint:** if Aztechnology observes the settlement/intelligence restrictions, Singer will avoid investigating why Reynaldo was still alive despite the old public history around Mercurial. Singer explicitly does not want an ongoing intelligence war with a AAA corporation.

At session close these are **terms**, not completed infrastructure.

Singer/Glaze have offered substantial silence and restraint **in return for performance**, not unconditional forgiveness. A later violation of the noninterference terms would change the posture and should be handled as a new fact rather than pretending the settlement never existed.
