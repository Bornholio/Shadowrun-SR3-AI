### Holly Brighton / dragon-interface detail (GM-added) ★
Singer explained to Glaze that Holly Brighton is a major media figure whose long-running Wyrm Talk relationship with Dunkelzahn eventually makes her one of the few people with unusually direct access to him. Singer knows her from future-history; in 2051 Holly has never met him. He traveled physically through astral space to Denver as James Taylor, met Holly at the Bright Center for Journalism, and gave a carefully bounded message: he and his partner have developed a non-human dry active electrode system suitable for western dragons, his partner already uses two sets, and they want a third set offered anonymously to Dunkelzahn. The point was not publicity; Singer emphasized privacy, offline biometrics, technical discretion, and that Dunkelzahn could confidentially tell other dragons if appropriate. Holly recognized it as strategically sensitive and technically years ahead of normal development, and agreed to pass it personally toward **Nadja Daviar**/Dunkelzahn without promising a response. **No response has arrived.**

The 'trodes are Singer's dragon-compatible dry active electrode arrays: non-invasive neural interfaces engineered for non-human anatomy and multiple dragon forms, built from Singer's later-era experience integrating 'trodes into armored helmets and unusual body plans. In 2051 comparable functional non-human dry arrays effectively do not exist on the known market; major corporate efforts only become viable later and initially require much longer calibration/training. The system is therefore a potentially important breakthrough in dragon-machine interfacing, not merely custom equipment. Glaze has used it successfully for computer training and full-X/simsense exposure; the offer to Dunkelzahn is a discreet proof that dragons can use such interfaces safely without surrendering control of their biometrics or identities.

### Aztechnology / Taetzel — containment, not peace
The **June 1** unauthorized Aztechnology military attack on Taetzel ended in the attackers' defeat and the death of **Reynaldo Texamachach**. Aztechnology shifted to information control, recovery and negotiated containment rather than open war.

Glaze led the settlement. Aztech obligations: repair/compensation and neighborhood restitution, especially potable water infrastructure, shelter capacity, and an eight-physician-capable clinic with five years of operating support. In return for performance, Singer and Glaze agreed not to pursue Corporate Court escalation, deliberately dump evidence publicly, or run an intelligence campaign against Aztechnology. Aztechnology agreed to noninterference: no retaliatory investigation, subsidiary harassment, astral scouting or equivalent intelligence action against Taetzel and its people.

The public water installation is functioning and heavily used, demonstrating partial performance. The relationship is **strategic containment, not reconciliation**. Evidence that Aztechnology violated noninterference or connected Tacoma to Taetzel would materially change the situation; neither is established.
