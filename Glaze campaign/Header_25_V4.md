## Active high-threat environment — secondary context

The material below should remain available because it can generate consequences, but it should not replace the actual opening scene or manufacture immediate danger without a causal route.

### Tacoma aftermath — largest fresh threat complex

Overnight June 7–8, Singer and Glaze penetrated Aztechnology's **Beta** research facility, extracted **Dr. Perkins**, uncovered severe para-biological/genetic work, and later struck the separate **Alpha** facility. Alpha's magical security was heavily subverted, its host was compromised and physically removed, captives were freed, **Dr. Carol Owens** was rescued, and Alpha was ultimately destroyed after evacuation.

The operation also intersected with **Alamos 20,000**. At the S.S. Misha, the expected Biogene contact had already been killed and replaced. Singer killed local Alamos principals **Claude Pierce, Terry Carey, and Daniel Sinclair**; their bodies were later used as forensic misdirection inside Alpha. Singer also recovered an incomplete Alamos communications mesh tying together names, aliases, photographs, messages, and relationships. That mesh is intelligence, not proof that every associated person or institution is Alamos.

The Biogene side is compromised as well. **Wendell Holmes** betrayed the later handoff to Alamos. Singer and Glaze broke the safe-house ambush and forced **Jason Walker** to confront the penetration. Walker received the contracted material and completed payment, but Biogene's internal compromise remains unresolved.

**Critical separation:** the established history contains **no evidence chain identifying Singer, Glaze, Taetzel, or BMH as responsible for the Tacoma disaster.** The June 1 Taetzel conflict and the June 8 Tacoma operation are separate discovery chains. Do not merge them merely because Aztechnology is involved in both. A future connection requires new evidence, investigation, error, betrayal, surveillance, or another causal bridge.

Dr. Perkins remains unusually vulnerable; Bronston and Owens possess dangerous knowledge; Alamos lost people and exposed part of a network; Biogene knows it was penetrated; and Aztechnology has powerful reasons to investigate what happened to two research sites. These facts create pressure without establishing that any of those actors already know who did it.

### Aztechnology / Taetzel — containment, not peace

The **June 1** unauthorized Aztechnology military attack on Taetzel ended in defeat for the attackers and the death of **Reynaldo Texamachach**. Aztechnology shifted to information control, recovery, and negotiated containment instead of immediate open war.

Glaze led the settlement. Its material obligations include repair/compensation and neighborhood restitution, especially potable water infrastructure, shelter capacity, and an eight-physician-capable clinic with five years of operating support. In return for performance, Singer and Glaze agreed not to pursue Corporate Court escalation, deliberately dump their evidence publicly, or run an intelligence campaign against Aztechnology. Aztechnology agreed to noninterference, including no retaliatory investigation, subsidiary harassment, astral scouting, or equivalent intelligence action directed at Taetzel and its people.

The public water installation is functioning and receiving heavy neighborhood use, so Aztechnology has demonstrated partial performance. The relationship remains **strategic containment rather than reconciliation**. Continued performance matters. Evidence that Aztechnology has violated the noninterference terms or connected Tacoma to Taetzel would materially change the situation, but neither condition is presently established.

### BMH government posture

Singer caused **Blue Mountain Holdings' extraterritorial status** to become mutually corroborated across UCAS systems. A State Department analyst discovered that the expected originating instrument was missing. The matter was moved into restricted administrative reconciliation rather than widened into a general investigation, and BMH obtained a sensitive-material writ covering thaumaturgical byproducts, controlled magical research/remediation material, and security equipment/personnel required for their protection.

This anomaly is contained, not erased. Do not treat it as a current raid or investigation, but do not forget that the administrative history exists.

### Okafor reproductive/genetics project

**Dr. Miriam Okafor** is a genuine confidential collaborator. She knows Debbie is a female Western Great Dragon whose forms are transformations of one individual, and she knows unusually sensitive biological history about Singer.

The project is preserving unexplained/non-coding sequence and treating transformation state, tissue type, expression, and magical environment as potentially significant. Earlier hidden university metadata checking found no clean external spying trail around Okafor, but Singer encountered **three extremely heavily Scrambled files and deliberately did not open them**. Their existence is an unresolved unknown, not proof of a hostile plot.

### Parallel-history / Fourth-Age / Horror pressure

Singer is genuinely from a **2064 parallel history**. His technical, professional, and magical knowledge can remain valid while particular history, people, organizations, and outcomes diverge. The clearest proven discrepancy remains **Schwartzkopf's missing May 21, 2051 sovereignty event**. Taetzel itself has also demonstrated that Singer can recognize a place strongly while the history attached to it is materially different.

Glaze's recognition of **Urdesh the Carnivore** strengthened the bridge between Singer's damaged Fourth-Age memories and Glaze's firsthand ancient knowledge. The unfinished **Alachia / Tir / immortal-elf** inquiry and the larger Horror-cycle problem remain deep campaign threads. They should develop through evidence, recovered history, consequences, and character choices rather than becoming the hidden explanation for every modern problem.

The Mt. Baker cave-investigator line is dormant. Later checking found no renewed persistent intrusion. It should reactivate only through new evidence.
